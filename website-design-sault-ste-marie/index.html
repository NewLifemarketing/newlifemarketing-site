Who is arriving

The Selling Was Done Before They Opened The Browser

A Sault Ste Marie of 72,051 with no comparable market for three hundred kilometres runs on recommendation, and recommendation changes what a website is for.

Somebody at work in Sault Ste Marie said to call you. A neighbour used you last spring. The question that brings a person to the site is almost never whether you are any good, because that was settled by a person they trust before the browser opened. That is the Sault Ste Marie starting position, and good website design sault ste marie buyers recognise begins from it.

What a Sault Ste Marie visitor arrives wanting is narrow and practical. Are you open on Saturday. Do you do that specific thing. Roughly what does it cost. How do I get hold of you without phoning during work. Four questions, and on most Sault Ste Marie sites all four take some hunting.

Meanwhile the Sault Ste Marie homepage is carrying a paragraph about commitment to excellence, a stock photograph of somebody else’s office, and a hero line that could belong to any business in the country. It is doing a job that was already finished, and not doing the one in front of it.

The reordering

What To Put Where The Sales Pitch Is

The same Sault Ste Marie page, two priorities. One of them answers what people came for.

None of this requires a rebuild. It is a reordering, and on most Sault Ste Marie sites it is an afternoon of work that moves more enquiries than a new design would. The reason it rarely happens is that reordering is not what anybody sells.

The one that costs real work is the price. Most Sault Ste Marie businesses will not state one, and in a city where people talk to each other that caution buys nothing, because the number gets passed around anyway. A range with the conditions attached ends more wasted calls than any qualifying form, and it ends them before either side spends time.

Where to spend

What Web Design Sault Ste Marie Budgets Should Buy

What usually gets sold here

A Sault Ste Marie redesign. New typography, a bigger hero image, a fresh palette, and the same four questions still three clicks deep. It photographs well and it changes very little, because the visitor was never hesitating over whether the business looked modern. A sault ste marie web design agency pitching on appearance is answering a question nobody in this market is asking.

What we do instead

Find out what Sault Ste Marie customers phone to ask, and put those answers where the sales pitch currently sits. Then write a price range with its conditions. Then, and only if the business sells outside the city, build the proof that strangers need and neighbours do not.

The other market

Selling Past The Recommendation Network

Everything above assumes the Sault Ste Marie customer is local. The moment a business here sells beyond the city, the rules reverse completely.

Decide which buyer you have

A trades business serving Sault Ste Marie needs the practical site. A firm selling into Michigan, or supplying the mills, or shipping across the country, needs the evidence site. Guessing wrong is how a budget gets spent on the wrong half.

The border is a real market

The International Bridge puts another country twenty minutes from Sault Ste Marie. If any of your work crosses it, those customers cannot ask a neighbour about you, and everything they will ever know has to be on the page.

Do not build both at once

A Sault Ste Marie site trying to reassure strangers and dispatch locals in the same breath does neither well. Pick the one that pays the bills now and build the second as a section rather than a second personality.

Questions

Websites In Sault Ste Marie: Common Questions

Does a business here even need a website?

Yes, but probably not the one it is being sold. In a Sault Ste Marie of 72,051 where most enquiries arrive already recommended, the site is not persuading anybody, it is answering them: hours, price, whether you do their exact job, how to get hold of you. A site that does those four things well is worth real money. A site that looks impressive and buries them is an expensive brochure.

Should we put our prices on the site?

In Sault Ste Marie, a range with its conditions, yes. The usual objection is that the work varies, which is true and is what the conditions are for. The second objection is that competitors will see it, which in a market this size is not a secret anyway. What it actually ends is the calls that were never going to convert, and it ends them before either party has spent an hour finding out.

Is our site too old-fashioned?

Possibly, and it is rarely the thing costing a Sault Ste Marie business work. Nobody who was recommended to you is deciding against on the basis of your typography. Before spending on appearance, check how many clicks it takes a visitor to find your hours, your price range and confirmation that you do their specific job. If the answer is more than one, that is the cheaper and larger problem.

We sell outside the city. Does that change things?

Completely. Somebody seven hundred kilometres from Sault Ste Marie, or across the International Bridge, has nobody to ask about you, so the site is the only evidence that will ever exist. That buyer needs named projects, named clients and specifics, which is almost the opposite of the fast practical site a local needs. Build the one that pays the bills now and add the other as its own section rather than changing the whole tone.

What does a site like this cost?

Website design sault ste marie work costs less than a rebuild, usually, because most of what matters here is reordering and writing rather than design. The parts that cost real money are the ones that need real material: a price range somebody has to commit to, project write-ups with named clients, photographs of actual work. We would rather spend the budget on those than on a new palette.
