What the market actually is

Belleville Marketing: A Market That Crosses A Boundary

The census figure is the one that matters here, and it is not the city figure.

111,184
people in the Belleville–Quinte West CMA

Statistics Canada, 2021 Census of Population

2
municipalities inside one labour market

Statistics Canada, 2021 Census of Population

Statistics Canada groups Belleville and Quinte West into a single census metropolitan area because they function as one labour market. People live in one and work in the other, and the businesses serving them do not stop at the sign on the highway.

Local advertising, by default, does. A radius drawn around a downtown, a service area set to a municipality, a page that names one town: each of those quietly excludes a share of the people who could buy.

Correcting it is free. Draw the service area to the catchment you actually serve, name both municipalities on the page in a sentence a human would write, and make the directory listings agree with both.

What the words say

Ten Phrases, And Nobody Agrees What To Call It

The vocabulary is unsettled here, which is a problem and an opportunity in the same sentence.

A settled market looks like a pyramid: one phrase carries the volume because people have learned what the thing is called. A plateau like this one means nobody has taught the town, and the business that publishes clearly enough to do the teaching gets to choose which phrase it owns.

The practical consequence is that writing eighteen pages, one per label, would be a mistake. Twenty searches cannot support a page, and the pages would compete with each other, which is the one self-inflicted loss in search that is entirely avoidable.

What works is a small number of substantial pages that use the vocabulary people actually type, naturally, because they genuinely use it. Not stuffed; used.

Spending order

Four Things To Fund Before Anything Else

Ordered by what stops being the bottleneck when it is fixed.

A page for each thing you sell

A service area drawn to the catchment

Both municipalities, if you serve both. It is a free setting in the Business Profile and in the advertising account, and it is almost never revisited after the listing was created.

A way to count enquiries

At this volume a month is noise. Counting enquiries by source on a rolling quarter is the only way to tell whether anything is working.

Paid media, last and small

With a cluster this size, advertising buys answers rather than reach. Worth running to settle a question; rarely worth running permanently.

Where a local result comes from

What Decides A Belleville Marketing Result

For a business people visit or call, most of the outcome sits outside the website.

The Business Profile first: the right primary category, honest hours, the service area drawn across both municipalities, duplicates removed. An afternoon of work, nothing a month to maintain, and most businesses have never audited theirs.

Reviews second, and the deciding factor is whether asking is a process or a good intention. A business that asks every satisfied customer systematically will out-rank one that remembers occasionally, independently of anything on its website.

Third, and most neglected: search your own business name, signed out, on a phone. An old address, a number from two providers ago, a duplicate listing somebody created years back. Each is a contradiction a search engine has to resolve and it will not always resolve it in your favour.

Only after those three does page-level work change much, which is the reverse of the order it is usually sold in — because the first three are hard to bill monthly and the fourth is easy to.

Being clear about where we are

We Are Not In Belleville

Our office and our team are in Minesing, near Barrie, which is a long way west of the Quinte region.

Search work, advertising, reporting and strategy are done at a distance by every agency including the ones with a local address. Where distance is a genuine cost is production: filming, photographing a site, sitting with a team. We travel for those and price the travel rather than folding it into a fee.

In a market this size that honesty matters more than usual, because the alternative supplier is frequently one person who genuinely is local and genuinely will drop in. If that is what the job needs, hire it.

What we bring instead is the measurement and the method. We manage over $70,000 a month in advertising spend for Ontario businesses, the owner runs the paid media personally, and fourteen client partnerships are published on our own site with the method described rather than summarised.

One thing distance does change, and it is worth naming because most agencies pretend otherwise: we will not hear the thing somebody says in a corridor. A supplier embedded in your building picks up that a competitor hired somebody, that a customer complained, that a contract slipped. We have to be told, which means a short recurring call rather than an assumption. That is a small operational cost and it is the honest price of working this way.

Our scope

A Narrow Offer, Said Plainly

Four things, and a list of what we do not do.

Paid media on Google and Meta managed against cost per enquiry. Search work built one cluster at a time so two of your own pages never compete. Video and photography of real work. And the systems underneath, so an enquiry can be traced to what produced it.

What a belleville marketing engagement should hand back

A belleville marketing engagement should leave you holding the registry of which page owns which cluster of synonyms, the profile audit with its corrections applied, and the list still unclaimed. In a market where the vocabulary is still moving, that record is what lets the next decision be made from evidence rather than from memory.

Not public relations, not events, not brand identity from scratch, and nothing needing somebody in the building weekly. Those are real needs and in a region this size there are usually local people who do them well.

What A Belleville Marketing Quote Should Separate

The management fee, which goes to the agency, from the media budget, which goes to Google or Meta, from production, which is somebody filming and writing, from software, which is a monthly licence to somebody else. A single number hides all four, and an agency paid a percentage of spend is paid more for spending more. Ask for each one in writing, ask whose name the accounts are in, and ask what you keep if you leave.

Straight answers

Belleville Marketing FAQ

Straight answers before you ever book a call.

Should we target Belleville or Quinte West?

Both, if you serve both, and the census figure is the argument: Statistics Canada counts them as one metropolitan area of 111,184 people because they function as one labour market. Draw the service area to where your crews actually go, name both on the page, and make the directory listings agree. Targeting one municipality excludes a real share of your market for no reason.

Which phrase should we target?

In a cluster this flat, not one. Ten labels at twenty searches each cannot each support a page, and a page optimised for a single one reaches a fraction of the demand. Write a small number of substantial pages that use the vocabulary people actually type, and they will pick up a share of all of it.

Is a market this size worth doing anything about?

It depends entirely on what a customer is worth. A handful of enquiries a month is transformative for a business selling five-figure projects and marginal for one selling small jobs. Work out the value per customer first; it decides whether any of this is worth buying and in what order.

How quickly does this work?

Faster than in a large market, because competition here is light. A specific page can move within weeks. What takes longer is accumulating enough enquiries to judge it, because at this scale one month is noise. Report on a rolling quarter and resist reacting to a quiet month.
