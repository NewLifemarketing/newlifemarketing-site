Straight answers

Kingston Marketing FAQ

Do you work with businesses in Kingston?

Yes. Strategy, media buying, SEO and reporting run remotely and work exactly the same at distance. When a project justifies filming on location, we travel — our crew has already shot in Thunder Bay, Sudbury, Aruba, Tampa and Washington DC.

What does digital marketing cost for a Kingston business?

It is scoped, not packaged — we do not sell bronze, silver and gold. What a business needs depends on its margins, its sales cycle and how competitive its category is locally. On the strategy call we scope it and price it plainly, and you keep the plan either way.

How long before we see results in Kingston?

High-intent search and paid social can produce enquiries within weeks of launch. SEO and organic compound over months. Anyone promising page-one rankings or a fixed lead count by a fixed date is guessing, and we would rather tell you the honest range.

Will you film at our Kingston location?

Where production is part of the scope, yes. We quote travel separately and plainly so you can see exactly what it costs — and we will tell you if a studio or design-led approach would get you more for the same money.

Do you already work with Kingston businesses?

Our published client work is across Ontario and is listed on our clients and case study pages with the real numbers attached. We are not going to claim a local portfolio we have not published — on the call we will tell you plainly what we have done in your market and what would be new.

Who actually does the work?

The people named on our leadership page. Nothing is offshored or subcontracted without telling you first, and you have direct contact with the crew running your account rather than a queue.
