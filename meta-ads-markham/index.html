Meta Ads Markham

Sold, Not Searched

Meta Ads Markham attracts ten searches a month in a city where 2,130 people a month look for a marketing supplier, which tells you something useful before any campaign exists: nobody here shops for this by typing, so every supplier you meet arrived by approach rather than comparison.

What the gap means

Meta Ads Markham: Ten Searches Against 2,130

A gap that large is a statement about how the service is sold, not about whether it works.

When something is sold rather than searched for, the version that travels is the version that is easiest to sell. Here that is a monthly retainer with a round number, a content calendar and a report full of percentages.

The same gap appears whether somebody searches for Facebook ads Markham or the newer name; both are small, both are dwarfed by general marketing demand, and both tell you the buyer is reacting to an approach rather than running a comparison.

That removes the usual filter. Without a shortlist to compare, the questions you ask have to do the work that comparison normally does.

Three of them are enough: what is this campaign for, what would end it, and what do we hold if we stop.

The third question is the one most worth insisting on, because it is cheap to answer at the start and impossible to recover later. Accounts opened in a supplier’s name, audiences built inside their business manager, and finished material that exists only as published posts all look like administrative details for as long as the relationship lasts, and like the whole asset on the day it ends.

What it is good at

Reaching People Before The Search Happens

Search intercepts a decision in progress. This reaches people before the decision exists.

Retargeting

Reaching people who already visited the site. The cheapest reliable return available, a day of work to set up, and the single most common thing missing entirely from the small accounts we are shown.

Reaching other businesses

A large commercial city is a B2B market that search cannot reach, because the people running those firms are not typing your service into anything today.

Recruitment

In a competitive labour market the campaign that fills a vacancy is frequently worth more than the one that fills the pipeline, and almost nobody runs it. Same audience tools, different creative.

Each has a clear test and an end condition. A campaign without either is the version that quietly becomes a permanent line item, which in a service nobody comparison-shops is the default outcome.

What it runs on

Stock Photography Is The Expensive Option

It costs less and performs worse, which is the worst combination available.

Every supplier has the same stock library. What almost none of their clients have is footage of the actual work: the job going in, the machine running, the finished result, the person who answers the phone.

Real material performs better and lasts longer, and the second half matters because creative fatigue in a single city arrives in weeks. A library is what lets a campaign keep running past the first month without the audience learning to scroll past you.

One planned shooting day produces months of material, and the marginal cost of each additional cut is small when the shot list is decided in advance rather than improvised on the morning.

There is a cheaper half that most suppliers will not mention because it is not billable. A phone and a clip microphone, in the hands of somebody already doing the work, capture the finished job, the unexpected problem and the thing that happened on a Tuesday, which is most of what an audience actually wants to see. The crew is for what needs lighting and planning; the rest only needs somebody to press record before deciding whether it is worth it.

A campaign budget with no production budget beside it is an arrangement for distributing material that does not exist.

Measuring it

How To Judge Meta Ads Markham Without Flattering Numbers

The platform offers several figures that cannot be acted on, and two that can.

What to ignore

Engagement rate is a percentage of an audience the platform chose. Reach is purchasable, so a reach figure with no spend beside it says nothing. Followers are the least predictive number on the page.

What to use

Cost per enquiry, and how arriving customers say they heard of you. The second is the only line in the report that comes from your business rather than from a platform.

Read both over a rolling quarter. Demand creation shows up later than interception does, so an account judged monthly will be cut in the month before it would have started working.

What goes wrong

Narrowing The Audience Until It Runs Out

In a single city this is the commonest self-inflicted failure, and it looks like success for a fortnight.

The temptation is to target only perfect prospects. The first week is flattering, and then the campaign is paying premium prices to show the same person the same thing for the fifth time.

A broader audience with sharper material outperforms it almost every time, because the platform is better at finding people than a set of manual filters is and the creative does the qualifying.

The second common failure is judging a slow channel on a fast standard: comparing cost per enquiry against search advertising, finding it worse, and moving the budget. Six weeks later the search campaigns are more expensive, because the pipeline feeding them has stopped.

The third is an account built, grown and held entirely by a supplier. Insist the accounts are in your company’s name from day one and the finished material is delivered as files rather than as posts, and that risk disappears at no cost.
