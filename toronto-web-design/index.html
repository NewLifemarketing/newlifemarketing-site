Toronto Web Design

Two Trades, One Word

The Toronto web design cluster contains 164 phrases and 17,890 searches a month, and roughly half of them are not asking for design at all — they are asking for software development, which is a different trade with different people, a different timeline and a different order of cost.

Design side: toronto web design, web design agency, web design firm, custom web design, website design companies. Development side: web development toronto, web development company, web development agency, web development companies. Two vocabularies, near-identical volumes, and almost every supplier in the city writes one page that answers both.

That is not laziness on their part so much as a commercial decision. A page that answers both captures both searches. The cost is borne later, by the buyer who arrives expecting one trade and is quoted for the other.

Keyword difficulty on the head term is 38 and the cost per click is $9.98, which between them describe a market where everybody has already noticed this and is paying to be in it.

Telling them apart

Toronto Web Design Or Web Development: Which Do You Need?

The test is not how complicated the site looks. It is whether anything on it has to think.

You need design

The site presents information, collects enquiries, and perhaps sells a catalogue of products through a platform that already exists. Nothing on it computes anything specific to your business. This is most websites, including most expensive ones, and it is a weeks-to-months job.

You need development

Something on the site has to do a job no platform does out of the box: a quoting engine, a portal, an integration with a system of record, a calculation your business depends on. This is a months-to-years job with ongoing maintenance, and it is procured completely differently.

You need both, in order

The common real answer. The presentation layer can ship in weeks and start earning while the software part is specified properly. Trying to launch them together is the single most reliable way to delay both.

You need neither yet

If the current site converts and the problem is that nobody arrives, a rebuild is an expensive way to avoid the actual problem. Traffic and offer come before cosmetics more often than any agency selling rebuilds will say.

Why a Toronto web design quote varies by a factor of ten

Because the quotes are not for the same thing. A templated build, a bespoke design on a known platform, and a custom application are three products sharing one word. When two proposals differ wildly, the question is not who is overcharging but which of the three each one priced.

Proof of work

A Toronto Web Design Case Study Beats A Gallery

A portfolio shows that something was made. A case study shows what it was for.

Take one project and write four paragraphs: what the client sold, what was going wrong, what was changed, what happened afterwards. Numbers where you have them and an honest sentence where you do not.

That page does work a gallery cannot. It demonstrates that you think about the commercial problem rather than the appearance, and it is the page a serious buyer sends to a colleague.

It is also the only kind of page in this category that anybody links to voluntarily, which is worth more over time than anything you could publish about yourselves.

Three of these are plenty. Thirty thumbnails and no explanation is the default, and it is the default because writing four honest paragraphs about a real project is harder than exporting screenshots.

Ask the client first, and ask for the figures rather than a quote. Most are willing, and a sentence saying enquiries roughly doubled in the six months afterwards is worth more than a page of adjectives about the collaboration.

How the site divides

By What You Do, Or By Who You Are

A business serving several kinds of client has to choose, and most try to avoid choosing.

Service pages answer “can you do this?”. Sector pages answer “do you understand my situation?”. Both are legitimate, and a site that builds both ends up with two sets of pages saying similar things to the same visitor.

Pick the one your buyers actually search with as the main structure, and let the other exist as a small number of supporting pages that link into it rather than duplicating it.

Where both genuinely matter, keep the sector pages short and make them point at the service pages for the detail. One page holds the substance, the other holds the recognition.

What fails is two complete parallel sets. They compete with each other, they both go stale, and a visitor who lands on the wrong one never sees the better page you wrote.

Decide it before anybody draws a menu. It is a commercial question about how your buyers describe their own problem, and once a site is built around the wrong answer, changing it is most of a rebuild.

How we work on this

What We Build And What We Do Not

Being specific about scope is more useful to you than a capability list.

We build and rebuild presentation sites, with the structure decided against the search clusters each page is meant to own, the redirects written before launch, and the content planned as part of the project rather than chased afterwards. We also build portals and platform work where the requirement is clear and the integrations are named.

We do not take on open-ended custom application development with an unspecified requirement, because that is a different discipline and the honest version of it starts with a paid discovery phase rather than a fixed quote. A supplier who gives you a fixed price for an unspecified application has priced the risk into the number or will be back for more.

In practice, most of the Toronto web design enquiries that reach us are the first kind with one piece of the second attached, and the useful first conversation is about separating them.

How much should a website cost in Toronto?

The spread runs from a few thousand to several hundred thousand and the number is set by how much is bespoke, what it connects to and who writes the content. A useful way to narrow it quickly: decide whether anything on the site has to compute something specific to your business. If not, you are in the lower part of the range regardless of how the site looks.

How long does a rebuild take?

For a presentation site with content ready, weeks. For the same site with content not written, as long as the content takes, which is usually the real answer. Add time for every system the site has to integrate with, and add a fixed week at the end for redirects and testing that nobody enjoys and everybody needs.

Will a new site improve our search rankings?

Not by itself, and it can easily make them worse if the redirects are mishandled. What a rebuild does is remove obstacles — speed, structure, a page for each thing you sell — so that search work can produce a result. Treat it as clearing the ground rather than as the crop, and plan the search work as a separate line that follows the launch.

Can you work with our existing developers?

Yes, and where there is an internal or incumbent development team that usually produces the best split: they own the application and we own the presentation layer and the search structure. The thing that has to be agreed up front is who owns the URL map, because that is the seam where rebuilds go wrong and it needs one owner rather than two.
