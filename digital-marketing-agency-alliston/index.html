The Alliston market

A Factory Town With A Farm Heart And City Growth

Alliston is the anchor of New Tecumseth — a town where Honda of Canada Manufacturing employs thousands, potato fields still ring the edges, and new subdivisions arrive faster than the main street can learn the new faces.

The plant shapes everything: stable, well-paid shift workers who eat, train, service their cars and raise families within minutes of the line; a supply chain of manufacturers and trades that runs on B2B relationships; and a hiring market where every employer competes with the plant's wages. Layer on the agricultural economy that built the town — Alliston is still proud potato country — and the commuter growth spilling north out of York Region into Alliston, Beeton and Tottenham, and you get three customer bases with almost nothing in common except the postal code.

Downtown Victoria Street's independents, the highway-side retail, the farm-gate businesses and the trades working the concessions all need different marketing — and most of them are still relying on the same word-of-mouth that worked when the town was half this size.

The problems

What New Tecumseth Businesses Run Into

Newcomers with city habits

The subdivision families choose businesses the way Vaughan taught them — by phone, by reviews, by whoever shows up first.

Paycheques leak south

Plenty of Alliston money is spent in Newmarket and Barrie out of habit. Local businesses have to give people a visible reason to stay.

Hiring against the plant

Every employer in town recruits in Honda's shadow. The ones who look alive online win the applications that are left.

Three towns, one budget

Alliston, Beeton and Tottenham each have their own gravity. Spraying one ad across all three wastes most of it.

B2B that's invisible online

Supply-chain and agri-businesses run on relationships — until a buyer Googles them and finds nothing worth trusting.

Shift-work rhythms

A town that eats lunch at 3 a.m. doesn't follow a 9-to-5 posting schedule. Timing is targeting here.

How we help

Marketing Built Around The Shift Whistle

Alliston rewards businesses that respect how the town actually runs — so that's how we build the campaigns.

Workforce-aware targeting

Radius and schedule-aware campaigns around the plant and the employment lands — offers that land at shift change, not just at noon.

Win the newcomers

New-mover audiences across the subdivisions of all three towns, with filmed local content that makes 'support local' feel like the better choice, not the charity one.

Trades & B2B capture

High-intent Google Search plus a credibility layer — real job-site and facility footage — for the trades and suppliers whose buyers check them out before calling.

Recruit like you sell

Culture film, targeted hiring campaigns and same-day applicant follow-up through the CRM — treating hiring with the same system as revenue.

Across New Tecumseth

Three Main Streets, One Municipality

New Tecumseth is three real towns in a trench coat, and each buys differently.

Alliston is the commercial centre — Victoria Street's independents, the highway retail, the plant's orbit of services and the new subdivisions absorbing the growth. Beeton keeps a small-town main street loyal to businesses that show up for it; Tottenham, closest to the York Region line, fills fastest with commuter families carrying city buying habits north. Between them run the concessions — farm properties and rural households that generate steady trades work and reward the contractors easiest to find and fastest to answer. We structure campaigns town by town because a Tottenham newcomer and a third-generation Beeton household respond to completely different messages.

What the first 30 days look like: strategy call and written plan in week one, honest scoping with Keegan, a crew day filming at your business, and campaigns live with verified tracking around the two-week mark — timed, where it matters, to the shift calendar and the season. Then weekly optimization and a plain-dollars monthly report.

Straight answers

Alliston FAQ

Does the Honda plant actually matter for my small business's marketing?

More than almost anything else in town. Thousands of well-paid workers cycle through shifts minutes from your door — lunches, coffees, gyms, auto services, housing, everything. Campaigns can be timed and targeted around that workforce reality, which is a lever most Alliston businesses never pull.

We're in Beeton — do you cover all of New Tecumseth?

Yes. Alliston, Beeton and Tottenham behave like three related-but-different markets, and we target them that way — plus the rural concessions between them, where a lot of the trades work actually is. One system, community-level audiences.

Everyone here knows everyone. Why would ads help?

Because the town is changing under that assumption. New subdivisions are filling with commuter families who don't know anyone yet — they choose from their phones like the city taught them to. Word-of-mouth keeps your base; ads and a live profile win the newcomers before someone else does.

Can you help us hire, not just sell?

Yes — recruitment is half the battle in a manufacturing town where the plant sets the wage bar. Filmed culture content and targeted recruitment campaigns show what working for you is actually like, and the CRM answers applicants the same day.

How does a shoot day work out here?

Easily — Alliston is under an hour from our office. The crew films at your shop, site or storefront with cinema gear; one day banks a month of reels, ads and design. Farm-gate, factory-floor or main-street — we shoot what's real.

What's the starting budget?

Scoped on the call, not off a menu. A Victoria Street storefront and a trades company working three towns need different builds. You'll get honest numbers from Keegan and a written plan you keep either way.

Our customers are older and not really online. Does digital still work?

They're online — they're just not on TikTok. New Tecumseth's established households live on Facebook, Google Search and the map pack, and they read reviews like a newspaper. Campaigns get built where your actual customers are, which around here usually means search, Facebook and a bulletproof Business Profile.
