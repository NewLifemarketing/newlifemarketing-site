How we build

What A NewLife Website Actually Includes

Built around the sales conversation

Structure before styling

We start from how the business actually sells — the plan catalogue for a home builder, the service-by-service explanation for a technical trade, the compliance gate for a regulated product. The structure follows that, not a template.

Written, not filled in

Real copy on every page

Service pages, project pages and FAQs written for the business and the searches its customers actually make — not lorem, and not a paragraph of adjectives repeated per page.

Local SEO structure from day one

So the build is findable, not just pretty

One page per job, clean heading structure, schema markup, service-area pages where the business serves multiple towns, and a sitemap that reflects all of it.

Wired to the CRM

A form that goes nowhere is a broken site

Quote forms, booking calendars and call tracking connected to GoHighLevel, so an enquiry becomes a tracked lead with follow-up attached instead of an email someone misses.

Fast, and fast on a phone

Because most of the traffic is mobile

Static builds with compressed media and no plugin bloat — pages that load on a job site with two bars of signal.

Maintained after launch

Optional monthly optimization

New pages, new projects, speed and conversion work as the business changes. Several of the builds above are on a monthly website optimization retainer.

A website is not a brochure. It's the landing page for everything else you spend money on.

Ads, SEO and social all end at the same place. If that page doesn't convert, the spend in front of it is wasted.

Straight answers

Website Build FAQ

Ownership, timelines, rebuilds and what happens after launch.

Do we own the website when it's done?

Yes. The site, the domain and the content are yours. We build on a fast static stack and can hand over the source and hosting whenever you want it — there is no lock-in and no proprietary page builder you have to keep paying for.

How long does a build take?

Most of the sites above ran a few weeks from kickoff to launch, and the pace is usually set by how quickly we get photos, service details and sign-off rather than by build time. Bigger builds — a plan catalogue, dozens of service pages — take longer.

Can you rebuild our existing site, or does it have to be from scratch?

Either. Several of these were rebuilds of a site that already existed. We keep what is working — the content that ranks, the pages people actually visit — and rebuild the structure around it, so you do not lose the search equity you already have.

Do you do the copy and photos too?

Copy, yes — it is written as part of the build. Photography and video are produced by our own crew on a shoot day, which is usually the difference between a site that looks like the business and a site that looks like a stock library.

What happens after launch?

You can take it and run, or keep us on monthly website optimization — new pages, new projects, speed and conversion work, and SEO on top. Both are normal; we will tell you which one your situation actually needs.
