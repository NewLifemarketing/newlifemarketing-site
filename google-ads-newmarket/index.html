Google Ads in Newmarket

An expensive York Region auction, three different markets, and a catchment that blurs at the edges.

Book a strategy callHow we run Google Ads

The market

Three Markets Inside One Expensive Auction

Newmarket sits inside the York Region auction, which is among the dearest in the province outside Toronto proper. Clicks cost real money here and a loose account shows it within a month.

The town's three economies, Main Street, the mall catchment and the health and professional cluster, mean an account built generically is bidding across audiences that have nothing in common.

The catchment blur is the third problem. Newmarket runs into Aurora and East Gwillimbury, and accounts routinely buy clicks from areas the business has no intention of serving.

What we would target

Separating What Should Never Have Been Merged

The structure separates the markets you are actually in and draws the geography tightly enough that you are not funding the neighbouring towns by accident.

Keyword sets stay narrow and intent-led, because at York Region click prices breadth is expensive.

The structure and ongoing management is explained on our Google Ads Management page.

The work

What Google Ads Management Involves In Newmarket

Campaign structure follows the market you are in rather than the services you list. A clinic and a Main Street retailer need different campaigns, different bids and different landing pages even inside the same account.

Keyword research is intent-weighted and the match types are kept tight. Broad match at York Region prices is an expensive way to discover what you did not want to buy.

Negative keywords are worked monthly from the search terms report, and the geographic report is read alongside it, because in a blurred catchment the waste is as often locational as it is keyword-driven.

Landing pages are matched to campaigns, conversion tracking is verified, and reporting is cost per lead by campaign so you can see which of the three markets is actually carrying the account.

All of that is standard Google Ads work. What is specific to Newmarket is the order we do it in.

Where we would start

What We Would Change First On A Newmarket Account

Split the campaigns by market. Blended, one of the three is almost always subsidising the other two and nobody can see which.

Then tighten geography to the radius you actually serve, which usually removes a surprising share of the spend.

Then the match types and the negative list, because at these prices breadth costs more than it returns.

Then the landing pages, particularly for the health cluster, where the competition is well funded and a generic page loses.

Straight answers

Google Ads in Newmarket - FAQ

Can a small business compete in the York Region auction?

On narrow, high-intent searches, yes. On broad category terms you are bidding against budgets set for the GTA and it rarely pays.

Should we target Aurora too?

Only if you serve it, and then as its own campaign. Folded in, it inflates volume and hides what a Newmarket lead actually costs.

Why is our cost per lead so high?

Usually a combination of broad match, a catchment drawn too wide, and all three of Newmarket's markets sharing one campaign. Those three together explain most of what we fix here.
