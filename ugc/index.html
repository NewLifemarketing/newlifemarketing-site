The rules

Short List, Strictly Applied

  • Original videos only. No reposting someone else's work as yours.
  • Quote only the numbers in your brief. Every figure we publish is reported from the ad account or the CRM, and we will not have it exaggerated.
  • Disclose the partnership on every post, using each platform's paid partnership or #ad label.
  • No promises of results. We show what happened for a client, never what will happen for a viewer.
  • Some client categories are off limits for clipping, cannabis for example. Your brief says which clients you can feature.

Questions

Before You Apply

What would I create?

Short vertical videos about NewLife: breakdowns of client results, walkthroughs of our systems, clips from our long-form content and sharp marketing takes. Your brief spells out the angle and the numbers.

How are Lane A and Lane B different?

Lane A pays more guaranteed base on every qualifying post. Lane B pays a smaller base but a higher rate per 1,000 views, with a higher per-video cap. Pick the one that suits how your videos perform.

Do I need a following?

There is no set follower count. We care about hooks, pacing and whether people watch to the end. Send a sample and tell us about your account.

Do I need a minimum number of views?

No minimum views. Base pay applies to qualifying original videos that are posted.

When do I get paid?

The timing and method are confirmed in your offer.

Can I run a clip page?

Yes. Dedicated clip pages are welcome if they follow the rules above and every post is disclosed. Tell us which accounts you run when you apply.

Can I post the same video on more than one platform?

Yes, as long as it is your original video. Eligible views are counted per the terms in your offer.

What happens after I apply?

We review your sample. If there is a fit we send an offer with the exact terms. Nothing starts until you accept.
