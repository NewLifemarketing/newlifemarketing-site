The Hub Of The Northwest, Half A Province From Anywhere

Thunder Bay is the regional capital of northwestern Ontario — about 110,000 people on Lake Superior's shore under the Sleeping Giant, and the commercial hub for a catchment that stretches hundreds of kilometres in every direction. Like Sudbury in the northeast, hub status is the prize: the port and grain elevators, the regional hospital, Lakehead University and Confederation College make this the place the northwest drives to for everything serious.

A Thunder Bay business that owns its category's search results owns customers from Nipigon to Fort Frances. We know this market from the ground, not from a map. Our crew spent the road-trip days here filming excavation sites, store walkthroughs and finished builds — and what stood out is how personal commerce still is: owners on first-name terms with customers, referrals carrying real weight, and a visible gap between the businesses investing in how they show up online and everyone else.

Two Downtowns, One Market

Thunder Bay is famously a tale of two cities — Port Arthur's north core and Fort William's south core, stitched together by the Intercity retail corridor between them — and customers still shop along those lines. The north core carries the waterfront district, restaurants and event traffic; the south core anchors its own loyal neighbourhoods; and Intercity is where the region comes to spend — the big-box gravity well every retailer competes against.

Around it all sits the catchment that makes TBay matter: Nipigon, Marathon, Geraldton, and west along the highway toward Dryden and Fort Frances — customers who research everything online because the drive is measured in hours. Our shoot days here covered both cores and the job sites between them; the targeting reflects how the city actually moves.

What the first 30 days look like: strategy call with Cruz or Keegan and a written plan in week one; tracking, CRM and campaign builds start immediately — and because our crew already cycles through Thunder Bay for existing clients, your first shoot day joins the next production trip rather than waiting for one.
