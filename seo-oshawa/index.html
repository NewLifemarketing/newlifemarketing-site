SEO Oshawa Has To Account For A City That Commutes

An Ontario commuter city has a problem no other market has: for eight hours a day a large part of its population is physically somewhere else.

175,400
people in Oshawa

Local search results are weighted by where the person searching is standing. That is the whole mechanism behind the map pack and it is why a business physically in a city holds an advantage no out-of-town competitor can buy. In Oshawa it cuts the other way for part of the day.

Somebody who lives in Oshawa and works downtown spends the working day inside Toronto’s proximity radius. If they look something up at lunchtime, the map results show them Toronto businesses, because that is where they are. The seo oshawa position you worked for is invisible to them in that moment.

They are still your customer. They are simply reachable at different hours and through different searches, and almost no Oshawa business has ever thought about which. This is not a ranking problem and no amount of additional optimisation fixes it, which is why it tends to go unnoticed.

When they are yours

Four Moments When An Oshawa Searcher Is Actually In Oshawa

The market is not smaller. It is concentrated into particular hours and particular kinds of search, and those are the ones worth building for.

Anything needed at the house is the largest category and the most defensible. A roof, a furnace, a driveway, a renovation: these cannot be bought near the office and the search happens in the evening, from home, inside your proximity radius. SEO Oshawa work aimed at home-based services is aimed at the part of the market the commute does not touch.

Searching the city by name is the other escape. When somebody types the city explicitly, proximity matters much less, because they have told the search engine where they mean. That is why ranking for seo services oshawa and its variants matters more in a commuter city than the raw volume suggests: those searches work from anywhere.

Straight answers

SEO Oshawa FAQ

Straight answers before you ever book a call.

We rank first but the phone does not ring. Why?

In a commuter city the commonest explanation is that your rankings are being measured from a place your market is not standing in. Local results are weighted by proximity, so somebody who lives in Oshawa and works downtown sees Toronto businesses when they search at lunchtime. Your position is real and they were never in a search that could show it. A rank report checked from the city centre cannot detect this at all.

What can we actually do about it?

Three things. Weight the work toward searches that name the city explicitly, because those work regardless of where the person is standing. Prioritise services that can only be bought where somebody lives, which the commute cannot touch. And make sure the profile and the site serve evenings and weekends properly, since that is when your commuting market is inside the radius and free to look.

Should our Google profile hours match our office hours?

They should be accurate, and the question worth asking is whether the office hours themselves are right for this market. A business whose profile closes at five is marked closed during precisely the window its commuting customers are free to look, and closed businesses are shown less prominently. If you take enquiries in the evening, say so. If you do not, that is a commercial decision worth making deliberately rather than by default.

Does this affect every Oshawa business?

No, and the difference is what you sell. A service somebody can only buy where they live, such as anything to do with the house, is barely affected: the search happens in the evening from home. A service somebody could equally buy near the office, such as a lunchtime errand or a professional appointment, is affected a great deal. Working out which you are takes one conversation and changes the whole plan.

How do we measure it properly?

Two changes. Track enquiries by hour of day and by day of week, which most businesses have never charted and which in a commuter city is immediately revealing. And check rankings from more than one location, including from downtown Toronto rather than only from inside Oshawa. The gap between those two positions is the part of your market the commute is taking, and it is invisible in standard reporting.
