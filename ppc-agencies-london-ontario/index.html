London Is The One Market Where A Radius Works

Draw a circle around a London business and almost all of it is London. In most Ontario cities that is emphatically not true.

Geography is the first and largest decision in any paid account, and in most cities it is where the money quietly leaks. A fifteen kilometre circle in Brampton spends a large share of its budget in Mississauga, Vaughan and north Toronto, where the costs are higher and a customer will frequently use somebody closer.

London has no neighbour to leak into. The circle is the city, the city is the market, and the default setup that causes expensive problems elsewhere is broadly correct here. Ppc agencies london ontario businesses hire tend to get this right without having thought about it.

Which is good news with an awkward consequence. If the hardest lever in most accounts is already pulled, the usual proposal has nothing much left to offer, and the gap between a mediocre ppc london ontario account and a good one has to come from somewhere else entirely.

Where it does come from

The Ad And The Landing Page, Which Nobody Audits

Two things decide a London account once the geography is settled, and both are usually inherited rather than designed.

The landing page is the biggest of the four and the least likely to be in scope. A campaign sending every ad to the home page is asking one page to serve every promise the ads made, so it serves none of them well. Splitting that into a page per service line routinely lifts conversion on identical traffic and identical spend.

The hour after the enquiry is the cheapest of the four and the one nobody treats as marketing. A google ads agency london ontario firms retain will optimise toward a form fill and has no visibility into whether anybody called the person back, which is where a meaningful share of paid budgets actually evaporates.

An account inherited from a previous agency with perfectly reasonable targeting, every ad pointing at the home page, conversions counting every form submission, and nobody measuring what happened after. Questions

Google Ads In London Ontario: Common Questions

Should we use radius targeting in London?

Usually yes, which is the opposite of our advice in a GTA city. A circle around a Brampton address spends a large share of its budget in Mississauga, Vaughan and north Toronto, where costs are higher and the customer will often use somebody closer. London has no neighbouring city to leak into, so the circle is the market. That makes the hardest decision in most accounts straightforward here, and it means the budget should go somewhere else.

If the targeting is fine, what are we paying for?

The conversion definition, the landing pages and the follow-up, which is where the gap between an average London account and a good one actually sits. None of those three live inside the ads platform, which is precisely why they are so frequently untouched: a platform report cannot see them and a monthly optimisation summary never mentions them. It is less visible work than targeting changes and it moves considerably more money.

Do we need a landing page for every service?

For every service you advertise separately, yes, and it is usually the largest single gain available here. Sending every ad to the home page asks one page to honour every promise the ads made, so it honours none of them well. A page matching each ad lifts conversion on identical traffic and identical spend. It is real work rather than a setting, which is why it tends to be proposed last and should be proposed first.

How much should we spend?

In a closed market this is answerable rather than guessable. Work out what a customer is worth and what share of the available searches you are currently capturing, and the ceiling becomes visible. London is small enough and self-contained enough that you can genuinely approach saturation on a service, which is both useful to know and impossible to establish in a market that bleeds into a larger neighbour. Most businesses have never run the calculation.

Can you audit without taking over?

Yes, and in London it is often the right outcome. Read access shows the targeting, the search terms, the conversion definitions and the bid strategies. If the account is competently run and simply needs three specific changes, we will say so and hand the list over. Given that the hardest lever in most markets is already correct here, that is a more likely finding in London than anywhere else we work.
