The wrong goal

More Enquiries Is Not The Goal For A Cambridge Supplier

For a Cambridge business whose quotes take hours to produce, an unqualified enquiry is not neutral. It is a cost.

A consumer business wants volume because quoting is cheap. A machine shop, a fabricator, a contract manufacturer or a specialist trade in Cambridge is in a different position: producing a real Cambridge quote can take an estimator half a day, and the enquiry that arrives without a drawing, a quantity or a realistic budget consumes that time and produces nothing.

So the website has a job most briefs never mention. It should be stating clearly enough what you do, and more importantly what you do not do, that a person who was never going to be a customer closes the tab. Every one of those is time given back to the people who can buy.

This is the opposite of the usual advice, and it is why a generic website design cambridge project can deliver a perfectly attractive site that makes the business worse. The form gets easier to fill in, the copy gets broader, the enquiry count goes up and the estimator drowns. Web design cambridge quotes are almost always written against enquiry volume, because that is the number everybody agrees is good.

It is worth saying that this applies to the industrial half of the city rather than all of it. Web design in cambridge for a clinic, a restaurant or a retailer follows the ordinary rules and more enquiries really are better. The filter argument is for businesses where producing a quote is expensive, and in a manufacturing city that is a large share of them.

What a filter looks like

The Four Things That Qualify An Enquiry Before It Arrives

None of them is a design decision. All four are usually missing.

Stating what you do not do is the one people resist hardest and it is the most effective. A shop that says plainly it does not take one-off prototypes under a certain value stops receiving them, and a buyer with a genuine production run reads that same sentence as evidence of a serious operation rather than as a rejection.

Quantities and tolerances do the same work in the opposite direction. A web design company cambridge suppliers hire will rarely ask for them, because they are awkward to gather and they look like detail rather than marketing. They are the difference between a site that sorts your enquiries and one that simply counts them, and neither the web design companies cambridge buyers shortlist nor a web design company in cambridge working to a page count tends to request them.

Where the money goes

What Web Design Services Cambridge Budgets Should Buy First

What usually goes wrong

One page called Services with eleven capabilities in a bulleted list. It ranks for none of them, answers none of the buyer’s questions, and makes every enquiry start from zero. Cambridge web design projects reach this shape because eleven real pages is eleven times the writing, and the writing is the part nobody budgeted.

What we do instead

One page per capability with the detail a buyer needs to shortlist you or rule you out. It is more work and it is the work. A web design agency cambridge firms hire should be pushing for the estimator’s time, not for a mood board.

What to photograph

Your Floor Is Better Than Any Stock Library

Cambridge buyers are trying to work out whether you are a real operation at the scale they need. One afternoon with a camera answers that better than any amount of copy.

Shoot the floor, not a boardroom

Machines running, parts in progress, the actual Cambridge space. Web designers in cambridge will usually suggest a clean hero image; the buyer wants to see whether your shop looks like it could handle their job. Cambridge web designers rarely ask to visit, and the ones who do produce better sites.

Put a ruler in the photograph

Scale is the question behind most enquiries. A finished part photographed next to something familiar answers it instantly and saves a round of emails.

Name the people

Who to ask for, with a role. A cambridge web design company that leaves the team page as stock silhouettes has removed the cheapest trust signal available to a small Cambridge supplier.

What share you cannot quote

Ask whoever handles enquiries what proportion are not worth quoting and why. That answer usually names the missing information exactly: no quantity, wrong size, outside the process, no drawing. The site gets built to pre-empt those, which is a brief nobody writes and everybody needs.

An hour with the estimator

Processes, materials, sizes, tolerances, minimum runs, certifications, and the things you turn away. Weeks two to five

Not one list of eleven

Each capability gets its own page, written in the words a buyer searches with, carrying the detail they need to shortlist you. This is also what makes the pages findable at all, which a single services page never manages.

Equipment, parts, people, building

The cheapest credibility available and the most commonly skipped. A website design company cambridge suppliers rehire is almost always one that insisted on the camera, even though it felt like an odd thing to spend a morning on.

Accounts in your name, and editable

Questions

Website Design In Cambridge: Common Questions

Should our website really say what we do not do?

For a supplier, yes, and it is the single most effective thing you can add. It sounds like turning away business and it does the opposite: the enquiries you lose are the ones your estimator was going to decline anyway, and the buyer with a genuine production run reads a clear statement of scope as evidence of a serious operation. The hidden cost of a vague site is not lost sales, it is hours of quoting time spent on work you were never going to take.

How many pages do we need?

One per capability, which is usually more than people expect and the reason most supplier sites have one page called Services instead. Eleven capabilities in a bulleted list ranks for none of them and answers nobody’s question. Eleven real pages is eleven times the writing, which is the honest reason it does not get done, and it is also where the entire return sits. If the budget will not stretch to all of them, build the three that make the most money properly rather than eleven badly.

What does a website cost in Cambridge?

The driver is how much technical detail has to be gathered and written, not the design. A supplier who can hand over process sheets, tolerances and finished-part photographs is a genuinely cheaper project than one where all of that has to be extracted in interviews. Any quote that does not change when you offer to supply the detail is not itemised properly. The design itself is rarely the expensive part and almost never the part that decides whether the site works.

Do we need to mention Galt, Preston or Hespeler?

For a consumer-facing Cambridge business in one of them, yes, because residents still describe where they are that way and a site that only says Cambridge is harder for them to find. For an industrial supplier it matters much less than proximity to the 401, which is the location fact a procurement buyer actually weighs. Both are one sentence, so there is rarely a reason to leave either out where it applies.

Can you work with our current web designer?

Usually, and often it is the cheaper answer. If somebody is already handling the visual side well, the parts that most commonly need outside help are the page structure, the technical detail, and getting the estimator’s knowledge onto the site. Those are content and architecture problems rather than design problems. A cambridge web designer doing good work does not need replacing to fix them, and we would rather hand over a specific list than a proposal.
