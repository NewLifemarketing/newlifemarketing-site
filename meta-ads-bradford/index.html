Meta Ads Bradford

Meta Ads in Bradford

Thousands of new households, and a cheaper way to reach them than the search auction allows.

Book a strategy callHow we run Meta Ads

The market

Expensive Search, Cheaper Social

Bradford's search auction is expensive because York Region bids into it. Paid social is not priced the same way, which makes it unusually attractive here by comparison, the same household costs far less to reach on Facebook than on Google.

And the households are the opportunity. The town has filled quickly with commuters who have no established suppliers and no local network, which is the condition under which recognition advertising works best.

The split economy still matters. The commuter audience and the agricultural side of the town share a postcode and almost nothing else, and a campaign aimed at both reaches neither properly.

What we would target

Reaching The Households, Not The Corridor

The audience is built around the residential growth areas and the behaviours that signal a recent move, rather than a radius that sweeps in the Marsh and the corridor traffic.

Creative does the persuading, because none of these people have heard of you.

The audience, creative and testing process is on our Meta Ads page.

The work

What Meta Ads Management Involves In Bradford

Audiences start with your own lists and then widen into recent-mover and homeowner targeting across the residential areas, deliberately excluding the parts of the catchment you do not serve.

Because the search auction here is costly, paid social often carries a larger share of the budget than it would elsewhere, and the structure reflects that: proper cold, warm and retargeting layers rather than a single boosted campaign.

Creative is filmed on site and rotated, with several variants tested per audience and spend moved to whatever is cheapest per lead.

Reporting compares cost per lead against the search side directly, which in Bradford usually makes the case for the balance between the two clearer than any argument would.

That is our Meta Ads programme, weighted for what Bradford actually needs.

Where we would start

Where We Would Start On A Bradford Account

By deciding which half of Bradford the campaign is for, because the targeting and the creative both change completely.

Then audience construction around the growth areas, with recent-mover behaviour layered in.

Then creative, filmed rather than sourced, since the audience has no prior relationship with you.

Then a direct cost-per-lead comparison with your search spend, which here usually justifies shifting some of the budget.

Straight answers

Meta Ads in Bradford - FAQ

Should we move budget from Google to Meta?

Possibly, and the honest way to decide is to run both properly for a period and compare cost per lead. In Bradford that comparison tends to favour social more than it does elsewhere.

Which audience should we target?

The one you actually sell to. The commuter households and the agricultural economy need entirely different campaigns, and trying to serve both halves the effectiveness of each.

Is social cheaper than search in Bradford?

Per lead, frequently yes, because the search auction is inflated by York Region and the social auction is not. We would rather show you the comparison from your own data than assert it.
