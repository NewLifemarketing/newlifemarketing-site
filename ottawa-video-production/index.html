Large organisations decide by committee, and Ottawa is a city of large organisations. The person who liked you is rarely the person who signs. They have to go back to a team lead, a technical authority and somebody holding the budget, and explain you from memory to people who were never in the room.

That is the job your video is actually doing in Ottawa. Not impressing a stranger on a home page, but travelling. It gets pasted into an email, watched between meetings, and judged by somebody with no context and no patience. Ottawa video production that is designed for the first audience and not the second looks beautiful and dies in an inbox.

Once you accept that, most of the hard decisions make themselves. It has to be short enough to watch without committing, specific enough to answer a technical question, and plain enough that the person forwarding it is not embarrassed to attach it.

The viewing conditions

Sound Off, At A Desk, On A Network That Blocks Things

Ottawa video gets watched in the least cinematic conditions available, and the production decisions should admit that.

What usually gets commissioned

A three-minute brand film with a score, drone footage over Ottawa, and the proposition arriving at ninety seconds. It screens well in the boardroom where it is approved and performs badly in the inbox where it has to live.

What tends to work here

Ninety seconds, burned-in captions, the specific claim in the first ten, and a file that plays anywhere. Corporate video production ottawa buyers forward looks less impressive and does more. The brand film is worth making second, not first.

Where the budget goes

What Video Production Ottawa Budgets Should Fund First

Make one per question

A separate short piece for each thing a buyer asks, rather than one film attempting all of them. Each can then be sent to the exact person who asked, which is what makes a video production company ottawa clients re-hire useful rather than decorative.

Caption everything, properly

Burned-in or well-timed, written rather than auto-generated. In an open office with the sound off, the captions are the video. Auto-captions mangle technical terms and product names, which is precisely the content that needed to be clear.

Hand over files that travel

A plain hosted link and a downloadable file, not only a social embed. A managed network that blocks the player blocks the pitch, and the person forwarding it will not troubleshoot it for you.

What to shoot

The Four Pieces Most Ottawa Firms Actually Need

Not a catalogue. These four cover almost every conversation a supplier has to support.

Capability

Who we are, in sixty seconds

Named people, the sectors served, the scale of work. This is the piece that gets attached when somebody introduces you, and most firms do not have one.

Explainer

How the thing works

The technical answer, on screen, once. It saves the same demonstration being given four times to four people and it reaches the technical authority who was never on the call.

Case study

It worked for them

A customer saying what changed, with numbers where permitted. Harder to arrange and worth more than anything you can say about yourself.

Recruiting

Why work here

In a city where large employers compete for the same people, a short honest piece about the team does more hiring work than a careers page. It also tends to be the most watched video an Ottawa business owns.

Shot together over a day or two, these cost far less than four separate commissions and share the same setup. That is usually the single biggest saving available in ottawa video production, and it is only available if the whole list is planned before anybody turns a camera on.

Bilingual delivery is worth deciding at the same time. In Ottawa and across eastern Ontario, a French caption track is inexpensive when it is planned and awkward when it is retrofitted, and it is noticed by exactly the audience you were making the video for.

Questions

Video In Ottawa: Common Questions

How long should a corporate video be?

Ninety seconds for anything that has to be forwarded, and shorter is usually better. The constraint is not attention span in general, it is that your video is being watched between two meetings by somebody who did not ask for it. A longer piece is worth making once the short ones are working and you have something that genuinely needs the room, such as a detailed case study or a recruiting film. Leading with the long one is the most common and most expensive mistake in this market.

Do we need French versions?

A French caption track is worth scoping in Ottawa and across eastern Ontario, and it costs very little when it is planned into the project rather than added afterwards. A full French voiceover is a bigger decision and depends on who is watching. What does not work is machine-translated captions, which mangle technical terms and are noticed immediately here. Decide it before the shoot, because retrofitting is where the cost appears.

What does video production in Ottawa cost?

The driver is shoot days and the number of locations, far more than the edit. That is why we plan the whole list first and shoot it together: four short pieces from one or two days cost a fraction of four separate commissions, because the crew, the travel and the lighting setup are shared. A single short capability piece shot on its own is the most expensive way to buy a minute of video, and it is how most firms start.

Will it work if our buyers are on restricted networks?

It will if it is delivered properly. Some managed networks block social embeds and certain players outright, so we hand over a plain hosted link and a downloadable file alongside whatever you publish socially, and we check it opens on a restricted connection before calling the project finished. Captions matter here too, because the same environments are often open-plan offices where nobody turns the sound on.

Who owns the footage?

You do, including the raw files. We hand over the finished pieces, the project files and the original footage at the end of the project. It matters more than it sounds: a year later you will want a thirty-second cut for something nobody anticipated, and if the raw footage sits with the production company that becomes a new project instead of an afternoon.
