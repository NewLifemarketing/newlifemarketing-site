Straight answers

SEO Company Markham FAQ

Straight answers before you ever book a call.

How do we compare SEO companies when the quotes all look the same?

Stop comparing documents and ask four questions of each. What would you change first and why that. What number should move as a result. What would you stop doing. What happens if nothing moves in six months. The answers will not be comparable in the way the proposals are, which is exactly what makes them useful. The most revealing is what to stop, because a supplier who proposes only additions has either not looked at your situation or is unwilling to reduce their own invoice.

Why is Markham more competitive than other cities?

Because more people here go looking for this than in any other city we work in, and because the people competing for them optimise pages for a living. You are not up against local businesses with ordinary websites; you are up against firms whose own ranking is their shop window. The practical consequence is that the structural work alone will not carry a page here, and anybody promising a fast result on the main term is describing something other than what they will deliver.

What should an SEO package include?

Three things worth paying for and one worth questioning. Finite structural work with a visible result. Genuine depth on whatever makes you the most money. Reporting, which is useful and is not the work. And volume activity such as directory submissions and frequent general blogging, which fills a retainer and moves very little. A reasonable test is to ask any quote which of those four categories each line item belongs in, and to notice the hesitation.

Should we hire locally?

It matters less than most people expect for the work itself and more than expected for one thing: whether anybody will sit down with you. Search work is done remotely regardless, and a firm two provinces away can do it competently. What a local supplier offers is the willingness to come in, look at how enquiries are actually handled, and photograph something. If a supplier is never going to visit, their location is irrelevant and should not be part of the decision.

How long should we commit for?

Long enough to be fair and not so long that leaving is expensive. Six months is usually the honest minimum for anything beyond the structural fixes to show, and a twelve month commitment with no break clause is asking you to take the entire risk. We would rather agree a short initial scope with a defined outcome, and let the result decide whether there is a second phase. A supplier confident in the work has no reason to need the lock-in.
