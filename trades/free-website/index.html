FAQ

Before You Apply

Is it really free?

Yes. We don't charge for the design or the build, and hosting is free once the site is in your GitHub. The only thing you provide is your own domain name. What we ask in return is listed above.

Do I need my own domain?

Yes. You provide the domain (your yourbusiness.ca address). If you already have one, we use it. If not, you buy it in your own name, and we help you connect it to the site.

Who owns the website?

You do. You set up a free GitHub organization for your business, and we transfer the site's repository into it. The code is yours from that point, whether you keep working with us or not.

Is there a limit on how many you'll build?

No fixed number. We review every application and build for every trades business that's a good fit.

I already have a website. Can I still apply?

Yes. A site that isn't bringing in calls is exactly what we want to fix.

Do I have to sign up for anything else?

No. There's no contract for ads, SEO or anything else. If you like the results and want more help, we can talk about it later, but it's not part of the deal.

Which trades can apply?

Roofers, plumbers, HVAC, electricians, landscapers, contractors, renovators, excavation, insulation, painters and more, based in Ontario and working today.
