FAQ

Questions Contractors Ask

How quickly will I see leads?

Paid ads can begin producing enquiries once tracking is verified and campaigns are live, while SEO builds over months. We won't promise a number before we've seen your market; on the strategy call we put the goals we'll be judged against in writing.

What makes NewLife different from other agencies?

The whole team is in-house. The person who buys your ads, the SEO lead, the creative director and the crew that films on your job sites are one team, and on your strategy call you talk to Cruz or Keegan, not a sales rep.

Which trades do you work with?

Construction, design-build, excavation and contracting, insulation, electrical and landscaping, plus plumbing, HVAC, roofing and renovation. If you sell a skilled service to homeowners or builders, we can scope it.

Do you handle the leads after they come in?

Yes. We build the CRM side too: instant responses, follow-up sequences and a pipeline so no lead sits in an inbox overnight.

What happens on the strategy call?

A real conversation about your numbers, your market and your goals. You leave with a written growth plan: yours to keep, hire us or not.

How is pricing set?

There are no bronze, silver or gold tiers. We scope exactly what your business needs, price it plainly and put it in a contract.
