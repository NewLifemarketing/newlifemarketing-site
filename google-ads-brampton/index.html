Same Brampton budget, two targeting setups. The difference is not reach, it is which auction you are standing in.

Questions

Google Ads In Brampton: Common Questions

Should we target a radius around our address?

Almost never in Brampton. The city borders Mississauga, Caledon, Vaughan and Toronto, so a circle drawn around a Brampton address spends a large share of the budget in markets with more advertisers, higher click costs and often a customer who will use somebody closer. The better shape comes from your own sales history: which postcodes have produced paying customers and how far people genuinely travel for what you sell. It is usually a smaller, odder shape than a circle, and it concentrates the same budget in a far less contested auction.

Why is our cost per lead rising?

Three causes account for most of it. The location setting is including people merely interested in your area rather than people in it, so you are competing in auctions you never chose. The conversion definition counts things that are not enquiries, so bidding is efficiently buying the cheapest of them. Or the negative list was never built from your own search terms report, so the budget is partly going on queries that were never relevant. All three are visible in an afternoon and none of them require changing agency.

How much should we spend?

Less than most Brampton accounts are spending, at least to begin with. Difficulty on the head term here is 3 out of 100, which means the Brampton auction itself is thin. The reason budgets feel inadequate is usually that most of the money is going into neighbouring auctions that are not thin. Tighten the targeting and the conversion definition first, measure for thirty days, and then decide the number. Scaling an account that is pointed at the wrong market just loses money faster.

Do we need separate landing pages?

Once the targeting and the conversion definition are sorted, yes, and it is usually the next biggest gain. Sending every ad to the home page means every visitor arrives at a page that has to serve all of them, so it serves none of them particularly well. A page per service line, matching the promise in the ad, lifts conversion rate on the same traffic and the same spend. It is work rather than a setting, which is why it tends to come second.

Can you audit an account without taking it over?

Yes, and often that is the right outcome. Read access is enough to see the location shape, the search terms report, the conversion definitions and the bid strategies, which is where nearly all of the recoverable waste sits. If the account is in good hands and simply needs three specific changes, we will say so and hand the list over. We would rather be useful than be the fourth agency in five years, which in this market is a common history.
