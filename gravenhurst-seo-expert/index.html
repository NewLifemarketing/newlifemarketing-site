A Stop Or A Destination

A gravenhurst seo expert should start with a question the town itself poses: a great many people pass through here on the way somewhere else, and whether you want to catch them or be the reason somebody sets out at all changes every search you should be trying to win.

Two different businesses

What A Gravenhurst SEO Expert Has To Decide First

Passing trade and destination trade are won with different pages.

A business catching passing trade is competing for a decision made in the car, minutes before it happens: open now, easy to stop at, obvious from the road, quick. Those searches are short, urgent and heavily weighted toward whoever is physically nearest.

A destination business is competing for a decision made days earlier, from somewhere else entirely. Those searches are longer, more specific and not bounded by proximity at all, which means they are won on pages rather than on a profile.

Most businesses here are some of both and have never decided which they are optimising for, which produces a site that does neither well: too vague for the planner and too slow for the person already driving.

Deciding is free and it takes an hour with your own records. What share of last year’s revenue came from people who had planned to come, and what share from people who happened to be passing?

Most businesses here guess, and the guess runs in a predictable direction: owners overestimate the planners, because planned customers are the memorable ones. The passing trade arrives, buys, and leaves without a conversation, which makes it invisible in recollection and perfectly visible in a till roll. An hour with last year’s takings settles it either way, and the answer frequently changes which half of the work is worth paying for at all.

If you want passing trade

Three Things That Win A Decision Made In The Car

All three are profile work rather than website work, and all three are free.

Hours that are right, everywhere

The profile, the site and any directory somebody else created. Somebody deciding in the car checks the first result they see, and a contradiction costs the visit.

Photographs that show the front of the building

Not a logo and not a stock interior. A driver is looking for something recognisable from the road, and that is a profile photograph rather than a design decision.

Reviews with recent dates

A stop is a low-stakes decision made quickly, and recent reviews are the fastest reassurance available. They work as a habit rather than an intention.

None of those needs a supplier and all of them are finished in an afternoon. They are also the work most likely to be undone, because nothing prompts anybody to revisit a listing that already exists. Put a note in the calendar for the week before the season opens and the week after it closes, and the problem never recurs.

If you want destination trade

The Pages Reach People Who Are Not Nearby

Proximity decides the map, so planners find you somewhere else entirely.

Somebody deciding from another city will not see you in a map result, however good the profile is. They find businesses through the ranked results below it, usually several days before they travel.

That audience wants different information: what you actually do, whether to book, how long it takes, what it costs, and what makes the trip worth it. Those are pages, and almost nobody in a town this size writes them properly.

Write them specifically rather than generally. A page about one thing you do, in detail, competes against very little; a page listing everything competes against everyone and wins nothing.

And write them out of season. Search work takes weeks to move, so a page published in June is helping next year, while the same page in March is in place when the planning actually happens.

Two kinds of search

Half Your Traffic Already Knew Your Name

And counting the two together makes every report look better than the work was.

People who search your business name were already coming. They heard about you, saw a vehicle, were recommended. They arrive through a search box and get counted as search traffic.

People who searched for what you do and had never heard of you are the ones the work is for. That is the number that should be reported, and separating the two takes one setting.

A report that combines them can show steady growth through a period in which nothing new was reached at all — because word of mouth got busier, which is good news about the business and no news about the marketing.

What a gravenhurst seo expert should separate in a report

Ask any gravenhurst seo expert to split branded from unbranded before agreeing anything. It takes a minute, it halves most reported figures, and the half that is left is the only part either of you can take credit for.

Reading the season

How A Gravenhurst SEO Expert Should Read A Seasonal Market

In a market with a season, month-on-month comparison is actively misleading.

Demand for almost everything here rises and falls on a yearly cycle that has nothing to do with anybody’s marketing. A quiet February after a busy August is not a decline; it is February.

Any arrangement judged month against month will be declared a failure every autumn and a triumph every spring, and both conclusions will be wrong.

So the comparison is the same month a year earlier, and the useful total is a rolling twelve months, which smooths the cycle out entirely.

That requires a year of data before anything is assessable, which is an uncomfortable thing to say at the start and considerably better than the alternative, which is drawing conclusions from noise.

The Free Look, And What A Paid One Adds

Most of what an audit finds can be found in an hour and should not be charged for.

Whether the listing is complete, whether the pages load, whether anything obvious is broken, and what each existing page is currently trying to win. That is an hour of looking and we would rather tell you than sell you a document about it.

What costs money is the next part: finding out what people genuinely search in your category with volumes attached, deciding which page should own which phrase, and writing the pages. That is real work with a real price.

The distinction matters because the first part is routinely packaged as a paid audit, delivered as forty pages, and used to justify a retainer.

Should we aim at passing trade or destination trade?

Decide deliberately rather than by default, because they are won differently. Passing trade is profile work — hours, photographs from the road, recent reviews — and destination trade is pages, read days before anybody travels. A site that has not chosen tends to do neither well.

How do we reach people planning from another city?

Through the ranked results rather than the map. Proximity keeps you out of their map result entirely. They want to know what you actually do, whether to book, how long it takes and what makes the trip worth it — pages almost nobody in a town this size writes properly.

What is the fastest free improvement?

The Business Profile, and specifically the photographs and the hours. A driver deciding whether to stop is looking for something recognisable from the road and an opening time they can trust, and both are set once when a listing is created and almost never revisited.

When should pages be published?

Out of season. Search work takes weeks to move, so a page published in June is helping next year while the same page in March is in place when people are actually planning. The quiet months are also when somebody has an hour to help write it.
