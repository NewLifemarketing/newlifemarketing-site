A long sale

Mississauga Sells To Businesses, And Businesses Buy Slowly

A Mississauga of head offices, logistics operators and the firms around Pearson has more purchases that take months rather than minutes than anywhere else in Ontario.

Observed across B2B client work

Consumer paid media is a tidy loop. Somebody searches, clicks, buys within a few days, and the platform can say with reasonable confidence which ad did it. Every default setting in every ads account assumes that shape, including in Mississauga where a great many accounts do not have it.

A Mississauga business selling to another business does not have that shape. Somebody searches, reads, leaves, discusses it internally, comes back directly two months later, gets quoted, waits for a budget cycle, and signs in the next quarter. The click that started it is long outside the attribution window, so the report attributes the sale to a branded search or to nothing at all.

The practical result is that the campaigns doing the real work look unprofitable and the campaigns capturing people who already knew you look excellent. A ppc agency mississauga firms hire will then do exactly what the data suggests, which is to shift budget toward the second group and away from the first. The account improves on the dashboard and the pipeline quietly empties.

What the report hides

Last Click Rewards The Campaign That Finished The Job

The ad that introduced a Mississauga buyer gets no credit. The ad they clicked after already deciding gets all of it.

Branded search is where this shows up most starkly. Bidding on your own company name produces spectacular numbers because everybody clicking it had already decided to find you. It is worth running, usually, and it is not producing demand. Treating it as the best performing campaign is reading the last step of a journey as though it were the whole journey.

Nothing about this is a flaw in the platform. It reports what it can observe, and it cannot observe a conversation in somebody’s procurement meeting eight weeks later. The flaw is in treating the report as the answer when ppc mississauga accounts routinely serve sales cycles the report was never built to measure.

Questions

Google Ads In Mississauga: Common Questions

Our branded campaign performs best. Should we spend more there?

Probably not much more, because it is not producing demand. Everybody clicking your company name had already decided to find you, so the conversions look excellent and the campaign is recording a decision rather than causing one. It is usually worth running to avoid handing those clicks to a competitor, and treating it as your best performer is reading the last step of a long journey as though it were the whole thing.

How do we know which ads actually work then?

Ask. Add a question to every sales conversation about how the customer first heard of you, and write the answers down. It costs nothing, takes thirty seconds, and after twenty customers it is more reliable than any attribution model for a business with a long cycle. Then wire the CRM into the ads account so bidding learns from signed business rather than from form fills. The simple version beats the sophisticated one almost every time here.

Should we lengthen the attribution window?

Yes, and it helps less than people hope. Extending it captures more of the journey and still cannot see the parts that happened off-platform: the internal discussion, the forwarded email, the colleague who recommended you. It is worth setting to the longest available and worth treating as an improvement rather than a solution. The customer conversations remain the more reliable source for a business selling on a multi-month cycle.

We sell online and to businesses. Does that matter?

A great deal, and it is the most common structural fault we find in Mississauga. Product sales convert within days and service sales take months, so running both under one bid strategy means the algorithm optimises toward the fast one because that is where it sees results. The slow half quietly loses its budget. Separating them into different campaigns with different goals, or different accounts entirely, is a one-time piece of work that resolves it.

How long before we can judge the account?

Longer than feels comfortable. A Mississauga campaign serving a four-month sales cycle genuinely cannot be assessed in a quarter, because the first customers it produced have not signed yet. We report quarterly so nothing is opaque, and we would resist switching anything off on less than a year of evidence. The cost of cutting a working campaign in a long-cycle business does not show up for another two quarters, by which point the connection is invisible.
