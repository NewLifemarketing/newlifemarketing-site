Meta Ads Agency Oshawa

A Phone On A Train

A meta ads agency oshawa is buying attention in a specific and unusual condition: a phone, held in one hand, on a train or a platform, with the sound off and about two seconds before a thumb moves — which is exactly the moment search advertising cannot reach at all.

The moment it reaches

What A Meta Ads Agency Oshawa Is Actually Buying

Search intercepts a decision already underway. This one happens before anybody is deciding anything.

Two hours a day of commuting is two hours a day of a captive audience holding a phone, and almost none of that time is spent searching for local services. It is spent scrolling.

That is the whole proposition. Reaching somebody during it does not produce an enquiry that afternoon; it produces recognition that turns into an enquiry weeks later, frequently through a search for your name.

Which means judging it against search advertising on a same-week cost per enquiry will always make it look worse, and cutting it on that basis is the commonest way a search account quietly becomes more expensive three months later.

The twenty searches a month naming a local supplier for this tell you something else: nobody here shops for it by typing, so every supplier you meet arrived by approach rather than comparison, and the questions you ask have to replace the shortlist.

What the creative has to survive

Sound Off, One Hand, Two Seconds

Four production decisions that matter more than the budget.

Captions on everything

Checked rather than auto-generated and left. A meaningful share of viewing happens with the sound off, and on a train that share is higher.

The point in the first two seconds

Not a logo, not a title card, not a slow pan. Whatever the piece is about has to be visible before a thumb moves.

Vertical, or at least square

A landscape video on a phone held in one hand wastes most of the screen, and the screen is the whole of the attention you bought.

Real footage rather than stock

Recognition is the entire objective, and a stock interior is not recognisable as anything. The job going in, the finished result, the person who answers the phone.

None of those four costs more to produce. They are decisions taken before filming, and taken afterwards they mean editing the same material twice, which is the commonest avoidable cost in a campaign that was otherwise planned carefully.

Where it earns

Three Jobs Worth Paying For Here

Each is testable, and one of them almost nobody runs.

Retargeting first. The audience has already visited your site, reaching them again costs a fraction of reaching a stranger, and in a commuter city a visit on a phone and an enquiry from a desk are frequently days apart.

Recruitment second, and it is the use most businesses never consider. In a city where a share of the workforce commutes an hour each way, an advertisement for a comparable job ten minutes from home is a genuinely strong offer, and almost nobody is making it.

Demand creation third: reaching people before they are looking, which is the only thing on this list that search cannot do in any form. It is also the slowest to show a return, which is why it needs its own standard rather than being compared with a search account.

Each needs a stated end or a fixed job. Without one, a campaign becomes a permanent line item and the first anybody reviews it is a year later.

There is one more thing worth settling before any of it runs, and it costs nothing. Decide what you would accept as evidence that the channel is working, in advance and in writing, because a channel judged on a standard chosen afterwards is always judged on whichever standard makes the most recent month look worst.

How the budget divides

What A Meta Ads Agency Oshawa Account Should Split The Money Between

Two jobs, two very different costs per result, and conflating them hides both.

Reaching people who have never heard of you is expensive per result and is the only thing that grows the business. Reaching people who already visited is cheap, converts well, and reaches nobody new.

An account reporting a single blended figure is averaging those two, and the blended number will look respectable while almost nothing is being spent on growth.

So ask for the split: what share went to each, and what each produced. The second half flatters the whole account and a supplier under pressure will quietly let it grow.

A reasonable starting shape is most of the money aimed outward, with a small, permanent, cheap campaign catching the people who nearly acted. Reversed, the account is a maintenance arrangement.

What a meta ads agency oshawa report should separate

The two halves separately, every month. A meta ads agency oshawa report that blends them is reporting a flattering average, and the drift from one shape to the other happens slowly enough that nobody notices it in a single month.

How often to look

A Slow Channel Judged On A Fast Schedule

Weekly scrutiny of something that moves monthly produces constant, random change.

Paid social reaches people before they are ready. The enquiry arrives weeks later, from somebody who may not remember where they first encountered you, and nothing in a weekly report captures that.

Judged every Monday, the channel looks erratic, and the response is to change something — which resets whatever the system had learned and makes the next week equally unreadable.

So the review cadence should be monthly at minimum and quarterly for anything with a long decision. Between reviews, the only weekly task is checking nothing has broken.

That is uncomfortable for everybody and it is the correct arrangement. The alternative is an account optimised against noise, which over a year drifts somewhere nobody intended.

It also sets the minimum sensible term. Judging a meta ads agency oshawa account fairly takes a quarter, which is worth agreeing at the start rather than arguing about in week six.
