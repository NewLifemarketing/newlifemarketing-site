Straight answers

SEO Vaughan FAQ

Straight answers before you ever book a call.

Why not just target the city keyword?

Take it — 590 a month in Vaughan is worth having. The problem is that every competitor is working from the same report, so the city cluster is the most crowded phrase in the market and the one where budget buys the least. The fifteen secondaries are all rearrangements of one word that happens to be an administrative boundary. What a resident says about where they live is a different word, and it is not in the report.

Should we build a page for each community?

For a Vaughan site, only once two things are true: the research says the demand is real, and you have work done in that community to write about. Neither alone is enough. Pages built on an assumption about volume, or built from a template with the name swapped, are the doorway pattern — and a city with five well-known community names makes that trap especially easy. Three real pages beat thirty generated ones, every time.

Does being a small business put us at a disadvantage?

In the Vaughan map results, usually the opposite. Proximity is measured to the person searching rather than to the city centre, so a business in Woodbridge and one in Maple are rarely competing for the same three results at all. Being small and nearby beats being large and across town. That advantage is completely invisible from a city-level rank report, which is one more reason not to run the engagement from one.

What is the fastest improvement here?

The Vaughan Business Profile category and service area, in that order, and both take minutes. Category is the strongest single signal in local results. The service area is where you name the communities you actually go to — and on most profiles we open it is empty or set to a radius nobody chose, which in a city of five centres throws away the thing the map leans on hardest.

How contested is this market really?

Contested enough that the Vaughan head term is not where a smaller budget should be fighting. There are 323,103 people here and a very large number of businesses serving them, and every agency pitching in this market is reading the same fifteen phrases. That is precisely why the question worth paying for is the one those fifteen phrases cannot answer.
