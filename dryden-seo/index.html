Dryden SEO: The Competition Is Absence

7,388

Statistics Canada, 2021 Census of Population

112.7

Statistics Canada, 2021 Census of Population

Advice written for a city assumes competitors who are actively working on their results. Here the realistic picture is a handful of businesses with a listing somebody set up years ago, a page on a social platform, and nothing else.

That changes what dryden seo work means. It is not out-competing anybody; it is being the only one present, which is a far cheaper objective and one a business can reach without a supplier.

It also means the first wins arrive quickly. A correct profile and one page per service will frequently take the result inside weeks, because nothing is defending it.

The short list

Three Things, In Order, And Then Stop

Most businesses here never finish this list, let alone need anything beyond it.

A correct Business Profile

Categories, service area, hours, photographs that are current. It decides a large share of the local result, it costs an afternoon, and in a town this size it is frequently the whole game.

Reviews, collected on purpose

A specific moment in the job when somebody asks, and a short link that makes it a thirty-second task. In a small town a handful of recent reviews is a genuinely strong position.

One page per thing you sell

Named the way customers name it. Five services on one page ranks for none of them; five pages cost the same to write and produce five results.

After those three, the honest advice in a market this size is to stop and watch for a quarter. Dryden SEO work beyond that point is frequently selling a solution to a problem this town does not have.

The bigger half

The Region Is Larger Than The Town

Forty local searches is the visible market. For many businesses here it is the smaller part.

Local results are bounded by where the searcher is standing. The ranked results underneath them are not, and for anything supplied, delivered, shipped or done remotely, that is the more valuable half of dryden seo work.

Those pages are slower to produce, because a page about a real process cannot be written by somebody who does not understand it. An hour with whoever does, per page, is the genuine constraint and it is worth scheduling rather than working around.

It is also the only route a town of 7,388 offers to a market larger than itself, which makes it the part of this work with the highest ceiling by a wide margin.

What not to buy

What Dryden SEO Should Never Include

Two things that are sold everywhere and make no sense at this scale.

The first is a monthly optimisation retainer with no end point. Link building and technical overhauls exist because contested results need pushing against; here there is nothing pushing back, and the same spending produces a change nobody can detect.

The second is a page for every nearby place name. Twenty near-identical pages naming twenty communities compete with each other, say nothing specific about any of them, and are transparently what they are to a reader who lives in one.

The honest version works better and takes longer: write about the places you genuinely serve, with real detail about what changes out there — the travel, the scheduling, the minimum job.

If a proposal arrives with a monthly figure and no stated end, ask what the first ninety days deliver and what happens in month seven. A supplier willing to say “less, and here is why” is worth keeping.

Judging it

Count Customers, Not Visitors

A position report is flattering and useless here. So is a traffic chart: the difference between twelve visitors and twenty is noise, and neither number says whether anybody worth having arrived.

Count enquiries by landing page, and ask every arriving customer how they heard of you. In a town this size most new business arrives by routes no tracking parameter survives, and that question is the only record of them.

Read it over a rolling quarter, and expect the honest answer to be small numbers. One additional customer a month from dryden seo work is a good outcome in a town of 7,388 if that customer is worth having.

Which is why the value of a customer is the first number to settle. Without it, a result of one enquiry a month looks like failure and might be the best return in the business.

Keep the answers year on year rather than discarding them each quarter. At these volumes a single year is the shortest period that shows a trend at all, and the comparison between this year and last is the only honest evidence that any of the work is doing something.

How we work

Build One Thing At A Time, Then Stop

Dull method, and in a market this size the stopping is part of it.

We start with what is already claimed: what your site ranks for now, what the profile and directory listings say, and where two of your own pages are competing for one search. Then one cluster at a time, each with a page that owns it and a record of the decision.

What dryden seo work should hand back

Dryden SEO work with us leaves you holding the registry of which page owns which phrase, the profile audit with its corrections applied, and a short list of what to do next in-house. In a town this size that list is usually shorter than anybody expects and we would rather say so than extend it.

We are in Minesing, a long way south, which changes nothing for this work and matters only for filming, where travel appears as its own visible line rather than folded into a fee.

What A Dryden SEO Quote Should Answer

How many hours a month, spent on what, and who does them. What happens to the pages if you leave. Whether anything is being bought on your behalf, and from whom. And what the first month covers, because on most businesses the first month is corrections that cost nothing and should not be charged for as though they were a strategy.

Next step

What Is One Customer Worth To You Over Everything They Will Buy?

That number decides everything else on this page, and most owners have never worked it out properly.

Not what one job is worth. What somebody is worth over the whole time they stay with you, including the work they send you. A trade in a town this size often finds the second number is several times the first, and once you know it, most of the arguments about what is worth spending answer themselves.

Then compare it against what you are currently doing to get one. In a market where the competition is mostly absence, the answer is usually that an afternoon on the profile would do it, and nobody has had the afternoon.

Send us the name of the business and we will look at what is there, whether or not you ever want anybody on a retainer.

Straight answers

Dryden SEO FAQ

Straight answers before you ever book a call.

Is this worth doing in a town of 7,388?

It depends on what a customer is worth, and the bar is lower than you think. A meaningful share of local competitors have no website, no current listing and no reviews, so a correct profile and a page per service frequently take the result inside weeks. One extra customer a month can be a strong return if that customer is valuable.

What should we do first?

Correct the Business Profile, collect reviews on purpose, and publish one page per thing you sell — in that order. All three are free or nearly so, and in a town this size most businesses never finish the list, let alone need anything beyond it.

Can we reach customers outside the town?

Through the ranked results rather than the map, and for many businesses that is the larger half. Phrases naming exactly what you supply carry a handful of searches each, are competed for by almost nobody, and reach a market far larger than 7,388 people.

Should we buy a monthly retainer?

Rarely. Monthly optimisation exists because contested results need pushing against, and here there is very little pushing back. Ask what the first ninety days deliver and what happens in month seven; a supplier willing to say that the answer is very little is worth keeping.
