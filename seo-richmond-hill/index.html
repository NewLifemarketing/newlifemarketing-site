The market

Richmond Hill SEO Starts With A Settled, Crowded City

Statistics Canada counted 202,022 residents in Richmond Hill in 2021, up only 3.6% from 2016. They live on about 101 square kilometres, at roughly 2,000 people per square kilometre.

That slow growth changes the search problem. In a city adding subdivisions, a new business can win customers who have no habits yet. In a settled city like this, most households already have a dentist, an accountant and a favourite bakery. Winning search in Richmond Hill usually means taking a click from a competitor the customer already knows, so the page and the profile have to give a reason to switch.

The customers are worth the fight. The median household income in 2020 was $102,000. In 2023 the City counted more than 5,180 businesses, and in the 2021 census the largest single industry among working residents was professional, scientific and technical services, at 15,485 people. Retail employs 11,735 residents, health care and social assistance 10,650, and finance and insurance 9,740.

Movement matters too. Of residents who travel to a regular workplace, about 30% work inside Richmond Hill, a third work elsewhere in York Region, and the rest head further into Ontario, including Toronto. Close to 87% of residents who commute go by car. In a city that drives this much, plenty of searches get made on the move along Highway 7, Yonge Street and the 404, which is one reason the map pack matters so much here.

Languages

SEO In Richmond Hill Means Pages In Mandarin, Cantonese And Farsi

Most local SEO plans skip this. In Richmond Hill it is too big to skip.

The 2021 census found about 50,000 residents whose mother tongue is a Chinese language: roughly 25,800 Mandarin and 22,700 Cantonese. Another 19,400 or so learned Iranian Persian first. Across the city, the census found 126 different mother tongues, and about four in ten residents speak a language other than English or French most often at home. China, Iran and Hong Kong are the three most common birthplaces of Richmond Hill's immigrants.

For search, this cuts two ways. Only about 6.4% of residents speak neither English nor French, so most can search in English and English pages still matter. But a query typed in Farsi or Chinese characters can only be answered by a page written in that language, and Google's own guidance is specific about how to do it: Google reads the language from the visible text on the page, recommends a separate URL for each language version, and warns against redirecting visitors by browser language.

So when a Richmond Hill client serves Mandarin, Cantonese or Farsi speakers, we plan a real language section: its own URLs, hreflang links between versions, and a Google Business Profile description and service list that match. The translation itself comes from a fluent writer, either someone on your team who knows how your customers talk or a professional translator. We build the structure, research the searches and check that Google indexes each version.

The problems

What Ranking In Richmond Hill Is Up Against

Six things make local search here harder than it looks from the outside.

Yonge Street is one long map

The city stretches along Yonge from the Highway 7 area to Oak Ridges. A clinic near 16th Avenue may hold the top three for a search made nearby and vanish for the same search made near Elgin Mills.

The neighbours rank here too

Markham and Vaughan firms sit minutes from the city line and can add Richmond Hill to their service areas. Your profile competes with theirs on every search near a border.

English-only sites miss searches

With tens of thousands of Chinese and Persian speakers in the city, a site in one language can't answer queries typed in another, however good the English pages are.

Plaza and suite addresses

If your business sits in a multi-unit plaza or office park, a missing suite number or an address that doesn't match your website confuses Google and customers alike.

Regulated professions

Richmond Hill dentists, physiotherapists and lawyers each answer to an Ontario regulator with its own advertising rules. Review requests and content get checked against yours before they go live.

One growth node, not new suburbs

The City names Richmond Hill Centre, at Yonge and Highway 7, its primary intensification area. Most of the city's planned change is aimed at that one spot, so businesses near it have the most to gain from pages written early.

Across the city

Local SEO Across Richmond Hill, From Langstaff To Lake Wilcox

Each part of the city has its own search pattern, so we plan area by area.

Langstaff and Richmond Hill Centre. The land around Yonge and Highway 7 is the City's primary growth area, planned as "a new downtown" around two stations on the Yonge North Subway Extension. Metrolinx's Bridge Station will sit between Highway 7 and Highway 407 and connect to Langstaff GO. The subway won't open until after the Ontario Line, but pages about this area written now will have time to rank before it does.

The business parks off the 404. Canada Computers broke ground in Barker Business Park on a new headquarters and main distribution centre, and Merry Electronics chose Richmond Hill for its North American head office. In the Headford Business Park, next to Highway 404 north of 16th Avenue, Amazon Canada opened a 20,800-square-metre delivery station in 2024. B2B firms here are found by buyers and office managers, so detailed service pages matter more than the map pack.

The historic downtown and Mill Pond. The City's downtown plan covers Yonge between Harding Boulevard and Levendale Road, near Mill Pond Park, which hosts concerts and the Winter Carnival. For independent shops and restaurants nearby, event dates and walk-by traffic are worth planning search around.

Bayview Hill and the east side. Home to the Bayview Hill Community Centre and Pool. For family services such as dentistry, tutoring, physiotherapy and renovation, we plan for searches made from home, close by, where being nearest counts.

Yonge and Bernard and the north end. The Official Plan names Yonge and Bernard, north of Elgin Mills, as a key development area. Retail and service searches in this stretch pull toward whoever ranks nearest, so a business at 16th Avenue may need a dedicated page to show up here at all.

Oak Ridges and Lake Wilcox. Oak Ridges sits wholly on the Oak Ridges Moraine, around Lake Wilcox, the largest kettle lake on the moraine, at the north end of the city. A business near Highway 7 is a long drive from these customers, so reaching them takes pages and profile details that name Oak Ridges.

Straight answers

SEO Richmond Hill Questions Owners Ask Us

Do I need my website in Chinese or Farsi to rank in Richmond Hill?

Not always. If your customers search in English, English pages are enough. If your front desk regularly takes calls in Mandarin, Cantonese or Farsi, a language section with its own URLs is usually worth it, because Google can only match a query in that language to a page written in it.

Can't I just add a translate button to my site?

A button that translates on the fly helps visitors who are already on your site, but Google may never find or index those versions. Google's guidance is a separate URL for each language, linked with hreflang, and no automatic redirects based on the visitor's browser language.

How do I get into the map pack along Yonge Street?

Start with an accurate Google Business Profile: the right primary category, your full address with suite number, real photos and a steady flow of reviews. Then build pages for the areas you serve, because the map pack favours businesses near the searcher. A business near 16th Avenue won't show up evenly in Oak Ridges without that extra work.

Markham and Vaughan competitors rank for Richmond Hill searches. Can we beat them?

Often, yes, on searches made inside Richmond Hill. A competitor's service-area listing doesn't give them a Richmond Hill address or pages about Richmond Hill neighbourhoods. A business with a real address in the city, complete profile details and pages about the areas it serves has an edge that a border listing can't copy.

How long does SEO take in a market this competitive?

Profile fixes and address clean-up can move map rankings within weeks. New pages usually take a few months to settle, and a new language section starts from zero, so plan on a longer build there.

I run a regulated health practice. Can you still ask patients for reviews?

That depends on your regulator. Some Ontario health colleges, dentistry among them, restrict testimonials in advertising, so we check your college's rules before setting up any review requests or patient-facing content, and we adjust the plan to fit.

Will the subway extension change search in Richmond Hill?

It will change where people live and walk. Metrolinx forecasts more than 94,100 daily boardings on the Yonge North Subway Extension, with stations planned around Highway 7 and Highway 407. Businesses near Richmond Hill Centre that build pages about that area now give themselves years to rank before new residents start searching.
