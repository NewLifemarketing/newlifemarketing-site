Where the rest is

Peterborough Is A Service Centre, And Service Centres Get Searched From Outside

The city holds 83,651 people. The metropolitan area holds 128,624, and the businesses here serve well past even that.

People drive into Peterborough for a trade, a clinic, a supplier or a contractor from townships that have no equivalent business of their own. When they search, they frequently search with the name of the place they are standing in, because that is how people describe where they need something done.

A site that mentions only the city has nothing to match that with. It is not outranked for those searches, it is absent from them, and nothing in the reporting will say so, because you cannot report an impression you never had.

The honest version

A Page Per Town Is Either The Answer Or The Problem

The tactic is right and the usual execution is exactly what Google penalises, so the difference is worth being precise about.

Build them slowly

Three good ones beat thirty thin ones, and thirty thin ones published in a week is the clearest possible signal of what they are. There is no deadline on this; the places are not going anywhere.

Write from real jobs

The material for a page about a place is the work you have done there. An seo consultant peterborough businesses can trust will ask for that before writing a word, because without it there is nothing to say that is true of the place.

Skip the ones you cannot fill

If there is nothing honest to write about a township, do not publish a page for it. A missing page costs you searches you were not going to win anyway; a thin one puts the rest of the site in the same bucket.

Questions

Search In Peterborough: Common Questions

Why not just target the main city keyword?

Because it is one contest. The sixteen phrases in this cluster are rearrangements of the same request, Google reads them as one intent, and one page wins or loses the set. That is 170 searches a month with a ceiling you can calculate before you start. It is cheap at a difficulty of 14 and worth having, but a plan whose best possible outcome is already known is not much of a plan in a market this size.

Where are the rest of the searches?

Outside the city. Peterborough is a service centre for townships that have no equivalent business of their own, and people search with the name of the place they are standing in. The city holds 83,651 people and the metropolitan area 128,624, and most firms here serve past even that. A site that mentions only the city is not outranked for those searches, it is absent from them, and no report will show you an impression you never had.

Is a page for every town not just doorway spam?

It is, when it is done the usual way: one template, the name swapped, nothing in it that is only true of that place, thirty of them linked from a footer list and published in a week. The test is whether a paragraph would stop being true if you deleted the place name. Done properly each page is built from real jobs done there, there are three of them rather than thirty, and the ones you have nothing honest to say about do not get published at all.

What is the single fastest improvement?

The Business Profile service area, almost every time. It takes an afternoon, it governs most of what happens in local results, and on more than half the profiles we open it is empty, set to a radius nobody chose, or still listing an address the business left. Until that is right, work on the website is being judged in a contest the profile has already conceded.

How long before any of this shows?

The profile changes can move local results inside a few weeks. Pages take longer: three to six months before a new page for a place settles into a position, and longer in a market where you are also building the reason for anyone to link to it. Anyone promising the head term inside a quarter is describing a difficulty of 14, which is real, and quietly not describing the part of the work that actually grows the business.
