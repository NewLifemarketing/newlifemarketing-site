Why it matters

Why Your Business Needs Go-To-Market Consulting

Accelerate market entry

Launches stall in the gap between "the product is ready" and "someone knows how to sell it." A GTM plan closes that gap before launch week, so the first quarter is execution rather than argument.

Maximise sales efficiency

When the ICP is vague, reps spend their time on deals that were never winnable. Sharpening who you sell to — and what makes you the obvious choice — lowers cost of acquisition without touching the budget.

Adapt and thrive

Markets move: a competitor repositions, a category gets crowded, a buyer's priorities shift. A living GTM strategy gives you a way to re-decide deliberately instead of reacting campaign by campaign.

The framework

Five Decisions, Then A Dated Plan

Every pillar above is a decision someone has to own. We make them explicit, write them down, and then cost the plan that follows.

The five pillars

  • Market — addressable market, segmentation, competitive set and where the white space is
  • Customer — ICP definition, buying committee, trigger events, the job being done
  • Offer — positioning, proof, objection handling, pricing and packaging
  • Channel — which channels earn budget, which are noise, and what each must return
  • Motion — sales model, territory and comp implications, service levels, handoffs

The 90-day track

  • Days 0–30 · Evidence — customer and lost-deal interviews, competitive teardown, data review, internal alignment sessions
  • Days 31–60 · Build — positioning and messaging locked, channel plan costed, content and enablement built, tracking wired
  • Days 61–90 · Launch & learn — campaigns live, weekly readouts against the metrics agreed on day one, plan revised on evidence

Straight answers

Go-To-Market Strategy FAQ

What GTM consulting actually covers, who owns it, how long it takes and what it costs — answered plainly.

What is a go-to-market strategy?

A go-to-market strategy is the plan for how you take an offer to a market and win: which segment you are selling to, what your position is against the alternatives, how the offer is packaged and priced, which channels carry it, and what the sales motion looks like. It is a set of decisions with numbers attached — distinct from a marketing plan, which is the activity that follows once those decisions are made.

What is go-to-market consulting?

It is bringing in an outside team to make and pressure-test those decisions with you. In practice that means interviewing your customers and lost deals, tearing down the competitive set, defining the ICP, writing the positioning, costing the channel plan, and producing a dated roadmap your team can execute. Good GTM consulting ends with a plan someone owns, not a presentation.

What is the role of the go-to-market team?

The GTM team is everyone whose work touches the revenue motion — product marketing, demand generation, sales, sales enablement, customer success and often finance and product. Their shared job is making sure the market hears one coherent story and gets one coherent experience from first touch to renewal. The common failure is each function optimising its own metric while the story fragments.

Why do you need GTM services?

Usually because something specific is wrong: a launch underperformed, a new segment isn't converting, sales and marketing disagree on who the customer is, or a competitor repositioned and your messaging suddenly reads like theirs. An outside team brings the customer evidence and the willingness to say the uncomfortable thing — which is much harder to do from inside the org chart.

What does a GTM consulting firm do?

Research (customer interviews, lost-deal analysis, competitive and market review), decisions (segmentation, ICP, positioning, pricing input, channel strategy), documentation (messaging framework, battlecards, roadmap), and enablement (getting sales and marketing operating from the same plan). Some, including us, will also execute the plan afterwards rather than handing it over and leaving.

Is GTM strategy just B2B marketing?

No. Marketing is one input. A go-to-market strategy also covers pricing and packaging, the sales motion and compensation implications, channel and partner decisions, and the service model after the sale. Marketing executes a big part of it, but a GTM strategy that only covers marketing is a marketing plan wearing a bigger name.

How long does a GTM engagement take, and what does it cost?

A focused engagement runs about 90 days: roughly 30 days gathering evidence, 30 building and locking the strategy, and 30 launching against it with weekly readouts. Cost is scoped to the work — the number of segments, whether we run the customer interviews, and whether we execute afterwards — and quoted plainly before anything starts. There is no package tier and no public price list.
