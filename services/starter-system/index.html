Content · A full 30-day plan

Every Day Covered, Written And Posted For You

You send what you have and we turn it into a month. The plan is written, designed, scheduled and posted to your accounts every day, by us. You approve it before it goes out, and that is the extent of your involvement.

  1. 1

    You send us what you have

    Photos from your phone, clips from a job, anything you have already shot. No equipment and no planning: just send it as you go.

  2. 2

    We pull from your website

    Finished work, service descriptions and past projects. Anything already online becomes content.

  3. 3

    We pull from your reviews

    What customers have already said about you is the most persuasive content you own, and most businesses never use it twice.

  4. 4

    We build the month

    A complete calendar: what goes out, on what day, with the caption written and the graphic designed. Sent to you before the month starts, approved in advance, then scheduled.

What it looks like

Content we have built for client accounts.

What you do not have to do. Write anything. Design anything. Remember to post. Think about what to say on a Tuesday. The point is that consistency stops depending on you having a spare hour.
The honest version. Content built from photos you send and work already on your site is good. Content filmed properly on site is far better, and that is the whole difference between the two levels. If you can stretch to it, you should.

Our Crew Comes To You And Films The Real Thing

Once a month our crew comes to you and films the work, the space, the people and the finished result. That one day fills the whole content plan. You stop having to remember to take photos, and the content stops being built from whatever happened to be on your website.

Roughly thirty new indexed pages a month, each written around a real search. A page published today is still bringing people in next year, and each day's blog also becomes that day's Google profile update, so one piece of writing covers two surfaces.

Why the Google profile matters most at this stage. When someone searches for what you do near them, the map results sit above everything, above the ads and above the websites. For a business without an ad budget that position is the single most valuable thing you can own, and it is earned rather than bought. Most businesses set the profile up once and never touch it again. Questions

Before You Ask

What is the Starter System?

The foundation, built properly before there is a budget for ads: a website, a search presence and a full 30-day content plan, all running every month, with a CRM underneath so enquiries do not get lost.

What is the difference between the Starter System and Plus?

Plus is the Starter System in full, with one thing added: our crew comes to you once a month and films. That produces four videos and twenty-six graphics and carousels of your actual work.

Do I have to send photos?

At the Starter System level, yes: photos and clips from your phone are one of the three things we build from, alongside your website and your reviews. At Plus we film it, so you do not.

How much of my time does it take?

You approve the month's plan before it goes out and take a twenty-minute call every Friday. You do not write, design, schedule or post anything.

Do I need a new website?

Not necessarily. If yours is not good enough we build a new one free as part of the offer, and either way we maintain it every month.

Do I need a CRM?

Only if the sale happens through a conversation: enquiries, quotes or bookings. If people buy straight from your website, there is nothing to manage and we will say so.

Is there a video report?

Not at this level. You get a live dashboard and a call every Friday. The weekly video report comes with the Growth Partner and Trades systems.
