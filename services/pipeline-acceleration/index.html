The problem

Why This Exists

Deals stall for reasons that have nothing to do with the product: the champion goes quiet, procurement adds a step, a new stakeholder appears with a question nobody prepared for. Meanwhile the forecast keeps showing them as live.

What we do

What Pipeline Acceleration Actually Involves

Find the stalls honestly

Before adding more pipeline

Stage-to-stage velocity analysis and a review of open deals — where they stick, for how long, and what changed when they moved.

Multi-thread the account

Single-threaded deals are fragile

Reach the rest of the buying committee deliberately, so the deal does not depend on one person's calendar or continued employment.

Arm sales with what moves committees

Not a brand deck

Business cases, objection handling, security and procurement answers, and content the champion can forward internally without editing.

Support live bids

For as long as the deal is live

Targeted content and executive air cover around specific open opportunities through review and approval.

Our methodology

The Account Engine

Every account-based programme we run moves through the same six stages, in the same order. It is the reason two different clients get the same rigour rather than whatever their strategist happened to prefer.

01 · Objective

The number the programme is judged on, the motion that gets there, and who owns each part. Agreed with sales and finance before anything is built.

02 · ICP

Firmographic, technographic and behavioural definition of accounts that can buy at your size and expand afterwards — not everyone who could theoretically use the product.

03 · Target accounts

A finite target account list, tiered 1:1 / 1:few / 1:many by what each account is worth, scored for fit and signed off by sales.

04 · Value proposition

What changes for the economic buyer, the champion, the technical evaluator and the blocker — each answered separately, because they are asking different questions.

05 · Playbooks

The channels, content and sequences that run against each tier, with entry and exit criteria so a play either earns its place or stops.

06 · Delivery & measurement

Account coverage, buying-committee reach, pipeline created and influenced, velocity and win rate — reported with the attribution window stated.

Measurement

What Gets Reported

The numbers

  • Stage-to-stage velocity
  • Committee members engaged per open deal
  • Stalled-deal recovery rate
  • Win rate on supported deals
  • Average deal size
  • Time from proposal to decision

How we report them

  • Platform-reported values labelled as such
  • The attribution window stated on every view
  • Early-stage data flagged as early-stage
  • No cross-platform summing presented as revenue
  • Nothing published we cannot show you the source for

The house rule does not change with the service.

Straight answers

Pipeline Acceleration FAQ

Is this a sales service or a marketing one?

Both, which is why it usually falls between the two and never gets done. It runs out of marketing but it is judged on sales outcomes and requires sales to participate.

How quickly does it show?

Faster than most programmes, because the pipeline already exists. Movement on stalled deals often appears within a quarter.

What if our problem is not enough pipeline rather than slow pipeline?

Then we will tell you that and point you at demand generation or ABM instead. Accelerating an empty pipeline achieves nothing.
