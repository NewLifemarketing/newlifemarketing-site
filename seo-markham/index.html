The Markham market

Markham SEO In A City Of 10,400 Companies

Markham counted 338,503 residents in the 2021 Census, the largest municipality in York Region and the 16th largest in Canada. The City now puts its population at over 353,000.

The City reports 10,400 companies, more than 400 Canadian head offices, and over 1,000 technology and life sciences firms employing 37,000 people out of a workforce of about 167,500. IBM, AMD, Lenovo, Honeywell, Aviva, Bank of China, Honda and Hyundai are among the head offices the City lists, and its 2018 economic profile described Markham as having the highest concentration of ICT employers per capita in Canada.

Between 2016 and 2021 Markham's population rose 2.9%, but its stock of private dwellings grew 8.3%, close to three times as fast. In practice that means more condos, towers and townhouses. Markham Centre, the new downtown between Highway 7 and the 407, is the clearest example, and a 2022 application proposed a dozen towers on CF Markville's Highway 7 frontage.

So a Markham business may be selling to a head office in a 404 business park, to a household in a new Highway 7 condo, or to both. Those buyers search differently, and one generic homepage rarely ranks for either.

The terrain

Why Markham Is Not A Smaller Toronto In Search

A plan copied from a Toronto client misses what is particular here.

More than one language of search

In the 2016 Census, 23% of Markham residents named Cantonese as their mother tongue and 13% Mandarin, with Tamil, Farsi and Urdu after them. In 2021, 34,405 residents, about one in ten, could not hold a conversation in English or French. Some of your customers search in a language your site lacks.

Steeles is a line on a map, not in the results

Markham's southern edge is Toronto. A shopper near Pacific Mall at Steeles and Kennedy is a few blocks from Scarborough, and Scarborough businesses show up in the same map pack as yours. The municipal boundary does nothing to separate the results.

Thornhill is two cities

Yonge Street splits Thornhill: the east side is Markham, the west side is Vaughan, and the postal address says neither. If your listing, your site and your citations name the place three different ways, Google has to guess which area you serve.

The city is getting denser

Dwellings grew nearly three times faster than population from 2016 to 2021. Each new tower in Markham Centre changes who is closest to whom, and map rankings shift with it.

B2B buyers do not use the map pack

A firm selling to IBM or AMD procurement teams is competing with vendors across North America, not with the shop down the street. Those searches reward technical depth, not reviews and hours.

Some searches are made for someone else

York Region reports that 43% of its seniors who speak neither English nor French live in Markham. Many of their searches are typed in English by a son or daughter, so a clinic or care provider's English page has to say plainly which languages the staff speak.

How we work

Markham SEO, Planned Market By Market

Our SEO services page covers the general method. In Markham the first question is different: which of the city's markets you actually sell into.

1. Decide which Markham you sell to

First we sort your revenue into local buyers and regional or national ones. A Unionville physiotherapy clinic and a Markham software reseller both "do SEO", but one needs the map pack in a few postal codes and the other needs to rank against vendors in Waterloo and Boston.

2. Map the languages your customers use

We look at who actually calls and books: which languages your staff already handle, and which your customers use in reviews and emails. If Cantonese, Mandarin or Tamil speakers are a real share of your business, we plan separate language pages at their own URLs, linked to each other. The copy needs a fluent writer, yours or a translator you trust. We build the structure and the markup around it.

3. Clean up the place names

Markham, Unionville, Thornhill, Cornell, Markham Village: your Google Business Profile, your site and your directory listings should agree on where you are and which areas you serve.

4. Build pages for real service areas

If most of your jobs come from Cornell, Markham Village and Stouffville, those places get pages written about them, not the same paragraph with the name swapped. For B2B firms, the pages are about industries and capabilities instead of streets.

5. Report each market on its own

Reporting from Search Console and Analytics is split the same way the plan is: local searches, B2B searches and each language section. A head-office buyer and a Cornell family are never averaged into one number.

Where you are

Markham SEO From Unionville To The Steeles Edge

Markham was stitched together from old villages, a planned new town and a new downtown, and customers still search by those names.

Markham Centre and the Highway 7 strip. The new downtown sits between Highway 7 and the 407, from Rodick Road to Kennedy. York University opened its Markham Campus here in fall 2024, a 10-storey building planned for 4,200 students in its first phase and up to 10,000 later. Students and condo residents search for food, fitness, tutoring and clinics close by, so hours, photos and exact location matter most.

Unionville. Main Street Unionville runs through a heritage conservation district whose plan won the Ontario Professional Planners Institute's Outstanding Planning Award in 1999. It is a destination street of restaurants and shops. Visitors search by the neighbourhood name, so a business here should use "Unionville" as well as "Markham".

Markham Village and Mount Joy. Main Street Markham is the old town centre, another heritage district, with Markham and Mount Joy GO stations on the Stouffville line nearby.

Cornell and east Markham. Cornell was planned as a new urbanist community, with building starting in 1997, grid streets and a retail corridor built into the neighbourhood. It sits in the northeast between the 407 and 16th Avenue, and Markham Stouffville Hospital on Church Street is next to the Cornell Community Centre. Healthcare, childcare and home services all compete for "near me" searches in this area.

Thornhill, east of Yonge. Markham's piece of Thornhill has its own heritage district and a strong local identity. Searches mix "Thornhill" and "Markham", and a business here needs both.

The Steeles edge. Pacific Mall at Steeles and Kennedy, often cited as one of the largest East Asian shopping malls in North America, has about 450 retail units. Retail and food businesses along this edge compete directly with Toronto businesses a few blocks south, in the same results.

The 404 and 407 business parks. Much of Markham's head-office and tech employment sits along these highways. Firms here need search for their buyers first, and for recruiting second, since the candidates they want tend to look a company up before they apply.

Industries

SEO Services Markham Industries Need, Sector By Sector

In the 2016 Census, Markham's largest industries by labour force were professional and technical services, retail, finance and insurance, manufacturing and health care. Each needs a different search plan.

Technology and B2B

With more than 1,000 tech and life sciences firms in the city, software, hardware and IT services companies are competing with their own neighbours as well as with the rest of the continent. They win on capability pages and content written for technical buyers. See our B2B technology work.

Finance, insurance and professional firms

Aviva, TD and Allstate were among the largest employers in the City's 2018 economic profile, and in the 2016 Census 10% of Markham's labour force worked in finance and insurance. Where a broker, accountant or law firm here serves clients in more than one language, pages that answer the same question in each of them help it make the shortlist.

Healthcare and senior services

Markham had 62,055 residents aged 65 and over in 2021, up 24% in five years. Clinics, pharmacies, home care and physiotherapy practices near Cornell and Markham Stouffville Hospital compete for those searches, often in Cantonese or Mandarin as well as English.

Restaurants and retail

From Pacific Mall to CF Markville at Highway 7 and McCowan to Main Street Unionville, food and retail searches are crowded and fast. Menus, photos, hours and reviews in the right language decide most of them.

Real estate and home services

Nearly 9,000 private dwellings were added between 2016 and 2021. New condo owners need movers, cleaners, renovators and property managers, and they search by building or neighbourhood. Pages for Markham Centre and the Highway 7 corridor, where new housing is concentrated, get in ahead of the demand.

Straight answers

Markham SEO Questions, Answered

About one in ten Markham residents can't hold a conversation in English or French. Do they need their own pages?

If they are your customers and your staff can serve them, yes. The 2021 Census counted 34,405 Markham residents who know neither English nor French, and in 2016 more than a third had Cantonese or Mandarin as their mother tongue. Those customers cannot use an English-only page, so separate pages in their language, at their own URLs and linked to your English pages, are the way to reach them.

How much does SEO cost for a Markham business?

It depends on scope: how many services, how many districts or towns, whether you need more than one language, and whether you are chasing local customers, B2B buyers or both. We scope it on the strategy call and give you a plain price, and you keep the written plan either way.

Can a Markham business rank when Toronto companies compete for the same searches?

Yes, but it depends on what you're aiming for. In the map pack, closeness to the searcher counts for a lot, so a Markham business has the advantage with Markham searchers. For broad terms, older Toronto sites have a head start, so we start with district, service and language searches, where competition is thinner.

We are in Thornhill. Should we say Markham or Thornhill?

Usually both. If you're east of Yonge, your municipality is Markham, but many customers search for "Thornhill". Keep your Google Business Profile address exactly as Canada Post has it, use Markham and Thornhill consistently in your service areas and on your site, and have a page that clearly talks about Thornhill. What hurts rankings is naming your location differently from one listing to the next.

How long does SEO take to work in Markham?

It depends which Markham market you are after. A Unionville or Main Street storefront can see map changes within weeks of a profile and citation clean-up. Pages for districts such as Cornell or Markham Centre take months. B2B terms where a Markham firm competes with vendors across North America take the longest. We don't promise rankings by a date.

Do you meet Markham clients in person?

We're Ontario-based, not in Markham, so strategy and reporting run remotely. When the work involves filming at your office, shop or job site, our crew travels into the GTA for the shoot.

Is B2B SEO different for Markham tech companies?

Very. A Markham software or hardware firm usually sells to buyers across Canada and the US, so the map pack barely matters. What matters is being found for the problems your product solves, the industries you serve and the systems you integrate with, and having pages detailed enough for an engineer or a procurement team to trust.
