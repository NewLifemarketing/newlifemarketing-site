Whiplash seasonality Powder weekends and long weekends carry the year; April and November test it. Marketing that ignores the calendar burns budget in the troughs and under-earns the peaks.

STRs bleeding platform fees Airbnb and VRBO take their cut of every stay, and your listing sits beside forty others. Direct bookings need standout media and a funnel of your own. Collingwood FAQ

Can you help a short-term rental get more direct bookings? That's one of the best-fit jobs we do. Cinematic property film, drone and photography make the listing itself outperform, and a booking funnel with retargeting brings past guests and lookers back to book direct — where you keep the platform fees. Our flagship vacation-rental production, Casa Bella y Dushi in Aruba, is exactly this system: film, 360°, 55+ listing photos, built to work across Airbnb, VRBO and paid channels.

We're slammed in winter and quiet in spring — how do ads fix that? They don't 'fix' seasonality; they monetize it properly. We build a seasonal calendar: capture maximum value in peak (higher rates, direct bookings, upsells), then run shoulder-season campaigns aimed at the audiences who do come in April and November — golfers, spa weekends, event groups, locals — instead of going dark and hoping.

Our customers are mostly from Toronto. Can you reach them from Collingwood? Yes — that's the point of geo-targeted paid media. We aim Meta and Google campaigns at the GTA feeder markets your guests actually come from, with creative filmed at your property or business that makes the two-hour drive feel worth it. You advertise where the wallets are, not just where you're located.

Do you work with Collingwood real estate agents? Real estate and hospitality is one of our dedicated verticals: cinematic listing media, agent personal-brand content, and listing funnels with ad distribution. In a market where buyers scroll dozens of listings from their condo in Toronto, the property with film-grade media wins the showing.
