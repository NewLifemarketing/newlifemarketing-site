Questions

Social Media In Brampton: Common Questions

What is the difference between social media management and advertising?

Management produces and publishes content and keeps the accounts credible. Advertising pays to put something in front of people who do not follow you. They solve different problems on different clocks: advertising can produce enquiries within days and stops when the budget stops, while management compounds slowly and is what makes every other channel convert better. The most common and most expensive mistake in this market is buying one to solve the other problem, which then gets judged a failure for doing exactly what it said it would.

How much should we spend on social media in Brampton?

It depends entirely which of the four jobs you are buying, which is why we name it before quoting. Paid advertising needs a media budget stated separately from any fee, and local reach here is genuinely inexpensive. Management is a monthly fee and should be sized to how much real material your business can supply, not to a number of posts. Content production is project work. Anybody quoting a single blended number for all of it is making it impossible for you to tell later whether it worked.

Which platform should a Brampton business be on?

Wherever your customers already are rather than wherever is newest. For most local consumer and trade businesses here that is still Facebook and Instagram, and for firms selling to other businesses it is LinkedIn. The useful question is not which platform performs best in general but which one the people who buy from you already use to ask each other for recommendations. That takes ten minutes to answer and saves a year of posting into the wrong place.

Our posts get no engagement. Is that the problem?

Not necessarily, and engagement is a weaker signal than it looks. What matters more is whether the profile survives being checked: current posts, real photographs of your own work, answered messages and visible reviews. A business can have very low engagement and still be converting well because people look it up before calling, and another can have high engagement from the wrong audience entirely. We would rather watch profile visits, direct messages and whether searches for your business name are rising.

Can you work with our existing agency or in-house person?

Often, and with the four services named it becomes much easier. If somebody is already handling management well, we are happy to take only the paid side, or only the strategy, and leave the rest alone. The pieces that most commonly go unexamined are the definition of what the work is for, the measurement, and the quarterly read on whether it reaches sales. Those can be added without replacing anybody, and they are usually what the arrangement was missing.
