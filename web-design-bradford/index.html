Bradford market

From Holland Street To The Black Soil

Bradford West Gwillimbury splits into distinct zones that behave like different towns wearing the same jersey. Downtown Holland Street is the independent spine — restaurants, services and shops serving a population that tripled around them. The new subdivision belts east and south of downtown hold the undecided households every campaign should court first.

Bond Head keeps its village identity and estate-home wallet on the west side; the 400 corridor's employment lands add a B2B and logistics audience that works in Bradford even when it doesn't live here; and the Marsh itself rings everything with agri-businesses whose story is stronger than their signal.

We draw the campaign map from those zones — and from the GO-platform reality that half the town's wallets ride to Toronto every morning and decide where to spend on the ride home. What the first 30 days look like: strategy call and written plan in week one, scope with Keegan, a shoot day at your storefront, site or fields, and campaigns live with verified tracking around the two-week mark.

Weekly optimization and a plain-dollars monthly report from there — with new-mover audiences refreshed as the streets fill in. Straight answers
