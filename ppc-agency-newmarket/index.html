PPC Agency Newmarket

Nine Separate Decisions

A ppc agency newmarket is managing an account whose single most consequential setting is geographic: York Region has nine municipalities, each costs something different to serve, and one unexamined radius decides all nine at once.

A radius drawn around the town reaches a fraction of the region. A radius drawn around the region reaches into the larger centres to the south, where click prices are set by much bigger budgets and the searcher has closer alternatives.

Nothing in the account announces that crossing. What you see is a cost per click drifting upward and a conversion rate drifting down, and the usual response is to adjust bids, which addresses none of it.

The fix is a list rather than a circle: the municipalities you genuinely serve, named, with travel beyond them priced as its own line. That is nine small decisions, each of which can be answered from your own invoices.

It costs nothing and takes an afternoon, and it is worth more than any optimisation applied afterwards.

How to decide the nine

Four Tests For Including A Municipality

Apply each to each place and the account configures itself.

Have you ever served there profitably?

Your own records answer this. A place that has produced three good jobs is a different proposition from one that has produced an enquiry you declined.

What does the travel cost?

Drive time against job value. A single price across a region quietly subsidises the furthest customers with the nearest ones, and the account makes that worse by bidding for both equally.

Who else is there?

The further from home, the more local alternatives the searcher has. Winning a click there is more expensive and converting it is harder.

Would you take the job tomorrow?

If the honest answer is probably not, exclude it. Paying for clicks you would decline is the purest form of waste in a regional account.

Four questions, nine places, one afternoon. The resulting list is also the answer to what the pages should say about where you go, which is the single most-read sentence on most local websites, and it is worth revisiting once a year rather than treating as permanent.

Before any budget

Three Numbers, One Afternoon

They make the fee conversation short and the reporting meaningful.

What a customer is worth

Over the whole relationship rather than the first job. Without it, cost per enquiry is a figure with nothing to compare it against and every report becomes a matter of opinion.

What share of enquiries convert

The bridge between the two, and the number most likely to be guessed. A guess here moves the defensible budget by a factor of two.

The maximum defensible cost per enquiry

Which follows arithmetically, and which can then be set per municipality rather than as one figure for the whole region.

That last refinement is the one worth insisting on here. A customer forty minutes away is worth less than an identical customer ten minutes away, and an account that bids the same for both is quietly losing money on the half it is proudest of.

What moves the cost

What A PPC Agency Newmarket Should Fix Before The Bid

None of them is the bid, and all four are usually neglected in a small account.

The page the click lands on comes first, and in a regional account it has one job above all others: say where you go. A reader two municipalities away is asking that before anything else, and a page that does not answer loses them.

The negative keyword list is second, and it is cumulative. An account two years old with no negatives has been paying for the same irrelevant phrases every month since launch.

Third, the geographic report, read quarterly against where paying customers actually were. It is the only evidence that settles whether the boundary is drawn correctly.

Fourth, retargeting. The audience has already visited and demonstrated interest, reaching them again costs a fraction of reaching a stranger, and in the small accounts we are shown it is the most common thing missing entirely.

All four share a property worth noticing: none needs a larger budget and all of them keep working at the budget you already have. That is the honest order of operations, and it is the opposite of the order most accounts get optimised in, where the bid is adjusted monthly and the page the clicks land on has not been touched since it was built.

Judging it

Cost Per Enquiry, By Place, Over A Quarter

One extra column turns a flat report into a decision.

Clicks are an input and conversion rate is a property of the page. Cost per enquiry is the first figure that means anything, and split by municipality it becomes something you can act on rather than something you can only read.

Read it over three months rather than one. A business taking a handful of enquiries a month cannot distinguish a good month from a bad one, and an account judged monthly gets cut and restarted on noise.

Keep asking every arriving customer how they heard of you and where they were. In a region this size that second half is the only record that survives, and it is what the quarterly boundary review is built on.

And set an end condition for any test before it starts: a number, a date, or an answer. Without one of those three a test quietly becomes a permanent line item.
