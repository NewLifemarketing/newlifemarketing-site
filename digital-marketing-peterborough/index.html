Digital Marketing Peterborough

Too Many Suppliers

Digital Marketing Peterborough is unusual in one measurable way: a city of 83,651 people generates 510 searches a month for somebody to do the work, which is a great deal of supplier demand per head and means your difficulty is not finding an agency but telling fifteen of them apart.

The shape of the market

Digital Marketing Peterborough: A Crowded Supplier Market

Fifteen phrases, 510 searches a month, in a city smaller than most people assume.

people in the City of Peterborough

128,624
people in the Peterborough census metropolitan area

510
monthly searches across the 15-phrase supplier cluster

Semrush, Canada database, October 2026

Search volume for a service is a reasonable proxy for how many people are shopping for it. Five hundred a month, in a city this size, says a large number of local businesses are actively looking for help and a correspondingly large number of suppliers are answering.

That is good news and a warning in the same number. The good news is that the work is normal here, so you are not explaining the concept to your own board. The warning is that a crowded supplier market selects for whoever sells best, not whoever works best.

The second number is the one most proposals ignore. The census metropolitan area holds 128,624 people against the city’s 83,651, so roughly a third of your likely customers live outside the city limits, in townships, on lakes, and down roads that take twenty-five minutes.

A plan written for a city of 83,651 and a plan written for a catchment of 128,624 are different plans, and only one of them matches where the money is.

Telling suppliers apart

Four Questions That Separate Fifteen Proposals

None of them is about price, and all four are answerable in a sentence by somebody who is actually going to do the work.

How far out does the targeting go, and why that far?

A specific radius with a reason behind it — drive time, service area, where last year’s jobs actually were — is a different answer from "Peterborough and surrounding area", which is what a template says.

What will you hand back if we stop?

The account, the domain, the analytics, the written record of what owns which search. A supplier whose answer is a login has kept the plan in their own head, which is a retention strategy rather than a service.

What are you not going to do?

A list of exclusions is the most reliable sign that somebody has thought about scope. A proposal covering everything covers nothing in particular and is usually the cheapest to produce.

Who does the work?

The person in the meeting is frequently not the person on the account. That is normal and it is only a problem when nobody says so, because the second person is the one your results depend on.

Ask all four of every shortlisted supplier and the field thins quickly. In a market with this much supply, the questions do more work than the comparison of fees.

What the money should do first

Order Matters More Than Budget Here

The same spend produces very different results depending on what it is pointed at first.

Start with what you already have. The Business Profile, the pages that already rank, the enquiries arriving now and where they come from. Almost every account we are shown has a free correction available in the first week, and spending before making it is paying to send people to something broken.

Then settle the catchment question, because it governs everything after it. Decide how far you will genuinely travel, price that honestly, and let the targeting match the answer rather than an aspiration.

Only then does paid media earn its place, and it earns it fastest as an instrument rather than a channel: a fortnight of spending settles which offer lands and which words the people who actually enquire use, and those answers direct the unpaid work that follows.

Reversing that order is the commonest expensive mistake, and in a supplier market this crowded it is also the easiest one to be sold.

The calendar nobody sets

What Digital Marketing Peterborough Has To Plan Around

A large part of this catchment runs on a season whose dates are published by the federal government.

Parks Canada opened the Trent-Severn Waterway locks on 15 May in 2026 and closed them on 12 October, and the navigation season runs from Victoria Day to Thanksgiving every year. For any business whose customers arrive by water, own property on it, or serve people who do, that is the commercial year stated in advance by somebody else.

It is an unusual advantage. Most markets have a season that has to be inferred from last year’s sales; here a large share of the catchment has one printed on a government page months ahead.

What that should change is when work happens rather than how much of it happens. Pages, photography and anything needing a sunny afternoon belong to the weeks before the season opens, not the week demand arrives.

And the quiet half of the year is not dead time. It is when the material gets made, the site gets rebuilt and the questions people asked all summer get answered in writing, so the next opening starts with something better than the last one did.

What to measure

Enquiries By Source, On A Rolling Quarter

Two habits, both cheap, and together they outperform any dashboard at this scale.

Ask how they heard of you

Every arriving customer, written down, every time. In a catchment this dispersed a large share of business arrives by routes no tracking parameter survives, and without the question the most recent piece of work gets the credit.

Report on a quarter, not a month

A business taking eight enquiries a month cannot tell a good month from a bad one. Judged monthly, good tactics get cut on noise and bad ones get extended on luck.

Add one line to both: where the customer actually was. Over a quarter that single column tells you whether the townships are paying for the fuel, and it is the only honest basis for deciding how far the targeting should reach.

Our scope

What We Do And What We Do Not

Narrow, said in advance, so a wasted meeting is avoided.

Paid media on Google and Meta run against cost per enquiry, search work built one cluster at a time so two of your own pages never compete for the same search, video and photography of real work, and the tracking underneath that connects an enquiry to what produced it. We manage over $70,000 a month in advertising spend for Ontario businesses and the owner runs the paid media personally.

What a digital marketing peterborough engagement should hand back

A digital marketing peterborough engagement with us leaves you holding the registry of which page owns which search, the profile audit with its corrections applied, and whatever was filmed. In a market with this many suppliers, the thing that survives a change of agency is the only thing worth paying for twice.

Not public relations, not events, not brand identity built from scratch. Those are real needs and naming them now is cheaper for both sides than discovering it in month three.

There are a lot of agencies here. How do we choose?

Ask four questions of each: how far does the targeting reach and why, what gets handed back if we stop, what are you not going to do, and who actually does the work. A supplier who answers all four in plain sentences has thought about your business. In a market producing 510 supplier searches a month, those questions thin the field faster than comparing fees.

Should we target the city or the whole area?

Whichever matches how far you will genuinely travel, which is a question about your costs rather than your ambitions. The census area holds 128,624 people against the city’s 83,651, so a third of the market is outside the city limits. Decide the drive time, price it, and set the targeting to match.

When should the work actually happen?

Earlier than most people schedule it. Parks Canada ran the 2026 navigation season from 15 May to 12 October, and for much of this catchment that is the commercial year. Pages, photography and anything needing good weather belong to the weeks before it opens; the quiet half of the year is when the material gets made.

How long before we can tell whether it is working?

A quarter, not a month. At the volumes a business this size sees, one month cannot distinguish a good month from a bad one, so an account judged monthly gets cut and restarted on noise. Count enquiries by source and by where the customer was, and read the trend across three months.
