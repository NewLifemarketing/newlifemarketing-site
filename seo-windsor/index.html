A border market

Proximity Does Not Stop At The River

Windsor is 229,660 people, the southernmost city in Ontario, with several million people immediately across the water, almost all of them in another country.

people in Windsor

1
river between this market and a city of four million

The Detroit River, roughly 1 km across

Local search is built on distance. A searcher standing in downtown Windsor is closer to a great many American businesses than to a Canadian one twenty minutes down the 401, and the engines know that. The signals that normally sort a local market cleanly do not sort this one cleanly at all.

The practical result is that national context matters in Windsor in a way it does not anywhere else in the province. Currency, spelling, phone format, whether the address is written the Canadian way, and whether the site ever states plainly that it serves customers in Canada. These sound trivial. They are the entire difference between being obviously the local Windsor answer and being one of forty results somebody has to disambiguate.

Most seo windsor work never addresses this because it is run from a template built for a market with no border in it.

What to make explicit

Say Which Country You Are In

Not as a slogan. As a set of small, boring, machine-readable facts that most Windsor sites leave ambiguous.

For a Windsor business the US customer question is the one worth deciding deliberately rather than by accident. Canada’s busiest commercial land crossing is at the end of the road, and for some businesses here American customers are a real and profitable market. For others they are an expensive distraction involving brokerage, duties and a currency you did not price in.

Either answer is fine and both should be visible on a Windsor site. A site that says nothing gets enquiries from both sides and disappoints half of them, which is the worst available outcome and the most common one.

Where to spend

Where Windsor SEO Budgets Should Go First

The tempting mistake

A keyword report for the Windsor region shows enormous volume a few kilometres away and it is difficult to ignore. Most of it belongs to a market you cannot service without a US entity, cross-border logistics and a price in another currency. Search engine optimization windsor work that chases it produces impressive impression counts and no work, and the reporting looks better than the business does.

What we do instead

Make the Canadian answer unmistakable on the Windsor site first, because it is cheap and it improves every other signal. Then decide the cross-border question deliberately and build for whichever answer is true. Only then the ordinary work, which here is the same as it is anywhere else.

The profile

Local Results Here Need More Help Than Elsewhere

When Windsor proximity is doing less of the sorting, everything else in the profile has to do more of it.

Name the municipalities

Essex County is several municipalities and people search with their own name. A seo in windsor engagement that never mentions Tecumseh, LaSalle or Amherstburg is leaving the easiest searches in the region on the table.

Reviews are comparative

For a Windsor profile the number that matters is not whether you have more than last year, it is whether you have more than the three results above you. In a market where distance sorts poorly, that comparison does more work than usual.

Category before content

On a Windsor profile a precisely chosen primary category moves local results more than a month of writing. It is a dropdown, it takes a minute, and it is wrong or vague on a surprising share of the profiles we are given access to.

Questions

Search In Windsor: Common Questions

Does being on the border actually change our SEO?

Yes, more in Windsor than anywhere else in the province. Local results are sorted substantially by distance, and here the nearest large population is in another country roughly a kilometre away. That makes proximity a weaker sorting signal than usual and everything else a stronger one, and it means small national cues — currency, country in the address, phone format, spelling — carry weight they would not carry in a market with no border in it.

Should we target customers in the United States?

Decide deliberately, and then say so. For some Windsor businesses American customers are a real and profitable market with Canada’s busiest commercial crossing at the end of the road. For others they mean brokerage, duties and a currency you did not price in. Both answers are fine. Saying nothing is the bad option, because it produces enquiries from both sides and disappoints half of them.

Why not chase the search volume across the river?

Because most of it is not servable from Windsor. A keyword report for this region shows large volume a few kilometres away, and pursuing it needs a US entity, cross-border logistics and a price in another currency. Without those, the work produces impressive impression counts and no actual jobs, and the monthly report ends up looking considerably better than the business does.

What is the single fastest improvement here?

The Windsor Business Profile category, and it takes a minute. It is the strongest single signal in local results, it is a dropdown rather than a project, and it is vague or wrong on a surprising share of the profiles we are given access to. After that, naming the municipalities you actually serve — Tecumseh, LaSalle, Amherstburg, Lakeshore — rather than drawing a radius nobody chose.

How many reviews do we need?

More than the three Windsor results above you, which is a different question from more than last year. Review count and recency carry extra weight in a market where distance does less of the sorting, so the useful target is comparative rather than absolute. Look at who currently appears above you for the searches that matter and work from that number rather than from a general ambition.
