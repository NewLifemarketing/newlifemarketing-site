Google Ads Agency Cambridge

Eighty Searches A Month

Any honest google ads agency cambridge conversation starts with the size of the thing: four phrases, eighty searches a month between them. That is not a volume channel, and treating it as one is how a small budget disappears without teaching anybody anything.

The size of it

The number is small and the conclusion people draw from it is usually the wrong one.

The wrong conclusion is that this city does not advertise. It does; it simply does not go looking for somebody to run the advertising, which is a statement about supplier demand rather than about budgets.

The right conclusion is about what a google ads agency cambridge account can actually do here. With a market this thin, advertising is not the way to reach everybody. It is the fastest way to answer a question, and that is a narrower and far more defensible purchase.

Does this offer land. Does this page convert. Is this product worth building a page for. Each of those can be settled inside a fortnight for money, and each answer then directs months of unpaid work that would otherwise have been guesswork.

A manufacturing town

Industrial Keywords Behave Nothing Like Retail Ones

Toyota has manufactured here since 1988 and a large supplier base grew around it. Those businesses meet a different auction.

A supplier’s terms carry ten or twenty searches a month, cost very little per click because almost nobody bids on them, and convert at rates no consumer term approaches, because somebody typing the precise name of a process is specifying it now.

The trap is that an account built on those terms spends almost nothing, which reads in a monthly report as a campaign that is not working. It is working; there is simply not more of it to buy.

The response that destroys the account is widening the match type to spend the budget. That converts a precise, cheap, high-converting campaign into a broad, expensive, irrelevant one, and it happens most months somewhere because a budget that is not spent looks like a failure to somebody.

The better response is to accept the ceiling, let the campaign spend what it spends, and put the remainder into the pages that capture the same demand for free.

What the money is for

Four Questions Worth Buying An Answer To

Each is answerable in a fortnight and each saves a quarter of guessing.

Does anybody respond to this offer?

The quickest, cheapest test available. A small spend against a single page tells you whether the proposition lands before a page is written for every service.

Which words do our customers actually use?

The search terms report is the only honest source for this. A month of modest spending produces a vocabulary list worth more than any workshop.

Is this service worth a page?

Rather than deciding by intuition which of eleven services deserves a page, buy a week of clicks for each of the top three and let the conversion rate decide.

Is the page the problem?

If ads produce clicks and no enquiries, the page is the issue and nothing in the account will fix it. Knowing that costs a fortnight and saves months of bid adjustments.

Account hygiene

What A Google Ads Agency Cambridge Account Should Have Set Correctly

In a thin market these four decisions do most of the work. Bids are much further down the list than anybody expects.

Match type, tight

In an eighty-search cluster, broad match finds adjacency rather than volume. The search-terms report shows within a month how much of the budget went somewhere unrelated, and in a small account that can be most of it.

Geography drawn for a three-centre city

The City of Cambridge records that the municipality was formed in 1973 from Galt, Preston and Hespeler. A radius from the geographic middle covers ground nobody thinks of as theirs. Draw where you actually go.

One page per campaign

A specific search sent to a general page converts badly at any click price, and it is the single highest-return fix available in a small account.

A schedule that matches the buyer

A trade serving households is searched for in the evening; a supplier selling to industry on a weekday morning. One schedule for both overpays in whichever half does not convert.

A longer decision

What A Google Ads Agency Cambridge Has To Account For In A Slow Sale

Where a purchase takes months, the usual reporting window measures the wrong thing.

Somebody searches, downloads something, speaks to two suppliers, gets internal approval and orders in the spring. The click that started it was in November and will be credited to nothing.

So the conversion to count is the enquiry rather than the order, and the figure that matters is how many enquiries became work — which only the business can supply, from its own records, months later.

That means the honest review period is a quarter at minimum and a year for anything with a long cycle. Monthly judgement on a six-month sale is judgement on an incomplete picture every single month.

It also changes what the advertisement should offer. Asking for an order from somebody four months away fails; asking for the thing they actually want now — a specification, a price range, a sample — does not.

Those early offers are also what make a long cycle measurable at all. A download or a sample request happens within days of the click, can be counted, and is a reasonable proxy for an order months later once you have seen enough of them to know what share converts.

Our scope

What We Run Here

Narrow, and shaped to a market that does not need much advertising.

We build and run Google and Meta accounts against cost per enquiry, write the pages the ads point at, and set up the tracking that connects an enquiry to what produced it. We manage over $70,000 a month in advertising spend for Ontario businesses and the owner runs the paid media personally rather than passing it to a desk.

What we would usually recommend to a google ads agency cambridge enquiry

A smaller account than you expected, running for a fixed period to answer specific questions, alongside a finite programme of search work. That is a less profitable recommendation than a permanent retainer and in a market this thin it is the correct one.

We are based in Minesing rather than Cambridge. For an advertising account that changes nothing; for filming a plant it means travel, which we price rather than hide inside a day rate.

What should we budget?

Enough to produce a few hundred clicks within a month, because below that the account cannot be optimised and managing it is guesswork. In a cluster this small that is a modest figure. The firmer constraint is your maximum defensible cost per enquiry, which comes from what a customer is worth rather than from any benchmark.

Our budget never gets spent. Should we widen the targeting?

Almost never. An underspending account in a thin market is usually a correctly targeted one that has run out of relevant searches to buy. Widening converts it into a broad campaign reaching people who were not looking for you, which spends the budget and produces nothing. Put the remainder into pages instead.

Should we advertise or do search work?

Both, in that order, for different reasons. Advertising answers within a fortnight whether the offer and the page work, for money. Search work makes the same traffic stop costing per click, over months. At the low difficulty in this market the second is unusually cheap, which argues for a short paid test and then a finite build.

Can you take over an existing account?

Yes, and read access with an assessment beats rebuilding, because an account with history carries learning that is expensive to recreate. The common finding here is not poor construction but a match type or a geography set once and never revisited, plus a budget that somebody widened to make the spend look busy.
