From Referral-Only To A Measurable Lead Engine

DMT Building Group is a residential and commercial construction business serving homeowners and businesses looking for construction and renovation work.

Services Used:

  • Google Ads
  • Meta Lead Gen
  • Daily SEO Content
  • CRM Buildout (GoHighLevel)
  • Creative
← All case studies

Published July 21, 2026 · By Cruz Lee, Founder & CEO

Google Ads/Meta Ads/Construction

This breakdown was published July 21, 2026 and its numbers are frozen as reported then. The partnership is ongoing, current scope is on the DMT Building Group client page.

The Issue

Before NewLife, DMT ran on referrals, repeat customers and organic word-of-mouth: great business, zero predictability.

Construction leads are expensive, and slow follow-up quietly kills most of them. The first contractor to answer usually wins the job.

The Goals

Lead flow beyond referrals

A predictable source of work, not just referrals and word-of-mouth.

Leads they can measure

Lead flow that can be counted and tracked, channel by channel.

Answer every inquiry in minutes

A system that responds fast, so slow follow-up stops killing leads.

The Approach

A measurable, multi-channel lead system, with every inquiry landing in a CRM pipeline that answers in minutes.

  1. Google Ads first. Launched to capture homeowners and businesses actively searching for construction and renovation work: 357 clicks and 9,089 impressions at a 3.93% CTR on $1,698.68 of spend.
  2. Meta lead generation. A Meta lead generation campaign then delivered 15 qualified leads at $42.87 each on $642.98.
  3. A CRM built around the job. The GoHighLevel CRM we built has pipelines for every job stage.
  4. Instant response. Instant SMS/email responses via Twilio, and missed-call text-back so no inquiry dies in voicemail.
  5. Tracking, next. Conversion tracking on the Google account still needs wiring, so those search leads aren’t yet attributed in-platform: a fix on our list.

The Results

Within a few months, DMT went from relying on referrals to operating a measurable, multi-channel lead system: 15 qualified Meta leads at $42.87 each, construction-intent search traffic on top, every inquiry landing in a CRM pipeline that answers in minutes. Next step: wiring Google conversion tracking so search leads are attributed too.
