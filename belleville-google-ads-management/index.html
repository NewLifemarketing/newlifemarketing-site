What the word says

Belleville Google Ads Management: The Word Is Management

A search phrase is cheap market research and this one is unusually legible.

$2.38
cost per click in the local web cluster

Semrush, Canada database, October 2026

"Management" and "services" are the words in this small cluster. Nobody here is searching for strategy, a growth plan or a marketing partner. They are searching for somebody to run a thing that already exists.

That matters because a proposal answering a different question loses. A business that has an account, a history and a monthly spend does not want to be told to start again; they want to know what you would change in the first fortnight and what it would do to cost per enquiry.

Where the honest finding is that the account is competently built and the problem is the landing page or the offer, say so. It costs the fee and it is the thing that earns the relationship.

What an audit finds

Four Things Almost Every Inherited Belleville Google Ads Management Account Has

Not faults in the sense of mistakes. Four settings that drifted because nobody was reading them.

Searches nobody would have chosen

Months of paid clicks for phrases adjacent to the business and useless to it, sitting in a report nobody opens. In an unattended account this is frequently the largest single line of waste.

Placements outside the search results

An opt-in made at setup that scatters advertisements across unrelated sites and applications. Cheap clicks, a flattering click count, almost no enquiries.

Hours nobody is there

An even spread across the week, including evenings and Sundays, for a business that answers the telephone between eight and five.

A conversion that is not one

A page view or a button press counted as success, which means every historical figure in the account describes something other than an enquiry.

All four are visible within an hour and none of them requires changing a bid. Fixing them is usually worth more than anything a new strategy would contribute in the first quarter.

The call you will get

Somebody From The Platform Will Phone You

It is worth knowing what that call is, because it shapes a great many small accounts.

Advertising platforms employ representatives whose job includes making suggestions about your account. The suggestions are frequently sensible and they are made by somebody whose employer is paid when you spend more. That is not corruption; it is an interest that is not identical to yours.

The recommendations follow a pattern worth noticing: broaden the match types, raise the budget, enable automated placements, switch to a bid strategy that spends the full budget. Each has a legitimate case. Each also increases spend.

The defence is not suspicion but a written objective: cost per enquiry, with a ceiling, agreed before any recommendation is evaluated. With that on the table every suggestion becomes a testable proposition rather than an authority argument.

A good supplier does the same with its own recommendations, and should hand you the ceiling to hold them to.

Budget shape

What Belleville Google Ads Management Should Do With A Small Budget

In a cheap auction the usual levers behave differently, and two of them barely matter.

At the prices in this market a modest budget buys real volume, which changes the job. Bidding is not the constraint; relevance is. A tightly matched campaign pointed at a page written for the search will outperform a larger budget spread across broad terms, and it will do so inside a month.

The second difference is the ceiling. A correctly targeted account in a market this size will not spend a large budget, because there are not enough relevant searches to buy. That reads in a monthly report as a campaign that is not working, and somebody reasonably proposes widening the targeting to use the money.

Widening converts a precise, cheap, high-converting campaign into a broad, expensive, irrelevant one. The budget gets spent, the clicks arrive, the enquiries do not. It happens most months somewhere, because an unspent budget looks like a failure to whoever reads the report.

The setting to check first

Switch Off The Half You Did Not Ask For

New accounts opt in to placements most advertisers never intended to buy.

Alongside search results, a campaign can place advertisements across a vast network of other websites and applications. The clicks are cheap, plentiful, and overwhelmingly worthless for a local service business.

In an account set up quickly, that is switched on by default, and it routinely consumes most of the budget while producing a flattering click count and no enquiries.

Check it before anything else in an inherited account. The symptom is a cost per click far below what the search auction would charge and a conversion rate near zero.

There are legitimate uses for the network, almost all of them for showing advertisements again to people who already visited. As a way of finding new customers in one town it is the fastest way to spend a budget on nothing.

Straight answers

Google Ads Management FAQ

Straight answers before you ever book a call.

Will you rebuild our account?

Usually not. An account with history carries learning that is expensive to recreate, and the common finding is not poor construction but a geography, a schedule or a match type set once and never revisited. We would rather start with read access and a list of findings than with a migration, and if the honest answer is that the account is fine and the problem is the page, we will say so.

Our budget never gets spent. Should we widen the targeting?

Almost never. An underspending account in a market this size is usually a correctly targeted one that has run out of relevant searches. Widening converts it into a broad campaign reaching people who were not looking for you: the budget gets spent and the enquiries do not arrive. Put the remainder into pages instead.

Should we accept the platform representative’s advice?

Evaluate it against a written objective rather than accepting or refusing on principle. Many suggestions are sensible. They are also made by somebody whose employer is paid when you spend more, so anything that increases spend needs the same test as any other proposal: what will it do to cost per enquiry, and how will we know within a month.

What is a sensible management fee at this budget?

At small spends a percentage of spend produces a fee too low to service the account properly, which is how small accounts end up neglected. A flat fee against a defined scope is usually the more honest arrangement, and the scope question that matters most is whether the landing pages are inside it or outside it.
