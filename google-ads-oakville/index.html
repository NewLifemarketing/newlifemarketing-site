Oakville Does Not Have Enough Searches To Prospect With

Seventy Oakville searches a month on the head term, at a difficulty of two. Both numbers are small, and the first one is the problem.

Paid search is a capture channel. It finds people already looking and puts you in front of them, which works beautifully when there are a lot of them. In Oakville, for most service categories, there are not. Seventy Oakville searches a month divided across a handful of advertisers is a trickle rather than a pipeline, in a town of over two hundred thousand people in Ontario.

Difficulty of two makes that worse rather than better, because it means almost nobody is competing and the clicks are cheap. Cheap clicks on a small volume produce a small number of cheap clicks. A google ads oakville campaign built on the head term will be efficient and immaterial at the same time.

Meanwhile something much larger is happening and nobody is spending on it. People arrive at your website from search, from a referral, from a map result, look around and leave without contacting you. In most Oakville businesses that number dwarfs the monthly search volume, and the advertising budget is pointed away from it.

The better target

Everybody Who Already Left Your Website

They have demonstrated interest, they know who you are, and reaching an Oakville visitor again costs a fraction of finding somebody new.

The size difference is the point. A modest Oakville website might see several hundred visitors a month, the overwhelming majority of whom leave without contacting anybody. That is an audience several times larger than the entire paid search market for the category, and it is made up of people who have already shown they were interested enough to look.

In a market with a high customer value, which Oakville generally is, that changes the economics completely. Google ads agency oakville proposals almost always lead with search campaigns because that is the recognisable product, and in a city this size the recognisable product is the smaller half of the opportunity.

What Google Ads Management Oakville Budgets Should Buy

What usually goes wrong in Oakville

A search campaign set up competently against seventy monthly searches, reporting an excellent cost per click and producing a handful of enquiries. Nothing is wrong with it and nothing can be, because the ceiling is the search volume rather than the execution. Oakville google ads accounts of this shape plateau in month two and stay there.

What we do instead

Build the remarketing audience first, because it is larger than the search market and nobody is competing for it. Run search alongside on the genuinely high-intent terms, accept that it is small, and judge the account on total enquiries rather than on cost per click.

Doing it well

Remarketing That Is Not Just Following People Around

Badly done it irritates. Four decisions separate an Oakville campaign that works from one that annoys people into remembering you unkindly.

Exclude your customers

An audience of past visitors includes everybody who already became a client, and continuing to advertise at them is both wasteful and slightly odd. A google ads expert will set this exclusion in the first hour and plenty of accounts have never had it.

Give them a reason

A reminder that you exist is weak. Something specific they did not see the first time, or an answer to the question that probably stopped them, is considerably stronger and costs nothing more to run.

Cap the frequency

The same advertisement twelve times reads as desperation and makes the brand worse. Google ad words marketing oakville campaigns without a cap are the reason people describe remarketing as creepy.

Questions

Google Ads In Oakville: Common Questions

Is Google Ads worth running in Oakville at all?

Yes, and not in the shape most proposals suggest. At seventy searches a month the head term will produce a steady trickle of good enquiries and will never be a growth channel, because the ceiling is the search volume rather than the budget or the execution. What makes paid worth running here is remarketing: the people already visiting your site each month are usually several times the entire search market, and almost nobody is bidding for them.

Is remarketing not annoying?

Badly configured, very. Four settings separate useful from irritating: a deliberate frequency cap, a window matching how long your sale actually takes, an exclusion list removing people who already enquired or bought, and something specific to say rather than a logo. The first three take minutes and are missing from most accounts, which is why remarketing has the reputation it does.

How much should we spend?

Start from the size of the audience rather than from a monthly figure. If several hundred people visit your site and leave each month, reaching them repeatedly costs very little, and the budget should be whatever covers that audience properly rather than a number chosen in advance. The search side is capped by volume, so there is a natural ceiling on it that no amount of extra budget will raise.

What about Google Shopping?

Different market, different rules, and genuinely worth it if you sell products. A google shopping ads agency oakville retailers use is working with short purchase cycles where the platform can see the whole journey and the attribution is broadly honest. Everything on this page about low search volume applies to service businesses; a retailer with a product feed is in a different situation and should be evaluated separately.

Our cost per click is excellent. Is that good?

At difficulty two it is close to meaningless. Almost nobody is competing, so clicks are cheap and a low cost per click reflects the market rather than the management. The number worth watching is total qualified enquiries and what each one cost, because an account can report an outstanding cost per click while producing four enquiries a month and plateauing there permanently.
