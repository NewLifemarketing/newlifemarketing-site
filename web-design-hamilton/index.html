The part that gets skipped

Built So It Can Be Found, Not Just Admired

A new site frequently ranks worse than the one it replaced. That is avoidable and it is nearly always the same three causes.

Every old URL is mapped

Before design starts. Whatever ranks today keeps its address or gets a redirect, so the history survives the rebuild. This is the single most common thing a hamilton website design project gets wrong, and it is as true of the larger web design companies hamilton firms approach as of a freelancer.

Copy comes before layout

Pages are written to the length the subject needs, then designed around that. Cutting 1,400 words to 300 because the template looks better empty is how a rebuild loses rankings it took years to earn.

Speed is a build decision

Image sizes, script count and font loading are set during the build, not tuned afterwards. On a phone over mobile data, this is the difference between a quote request and a back button.

The practical effect is on the page map rather than the design. Web Design Hamilton: Common Questions

What does web design in Hamilton cost?

It depends on how many pages genuinely need writing, which is the real driver rather than the design itself. What we can say plainly is where the money should go first: speed, the booking or quote path, and the handful of pages that actually get found. Those three are inexpensive and move revenue. A full rebuild is worth doing once they are working, not instead of them. When you compare quotes, note that a web design company hamilton owners recommend and the larger hamilton web design companies often price the same scope very differently, because one is quoting pages and the other is quoting outcomes.

Will a new site hurt our Google rankings?

It can, and it usually does when three things happen: URLs change without redirects, copy is cut for visual reasons, and the new build is slower on mobile. All three are decisions made during the build. We map every existing URL and what it ranks for before design starts, so whatever is working keeps its address or gets a redirect.

Can we update it ourselves afterwards?

That is the point. With 56.9% of Hamilton businesses employing fewer than four people, the person updating the site is also running the business, so editing is in plain labelled fields and nothing important is trapped inside a layout. Everything is version-controlled too, which means a mistake is a revert rather than a support call.

Do you work with Hamilton website designers we already use?

Often, yes. If you have hamilton website designers who handle the visual side well, we will take the structure, the migration and the measurement and leave the design where it is. That applies equally to in-house website designers hamilton firms employ and to an outside website design company hamilton businesses already trust. The parts that most commonly go wrong are the redirect map and the content length, and both can be handled without replacing anyone.

How long does a build take?

Four to six weeks for a typical small-business site, with the first two spent on inventory and structure rather than visuals.
