Who reads it here

Your Site Is Being Assessed, Not Just Browsed

That changes what the site has to do. In most cities a website persuades a buyer. In Ottawa it frequently has to satisfy somebody checking whether you are a credible supplier before a conversation happens at all, and that person is looking for different things: clear corporate details, real capability statements, accessibility that holds up, and often French.

It is the single most common gap we find in ottawa web design work done elsewhere. The site looks current and converts a retail visitor fine, and it falls over the moment a program manager tries to navigate it with a keyboard or asks where the French is. Neither of those is expensive to get right during a build. Both are expensive to retrofit.

The two requirements

Accessibility And French Are Not Optional Here

Ontario businesses of a certain size already have accessibility obligations. In Ottawa the commercial consequence arrives sooner, because your buyers check.

Keyboard and screen reader

Every control reachable without a mouse, every image described, visible focus states, and contrast that passes. A website design company ottawa buyers trust should be testing this before handover, not after a complaint.

French, properly or not at all

A half-translated site reads worse than an English one. Either commit to both languages with real translation and working language switching, or be clearly an English-language supplier. Machine-translated French is noticed immediately here.

Documents too, not just pages

Capability statements and spec sheets are usually PDFs, and usually the least accessible thing on the site. Tagged, reflowable PDFs matter in a city where procurement reads them.

Web design in ottawa that treats these as a compliance checkbox misses why they pay. An accessible site is faster, better structured, and easier for search engines to read, so the same work that satisfies a reviewer also improves rankings. Website design ottawa ontario projects that skip it end up paying twice.

Where the budget goes

What To Spend On, In What Order

We work left to right. A web designer ottawa firms keep tends to be one who fixed the capability page and the keyboard navigation in week two, rather than the ones who delivered the showpiece in month four. Both can be the same team; the order is what differs, and in a year when 40,600 jobs left the city the order matters more than usual.

The review

What Somebody Actually Checks Before Shortlisting You

Suppliers get looked up before they get called. These are the things that decide whether the call happens.

Name the sectors you have served

Vague capability language reads as inexperience. A web design company ottawa suppliers hire and any web design agency ottawa suppliers shortlist should be pushing you to say defence, health, municipal or whatever it actually is, because that is the word being searched for.

Put people on the page

Named staff with real titles. Web designers in ottawa rarely suggest this and it matters: in a city this small professionally, a reviewer often recognises somebody, and anonymity reads as a reseller rather than a firm.

Keep the dates current

An old copyright year, a news page ending in 2022, a team page with people who left. Each is small, and together they answer whether you are still operating. An ottawa website design company worth its fee checks these before handover.

How it goes

What The Build Actually Looks Like

Five stages, with something real to see at the end of each.

01

Before anything

English, bilingual, or honest about it

This is decided first because it changes everything downstream: the page count roughly doubles, the content budget doubles, and the navigation needs a language switch that actually holds its place. Deciding it in week four instead of week zero is the most expensive mistake available in web design in ottawa, and the reason ottawa websites design quotes vary so wildly.

02

Week one

What you have actually delivered

Sectors, scale, outcomes, named references where permitted. This is the material a reviewer looks for and the material most suppliers have never written down. It takes a morning of interviews and it is usually the single highest-return hour of the project, and the part a website design agency ottawa buyers hire most often leaves to the client.

03

Week one

Set as constraints, not audited later

Contrast ratios, focus states, keyboard paths and how documents get published, written down as rules the build follows. Web designers ottawa teams bring in late always find this costly, because by then it is rework rather than a decision.

04

Weeks two to five

In both languages at once if bilingual

Not English first with French bolted on afterwards, which is how half-translated sites happen. Performance budgets enforced throughout, everything version-controlled so any change reverses cleanly.

05

Before launch

We check it the way a buyer would

Keyboard only, screen reader on, documents opened, every date checked, French read by somebody who speaks it. An ottawa web design company that skips this hands you a site that passes its own tests and fails the one that matters.

Questions

Ottawa Web Design: Common Questions

Do we need a French version of the site?

It depends who buys from you. If your customers include federal departments or the general public in this region, then yes, and it should be real translation with proper language switching rather than a machine-translated mirror, which is noticed immediately here. If you sell to a specialised technical market that works in English, an English site is honest and fine. What does not work is a half-translated site, which reads as unfinished to exactly the people you were trying to impress.

How much does web design in Ottawa cost?

The real driver is how many pages genuinely need writing, and whether French is in scope, rather than the design itself. Where the money should go first is consistent: accessibility, speed and the capability pages buyers actually read. Those are inexpensive and they move decisions. A full bilingual rebuild is worth doing once those work. When you compare quotes, note that an ottawa web design agency and a freelance ottawa web designer often price the same scope very differently because one is quoting pages and the other outcomes.

Will a rebuild hurt our search rankings?

It can, and usually does when addresses change without redirects, copy is cut for visual reasons, or the new build is slower on a phone. All three are build decisions. We map every existing address and what it ranks for before design starts, so whatever is working keeps its address or gets a redirect. That one step prevents most of the damage we get called in to repair.

Can you work with our existing designers?

Often, yes. If you have ottawa web designers handling the visual side well, we will take the structure, the accessibility baseline, the migration and the measurement and leave the design alone. The parts that most commonly go wrong are the redirect map, the content length and keyboard navigation, and all three can be fixed without replacing anybody. That applies equally to in-house website designers ottawa employers already have and to an outside website design company in ottawa you are happy with.

How long does it take?

Four to six weeks for a typical supplier site in English, longer if French is in scope. The first two weeks go on inventory and structure rather than visuals, which feels slow and is the reason the back half does not go wrong. Web design ottawa ontario projects that compress that front half are the ones where the migration costs rankings, and a website designer ottawa businesses rehire is almost always one who insisted on it.
