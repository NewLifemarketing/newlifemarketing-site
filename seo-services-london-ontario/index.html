Four Products, One Heading

SEO Services London Ontario covers at least four different products — an audit, technical work, local search, and whatever the current wave of AI tooling is selling — and because they are all quoted under one heading, two proposals for the same money are frequently not describing the same work at all.

A difficulty of 20 in a city this size means the results are contested but not locked: real businesses are working on them, and ordinary work done consistently still moves.

What makes the market confusing is not the competition. It is that the same heading covers an audit (a report), technical work (changes to a site), local search (a profile and reviews) and AI tooling (a method, not an outcome), and a buyer comparing two monthly fees may be comparing a report against a rebuild.

So the useful first step is not choosing a supplier. It is deciding which of the four you actually need, which is usually obvious once the four are named separately.

The sections below take them in the order they are worth buying, which is rarely the order they get sold.

The first product

What An SEO Audit London Ontario Buyers Pay For Should Contain

Ninety searches a month name an audit specifically, and most audits are worth less than they cost.

An SEO audit should be a short document naming what is wrong, in priority order, with an estimate of what fixing each item is worth. It should be readable in twenty minutes by somebody who does not do this for a living.

What usually arrives instead is a forty-page export from a crawling tool, listing every missing attribute on every page with no indication of which three things matter. That document is produced in minutes and cannot be acted on, which is why so many audits are bought, filed and never mentioned again.

The test before buying one: ask what the deliverable looks like and whether it will tell you what to do first. A supplier who says “a prioritised list of the five things worth fixing, and what each is worth” is selling something useful.

And ask what happens afterwards. An audit that only makes sense if the same supplier implements it is a sales document, and in a city with this many suppliers you should be free to take it anywhere.

The second product

When Technical SEO London Ontario Work Is Worth Buying

Ninety searches a month name technical work, and for most businesses it is the third priority rather than the first.

Technical SEO means making a site readable and fast: how pages are generated, how quickly they load, whether anything is blocking them from being indexed, whether two of your own pages are competing for the same search.

That last one is the item that genuinely matters for most businesses and the one least often included. Where two pages chase the same phrase, a search engine picks one, usually not the one you would have chosen, and the other contributes nothing but the dilution. Finding it takes ten minutes and fixing it takes a day.

The rest — crawl budgets, structured data beyond the basics, elaborate internal linking schemes — matters at a scale most local businesses never reach. On a thirty-page site it is work that can be done perfectly and change nothing.

So the honest version of technical SEO here is short: make sure nothing is blocking the site, make sure it loads quickly on a phone, and make sure your own pages are not competing. Then stop and spend the money on the pages themselves.

The third product

What AI SEO London Ontario Suppliers Are Actually Selling

Seventy searches a month name it, which makes it worth addressing plainly rather than avoiding.

AI SEO is a method rather than an outcome. It usually means pages drafted by a language model, keyword research assembled by one, or both. Neither is disqualifying and neither is a strategy.

What it changes is cost and volume, which cuts in two directions. Producing forty pages is now cheap, and forty pages that say nothing specific about your business are forty pages that compete with each other and convince nobody. The constraint was never typing.

Where it genuinely helps is the dull middle: drafting from an interview with somebody who knows the work, turning a recorded answer into a readable page, assembling research that a person then judges. In each case a human supplies the substance and the judgement.

The question to ask a supplier is therefore not whether they use it. It is where the substance comes from — and if the answer does not include an hour of somebody in your business, the pages will read like everybody else’s.

The fourth product

Where SEO Services London Ontario Should Usually Start

The profile and the reviews decide a large share of a local result and both are free.

The Business Profile

Categories, service area, hours, photographs and the services listed. Usually set once and never revisited, and it decides more of the local result than any page does.

Reviews, as a process

A specific moment in the job when somebody asks, and a short link that makes it a thirty-second task. Intention collects nothing; a habit collects steadily.

Proximity, which cannot be bought

In a city of 422,000 one address does not rank across every neighbourhood. Pages and advertising cover what the map will not.

For a business whose customers chose it partly because it was nearby, this is where the first month belongs. For a business whose buyers are elsewhere, it is housekeeping that still has to be correct.

Judging it

How To Judge SEO Services London Ontario Honestly

In a twenty-one-phrase cluster, a position report flatters and explains nothing.

Rankings move for reasons that have nothing to do with you, and the result shown to somebody in one part of the city is not the result in the report. Neither tells you whether anybody worth having arrived.

Count enquiries by landing page instead. Across twenty-one phrasings it is the only way to find the handful that matter to your business, as opposed to the ones that produce traffic.

Add the question at the counter: how did you hear of us. It is the only line in the report that comes from your business rather than from a platform, and it catches the arrivals no tracking parameter survives.

Read both over a rolling quarter. At these volumes one month cannot distinguish a good month from a bad one, which is how useful work gets cut and lucky months get extended.
