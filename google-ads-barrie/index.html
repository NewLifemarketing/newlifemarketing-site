Google Ads Management Barrie

Google Ads in Barrie

You are bidding in the same auction as firms with ten times your budget. That is survivable, but only if you stop bidding on what they bid on.

Book a strategy callHow we run Google Ads

The market

Barrie Pays GTA Prices For Barrie Clicks

Barrie sits close enough to Toronto that GTA advertisers spill into the market routinely. A firm buying keywords for a client in Vaughan will happily take Barrie clicks on the way past, and they are not price-sensitive about it.

The result is an auction priced above what the town's size would suggest. Local businesses look at the cost per click, conclude Google Ads does not work for them, and stop — usually without ever having tested a structure that could have worked.

The second thing that kills accounts here is the follow-up. Barrie owners tell us constantly that leads go cold because nobody got back to them quickly enough. In paid search that is not a minor inefficiency. You are paying for every one of those clicks whether you call the person back or not.

What we would target

Narrowing Instead Of Broadening

So the work is narrowing rather than broadening. Tight match types, aggressive negative keyword lists, and campaigns built around the specific jobs you want rather than the category you are in.

Geography does a lot of the lifting too. Bidding on the whole of Simcoe County at the same rate as central Barrie wastes money on clicks you cannot service profitably, and the fix is simply being honest about where the work is worth having.

How the account is structured and managed month to month is set out on our Google Ads Management page.

The work

What Google Ads Management Actually Involves In Barrie

The account gets built around the searches that signal a buying decision rather than the ones with the biggest volume. That means keyword research with intent attached, not just a volume figure — "emergency plumber Barrie" and "how to fix a tap" are not the same customer and should not be in the same campaign.

Negative keywords are where most of the saving happens, and most inherited accounts have barely any. Every month we read the actual search terms your ads showed for and cut the ones that were never going to buy. In an auction priced by the GTA, that is the single most valuable recurring job.

Then the landing page. A click that arrives on your homepage converts far worse than one that arrives on a page about the exact thing the person searched for. Where that page does not exist, building it is part of the work rather than an upsell.

Bids are managed against geography and time, reporting is on cost per lead rather than impressions, and the conversion tracking is checked properly — a surprising share of the accounts we inherit have been optimising against a conversion that was never firing.

All of that is standard Google Ads work. What is specific to Barrie is the order we do it in.

Where we would start

What We Would Change First On A Barrie Account

First, the search terms report. Almost every inherited account is paying for clicks nobody would have approved if they had seen the list, and that is a same-week saving rather than a quarterly one.

Second, the geography. Barrie businesses routinely bid across the whole county at one rate. Splitting that so the city and the outlying areas are bid separately usually improves cost per lead without reducing volume.

Third, the landing pages. If every campaign points at the homepage, the account is being judged on the worst possible version of itself.

Fourth, conversion tracking, because none of the above can be measured honestly until it is right.

Straight answers

Google Ads in Barrie — FAQ

Can a Barrie business make Google Ads pay against GTA budgets?

Usually yes, but not by competing on the broad terms. Narrow intent, hard negative lists and a landing page that matches the search beat a bigger budget spent generically.

How quickly do we know if it is working?

Faster than SEO — weeks rather than months, because you are buying the traffic. What takes longer is getting the cost per lead somewhere sensible, and that is the number worth judging it on.

Why is our cost per click so high in Barrie?

Mostly because GTA advertisers bid into this market without caring what a Barrie lead is worth. You will not out-bid them, which is why the work goes into being more specific than them instead.
