The stack

Platforms We Build On

Every client is activated on this stack from day one, white-labelled under NewLife and licensed by us — including our own reporting platform, built in-house. These are the tools we operate in daily, listed as capability rather than as a claim of partner status.

Meta

Facebook & Instagram advertising

Prospecting-to-retargeting funnels, lead forms and catalogue campaigns run daily in Meta Ads Manager.

Google

Ads, Analytics & Business Profile

Search, Shopping, YouTube and Local Services campaigns, GA4 conversion tracking and Google Business Profile management.

Semrush

SEO & local visibility

Keyword research, rank tracking, technical audits and local listing management.

GoHighLevel

CRM & automation

Pipelines, calendars, automations and speed-to-lead follow-up, white-labelled under NewLife for every client.

Sprout Social

Social command centre

Scheduling, publishing, inbox management and reporting across every social channel we run.

Adobe

Creative production

Premiere, After Effects, Photoshop and Illustrator — the full post pipeline for everything our crew shoots.

In-house software

One Platform We Did Not License

NewLife Reporting

Our own client dashboard, built and maintained in-house. Meta, Google, Google Business Profile, SEO and organic numbers land in one place and refresh as the month runs — rather than arriving as a PDF after it ends.

Because we own the codebase, reporting can be changed to match how a client actually measures their business instead of how a vendor decided to display it.

Partner status

What We Can Put In Writing

Platform partnerships

Formal partner and reseller badges are published here only once held and verifiable. We license and operate every platform above; we do not describe that as a partnership unless the platform says so.

Certifications

Individual platform certifications held by the team are listed here as they are completed and can be verified.

Security & compliance

We are not SOC 2 or ISO certified today and will not imply otherwise. What we can provide is a written description of how access and client data are handled — see Trust & Data.

Insurance

Liability insurance certificates are provided directly to procurement on request during vendor onboarding.
