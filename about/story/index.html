The milestones

How We Got Here

2022 · Founded

Cruz Lee starts NewLife Marketing in Barrie, Ontario — building campaigns for local businesses with a camera in hand and a rule that has not changed since: the work has to pay for itself.

The in-house crew

Rather than subcontract, the team is built in-house — paid media, SEO, film, design and CRM under one roof, so the people who shoot the content are the people who run the ads and report on them.

Cameras leave Ontario

Client productions take the crew to Aruba, Thunder Bay, Tampa and Washington DC — cinematic property, hospitality and brand work shot on location.

The reporting problem

Clients kept asking the same question between monthly PDFs: how is it going right now? So we built NewLife Reporting in-house — a live client dashboard rather than a report that arrives late.

Beyond local

The same discipline applied to longer, committee-led sales: account-based programmes, go-to-market strategy and demand generation for B2B and enterprise teams.

Today

A small senior crew running paid media, SEO, social, creative production and CRM for Ontario businesses — and named-account programmes for companies with a pipeline number to hit.

The rule

The Work Has To Pay For Itself

That is the whole thesis. It is why we publish platform-reported numbers with the attribution window stated, why we will tell you when a channel is not working, and why we would rather lose a pitch than promise a result we cannot stand behind.
