The prevailing agency model

Under that model the agency earns more when the budget goes up and less when it comes down. So the recommendation to expand into a new campaign type is well paid, the recommendation to raise the budget is well paid, and the recommendation to cut spending on something that is not working costs the agency money to make.

None of that makes ppc management toronto agencies dishonest. That is not an accusation of bad faith against anybody in Toronto. Most people running these accounts are honest and competent. It is a statement about which conversations happen easily and which ones require somebody to argue against their own invoice, and over three years that difference compounds into a particular shape of account.

Toronto ppc management is bought this way almost universally, which is why the alternative is worth knowing about before the first contract rather than after the third quarter of flat results.

What the incentive builds

How The Account Ends Up Looking

The Toronto fee model leaves fingerprints. Once you know what to look for, an inherited account tells you how it was paid for.

In a Toronto account the branded campaign is the clearest example, because it is the easiest to defend and the easiest to measure. Bidding on your own name captures traffic that was already arriving, reports an outstanding cost per conversion, and makes the whole account look better than it is. There are genuine reasons to run one. There are also reasons it appears in almost every account managed on a percentage.

The Toronto test is simple and nobody runs it: pause the branded campaign for two weeks and watch what happens to total enquiries rather than to paid enquiries. If the total holds, you were paying a percentage to buy traffic you already had.

The alternatives

Three Ways To Pay A PPC Agency Toronto Firms Will Quote

Why percentage persists

It is easy to explain, it scales with the client, and it lets an agency say its interests are aligned because it earns more when you grow. The flaw is that it earns more when you spend, which is not the same thing. A google ads agency toronto buyers pick on a percentage has agreed, structurally, to be paid least in the quarter it does its most useful work.

What a flat fee changes

The conversation about cutting a campaign becomes free to have. So does the conversation about pausing for a month, moving budget to a different channel, or telling you the account is already at the size the market supports. None of those are available for the same price under a percentage.

The other half

What PPC Services Toronto Budgets Are Really Competing With

Fee structure aside, Toronto is the most expensive auction in Ontario, and the way to survive it is not to bid harder.

Narrow beats loud

The Toronto head terms are contested by national advertisers with budgets you cannot match. The three and four word searches underneath them are not, and they convert better because the person typing them already knows what they want.

The landing page is half the auction

In Toronto, relevance between the ad and the page affects what you pay per click. A ppc company toronto businesses hire to manage bids while pointing every ad at the homepage is working on the cheaper half of the problem.

Count something real

Optimise to a number that corresponds to money. Toronto ppc services reporting form fills will dutifully produce more form fills, which is not the same as producing more work, and the gap between them is where most budgets disappear.

Questions

Google Ads In Toronto: Common Questions

How should we pay an agency to run our ads?

In Toronto, a flat monthly fee for the work, in most cases. The market default is a percentage of ad spend, which is easy to explain and pays the agency more for spending more. Under it, the recommendation to increase a budget is well paid and the recommendation to cut one costs the agency money to make. A flat fee makes every honest recommendation cost the same, which is the point.

Is a percentage model dishonest?

No, and that is what makes it worth discussing with any Toronto agency. Most people running these accounts are competent and well intentioned. The model does not produce fraud, it produces a bias in which conversations happen easily: expanding is easy to propose, trimming requires arguing against your own invoice. Over three years that difference compounds into a particular shape of account, which is visible once you know what to look for.

Should we bid on our own brand name?

Sometimes, and in Toronto it is tested far less often than it is defended. A branded campaign captures traffic that was largely arriving anyway, reports an excellent cost per conversion, and improves the look of the whole account. The test is to pause it for two weeks and watch total enquiries rather than paid enquiries. If the total holds, you were paying a management percentage to buy traffic you already had.

How does a smaller advertiser compete here?

By not competing where the Toronto money is. The head terms are contested by national advertisers with budgets you cannot match, and bidding harder is the one lever that is always available and almost never the right one. The three and four word searches underneath them are cheaper and convert better, because the person typing one already knows what they want. Add a landing page that matches each ad and weekly negatives, and the cost per result falls without the budget rising.

What is the first thing you look at in an inherited account?

In a Toronto account: the contract, then the conversion actions, then the search terms report. The contract tells you what the account was built to do. The conversion actions tell you what the bidding has been trained towards, which is frequently a form fill rather than a customer. The search terms report tells you what you have actually been buying, which in this market is usually a long list of queries nobody would have chosen to pay for.
