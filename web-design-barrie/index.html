Reading a quote

The Six Questions That Separate Barrie Website Design Quotes

Ask all six of everybody quoting. The answers vary enormously and none of them is in the proposal.

The ownership question is the one that costs Barrie owners most. A site built on an account the designer controls, with a domain registered in their name, is a site you are renting. It is not dishonest and it is rarely explained, and it becomes a real problem the day you want to move. Ask it in writing and keep the answer.

The rankings question is the second. A rebuild that changes addresses without redirects can cost you traffic that took years to build, and the person quoting may genuinely not know that, because a web designer barrie businesses hire is often a designer rather than a search person. Neither is wrong. You just need to know which one you are buying. Web site creation barrie firms advertise and search migration are genuinely different trades that happen to arrive in the same quote.

Where the money goes

What Changes A Barrie Price, And What Does Not

What makes a Barrie quote genuinely cheaper

You already have the words written and real photographs taken. That is not a small discount. On most projects the writing and the imagery are the largest honest line, and a client who arrives with both can legitimately pay half. Any website design barrie quote that does not change when you supply those is not itemised properly.

What makes a Barrie quote legitimately more

A migration that has to preserve rankings, an integration with a booking or quoting system, a genuine bilingual build, or a product catalogue with real data behind it. These are hours that cannot be compressed, and a quote that prices them the same as a brochure site has misunderstood the job.

What is actually wrong

Most Barrie Sites Need Four Fixes, Not A Rebuild

Before paying any Barrie firm, it is worth knowing whether a rebuild is the answer at all. On the sites we are called about, usually it is not.

Get the speed measured first

On a phone, on mobile data, not on the office connection. Barrie web development quotes rarely mention a load time target, and it is the one number that affects both ranking and conversion at the same time. Ask for it before you sign anything.

Ask what a change will cost

Specifically: adding a service page, changing a price, swapping a photograph. If the answer is a call to the designer every time, you have bought a dependency. Custom websites barrie firms are happiest with are the ones their own staff can edit.

Check it on a phone in a Barrie parking lot

Mobile website design barrie buyers actually experience is the version loading on a weak signal with one hand. Testing it anywhere else is testing the wrong thing.

Questions

Web Design In Barrie: Common Questions

Why do Barrie web design quotes vary so much?

Mostly because of what is and is not included rather than the quality of the design, and Barrie has enough firms quoting that the spread is wider here than in smaller Ontario towns. The large honest variables are who writes the words, whether photography is included, whether an existing site has to be migrated without losing rankings, and what happens after launch. A five thousand dollar quote and a fifteen thousand dollar quote are frequently the same design work with completely different answers to those four. Ask all four of everybody and the quotes become comparable for the first time.

Do we own our website?

Ask, in writing, before you sign. You should own the domain, the hosting account, the analytics property and the files. A site built inside an account the designer controls is a site you are renting, which is not dishonest and is rarely explained, and it becomes a real problem the day you want to move or the designer stops answering. This is the single most common unpleasant surprise we are called in about, and it is entirely avoidable with one question at the start.

Will a new site hurt our Google rankings?

It can, and it frequently does, when addresses change without redirects, copy gets cut for visual reasons, or the new build is slower on a phone. All three are decisions rather than accidents. We map every existing address and what it ranks for before design starts, so whatever is working keeps its address or gets a redirect. If you are getting quotes elsewhere, ask how they handle this specifically; the answer tells you a great deal about what you are buying.

Can we just fix the site we have?

Very often, yes, and it is usually the right answer for a Barrie business. Four problems account for most of what is wrong with the sites here: phone load speed, a first screen that does not say what you do, no proof of your own work, and an awkward contact path. All four can be fixed on an existing site in a few weeks for a fraction of a rebuild. A rebuild is genuinely the answer when the platform is unsupported, the structure cannot carry the services you now sell, or you do not own it.

Do you work with other designers in town?

Regularly, and we are happy to. There are good people doing web design in Barrie, and the parts that most often need outside help are not design: the redirect map, the performance budget, the structured data and the measurement. If you have a designer you like, we would rather hand them a specific list than replace them. We are based here, we run into each other, and nobody in a city this size benefits from the alternative.
