The problem

Where Web Design Barrie Projects Go Wrong

Four failures account for most of the sites rebuilt within three years, and none of them is about how the site looks.

One page for six services

A single services page can rank for one of them at best. Six pages, each about one job, can rank for six, and each can say enough to be worth reading rather than a bullet in a list.

Nobody inside can change a word

If updating an opening hour needs a supplier, a purchase order and a fortnight, it will not happen, and within two years the site describes a business that no longer exists.

The old addresses were dropped

Every page on the previous site has to point somewhere on the new one. Where it does not, years of accumulated value disappear quietly and recovery is never guaranteed.

The words were never written

Launches slip on copy far more often than on build. The material is in the owner’s head and the project waits for somebody to find a free afternoon.

What it is

Web Design Barrie And Web Development Barrie Are Not The Same Purchase

The words get used interchangeably and they are two different jobs with two different prices.

Design is the pages: what they say, in what order, how they look and how fast they load. For most businesses here that is the whole project, and a competent web design company Barrie has plenty of will do it in weeks.Development is anything that has to think — a login, a booking that checks availability, a calculator, a connection to the system you actually run on. Web development Barrie firms quote for is priced by the hour because the work is genuinely unbounded until somebody has mapped it.

The test is one question: does anything on the site have to compute something specific to your business? If not, you are buying design, and anybody quoting development rates is selling you the wrong thing. If yes, a web development company Barrie businesses hire should be scoping it before quoting, not after.

Barrie web development and design are frequently sold together as one number, which is how a project ends up costing three times what the pages alone would have.

What a build contains

The Page List Comes Before The Design

An hour with a pencil that settles most of what a design argument would otherwise be about.

Write every page the site will have, grouped the way a visitor would expect to find them. Not a wireframe — a list of titles, with one line each saying who that page is for and what it has to make them do.

Almost every scope dispute lives in that list. A quote without one is meaningless, and two quotes against the same list are finally comparable, which they never are otherwise.

It also exposes the pages with nothing to say. A title nobody can write a sentence about is a page that will be padded, and a padded page is worse than an absent one.

Then the words, gathered by interview rather than by waiting. An owner handed a blank document produces nothing for six weeks; the same owner asked the same questions out loud answers all of them in forty minutes.

On a phone

Mobile Website Design Barrie Businesses Actually Need

Your site is approved on a large screen in good light and used on a small one in a vehicle.

Before final payment, open every important page on an ordinary phone, outdoors, on mobile data rather than office wi-fi. Tap the phone number. Fill in the form and confirm the message arrives somewhere a person will see it. Try to find the hours in five seconds without scrolling twice.

Most of what makes a site slow is photographs straight from a camera at several megabytes each. Sized for the space they occupy and saved properly they are visually identical and a fraction of the weight, and it is the easiest fix on this page.

Ask for a speed figure measured on a mid-range phone over a mobile connection rather than on a laptop over office broadband. The two numbers differ enormously and only one of them describes your visitors.

Mobile website design Barrie suppliers quote for is rarely a separate line and should not be: a site that does not work on a phone is not finished, it is broken on the device most people will use.

Template or custom

Custom Website Design Barrie Buyers Pay More For, And When It Is Worth It

A bought template gets a reasonable site live quickly for very little. The saving is real and so is the ceiling.

For a business that needs a presence rather than a sales tool, a template is a sound decision honestly made. The cost arrives the first time you want something it was not built to do: a different layout, a particular form behaviour, a section arranged the way your work actually divides. Fighting a template to do that costs more than building the piece would have.

So the question is how much you expect to change. A business whose offering is settled can live inside a template for years. One still working out what it sells will hit the edges within months.

Custom website design Barrie firms charge for buys exactly one thing: the absence of that ceiling. It is worth paying for when you already know of two things the template cannot do, and it is worth nothing when you do not.

There is no wrong answer here, only an answer made in advance or discovered later. Made in advance it is a saving; discovered later it is a second project.

Connected at build time

Wired In From Day One, Or Not At All

The difference between a site that produces enquiries and one that produces visits.

Where does a form submission go? To an inbox somebody checks twice a day, or to a phone? Does the sender get an acknowledgement naming a time, or a bare thank-you line? Is there a record a week later, or did it only ever exist as an email?

An acknowledgement that names a time is worth more than any design decision on the page before it. It stops the person contacting a competitor while they wait, which is otherwise exactly what they do.

The same applies to counting. Analytics on the old site for a month before a rebuild is what makes the new one assessable; without it every later conversation about whether it worked is two people exchanging impressions.

None of this is expensive and all of it is decided before the build rather than bolted on after, which is why it belongs in the quote rather than in a conversation six weeks later.

Logins and portals

When A Site Needs A Door Behind It

A portal is the right answer surprisingly often and the expensive answer almost always.

The signal is people emailing you for things that should live somewhere they can log into: documents, updates, an order history, a schedule. If that email happens weekly, a portal pays for itself in the time it returns.

What it needs decided before anything is built is who sees what. The permission model is the part that has to be right first time, because retrofitting roles onto a system that assumed one kind of user is most of a rebuild.

What it does not need is everything at once. One login, the two or three things people actually ask for, and a decision in six months about whether to add more. A portal scoped to everything somebody might one day want is a portal that never launches.

And it needs the dull half settled: who pays the hosting bill, whose name the accounts are in, and what happens on the day the person who built it is unavailable.

Choosing somebody

How To Judge A Web Design Barrie Company

Four questions, none of them technical, all answerable on a first call.

“Can I see the page list before the design?” If the answer is a mood board, the scope has not been agreed and the argument is coming later.“Who writes the words, and when are they due?” Content is the commonest cause of a late launch and it is almost never in the quote.“What happens to the old addresses?” A web design agency Barrie businesses should hire answers this in one sentence: every URL mapped to its replacement, written before launch, applied at launch.“What will it cost to run, and who can edit it?” Hosting, patching, backups and a named person inside your business who can change a paragraph without phoning anybody.

On looking for the best website design company Barrie has

There is no best website design company Barrie or anywhere else, and the pages ranking Barrie website design companies are usually selling the slots. Judge on the four answers above, which any Barrie web design company can give you in ten minutes and none of which can be bought.

Price

What A Web Design Barrie Build Costs, And What It Costs To Run

Two numbers, and the second one is the one nobody quotes.

Nobody can price web design Barrie work from a phone call, and anybody who does is quoting for a project they have not scoped. The build is set by four things and none of them is the page count: how much is bespoke rather than a design system applied consistently, what it connects to, who writes the words, and whether anything has to compute. A Barrie web design service quoting from page count alone has not asked about any of them.

What A Web Design Barrie Quote Should Contain

The running cost is hosting, the platform and its components kept patched, a working backup and a stated response time when something breaks. That is a real service worth paying for, and the figure belongs in the first quote rather than in year two.

Ask how many hours of edits the monthly fee contains. A retainer with none is a maintenance contract, not a relationship, and every text change will be quoted separately.

And ask what happens on the day you stop paying. A site that cannot be moved is a site where the fee is not really for maintenance.

Who it is for

Who This Suits, And Who We Talk Out Of It

The second half saves more money than the first half makes.

It suits a business with more than one thing to sell, where an enquiry is worth enough that a handful more a month changes the year, and where somebody inside will own the content afterwards.It does not suit a business whose current site is fine and whose enquiries are not arriving. That is a problem upstream of the website and a rebuild will not fix it — the twenty-minute phone test settles which situation you are in, and we would rather run it than quote for the wrong project.

It also does not suit anybody who cannot supply photographs, answers and decisions inside a few days. Every step above needs one, and a project waiting on the client is the commonest cause of overrun in this trade.

Ordinary, expensive, and four of the five are free to avoid.

Designing before the page list

Every scope dispute later is this one, surfacing late.

No redirect map

The single commonest way a rebuild destroys something that was working.

No measurement before the switch

Nothing to compare against afterwards, so the question is unanswerable forever.

A site nobody inside can edit

Two years later it describes a business that has moved on.

Approving it on a laptop

It is used on a phone, outdoors, on mobile data, and that is a different experience.

No answer about price anywhere

Straight answers

Web Design Barrie FAQ

Straight answers before you ever book a call.

Will a new site bring us more enquiries?

Only if the current one is losing them, and that is testable in twenty minutes before you spend anything. Open it on a phone outdoors, try to dial the number with a thumb, send yourself a test enquiry and see where it lands. Most of what a rebuild would fix shows up in that test, and some of it can be fixed without one.

What does Web Design Barrie cost?

Web design Barrie quotes vary by a factor of five for the same brief, and the way to narrow it is to write the page list first. Two quotes against the same list are comparable; two against a conversation are not, and the difference between them is usually scope rather than quality.

Do we need web design or web development?

Design, unless something on the site has to compute — a login, a calculator, a connection to a system you depend on. Ask any supplier directly which they are quoting for, because the words get used interchangeably and the prices do not.

How long does it take?

The build is rarely the constraint. Content is. A project with the words ready runs to weeks; one waiting on an owner to write them runs to months, and the difference is entirely on the client side of the line.

What happens to our old pages?

Every address gets mapped to its replacement, written before launch and applied at launch. Done afterwards it still works, but the gap costs rankings that took years to earn.

Can we edit it ourselves?

You should be able to change the text on any page and add a new one from a template, without phoning anybody. Ask for it to be demonstrated before final payment, by the person who will actually do it, on a real page.
