Marketing Agency Thunder Bay

A Market That Already Knows

A marketing agency thunder bay is selling into something unusual: this city produces 260 monthly searches for search help against 90 in Sudbury, a city half again as large, which says the businesses here are already looking rather than waiting to be told.

What the demand says

What A Marketing Agency Thunder Bay Is Walking Into

Nearly three times the demand from two-thirds the population is a large gap, and the most likely explanation is the geography: with the nearest larger city roughly 700 km away, businesses here cannot lean on a neighbouring market’s suppliers or its habits and have had to work it out themselves.

That changes what a supplier should bring. A market that has already tried things does not need the introductory conversation; it needs somebody who will say which of the things it is doing are worth continuing and which are not.

It also means the questions you will be asked are sharper than average, and a proposal built on general claims will be recognised as one quickly.

So the useful offer here is specific, bounded and willing to say what it will not do — which is a smaller engagement than most agencies prefer to quote and the one this market actually buys.

The part of the cluster that names it

What Thunder Bay Consulting And Marketing Should Hand Over

Thirty searches a month ask for consulting rather than execution, and the deliverable is the whole point.

Thunder Bay consulting and marketing is bought when a business has somebody who can execute and nobody who can say what to execute. The engagement is short and the thing that makes it worth paying for is what you are left holding afterwards.

That should be four documents, none of them long. The registry of which page owns which search, so two of your own pages never compete. The profile audit with its corrections listed. The value of a customer and the maximum defensible cost per enquiry that follows from it. And a service-area decision: how far you go, and what changes beyond it.

Those four govern everything spent afterwards, they are readable by whoever does the work, and they survive a change of supplier — which in a city 700 km from the nearest alternative is not a small consideration.

A consulting engagement that ends in a presentation and no documents has sold you a meeting. Ask what you will be holding at the end before agreeing to anything.

What to buy

What A Marketing Agency Thunder Bay Should Build First

Each compounds, and none of them needs somebody to tend it weekly.

A correct Business Profile: categories, service area, hours and photographs that match what you do now. It decides a large share of local results, it is free, and it is almost always wrong somewhere.

One page per thing you sell, named the way customers name it. Five services on one page ranks for none of them; five pages cost the same to write and produce five results, and they keep earning without anybody touching them.

And reviews collected as a process rather than an intention: a specific moment in the job when somebody asks, and a short link that makes it a thirty-second task.

In a city where the market has already tried the visible things, these three are frequently what has been skipped, because none of them produces anything to look at in the first week. That is also why they are still available: the work that shows nothing early is the work nobody finished.

Distance

What To Ask A Supplier 1,400 Kilometres Away

Toronto is 1,400 km from here and Winnipeg is 700. Almost every supplier is remote, including us.

What happens at a distance

Search work, advertising, reporting and strategy, by almost everybody. A southern supplier is not worse at these, and most of the work sits in this column.

What does not

Filming, photographing a site, sitting with a team. These carry travel, and from 1,400 km away that travel is a real number rather than a formality.

So the questions are specific. How many days on site does this proposal contain, and at what cost. What happens when something needs to be seen rather than described. Who answers when the person who sold the work is unavailable. And what would you hand over if we stopped — a question with real force when there is no second supplier down the road.

What to write down

One Sheet, Two Columns, Kept For A Year

The whole measurement system most businesses this size actually need.

Every enquiry: the date, and what they say when asked how they found you. Nothing else. Written down by whoever takes the call, in the moment, without anybody interpreting it.

Across a year that sheet answers the only questions that matter: whether enquiries are rising, and which routes are producing them. No dashboard covers the second, because most of those routes are invisible to every platform.

It also survives a change of supplier, a change of platform and a change of fashion, which nothing else in this subject has managed.

Read it quarterly rather than monthly. At these volumes a single month contains too few rows to show anything, and reacting to it produces changes that are random by construction.

Our scope

A Narrow Offer, Said Plainly

Four things, and a list of what we do not do.

Paid media on Google and Meta managed against cost per enquiry. Search work built one cluster at a time so two of your own pages never compete. Video and photography of real work. And the systems underneath, so an enquiry can be traced to what produced it. We manage over $70,000 a month in advertising spend for Ontario businesses and the owner runs the paid media personally.
