What Management Covers

Hiring a ppc agency markham almost always produces a line on an invoice reading management, and almost never a list of what that word contains — which is the single most useful thing to settle before anything is signed, because the list is what you are buying.

An account can be genuinely managed in an hour a week or genuinely neglected for the same fee, and nothing on a monthly report distinguishes the two. Both show spend, clicks and a chart.

So the useful document is not the proposal; it is the list of what happens, how often, and what triggers an exception. A supplier who can produce it in a sentence has one. A supplier who cannot is quoting availability.

In a GTA auction that matters more than it would elsewhere, because click prices are set by competitors with real budgets and an unattended account loses money steadily rather than suddenly.

It also makes the fee comparable. Two quotes for the same number are not the same purchase when one includes the search terms report being read and the other does not.

The part of the cluster that names it

What PPC Management Markham Businesses Buy Should Contain

Thirty searches a month name management specifically. Here is the list it should mean.

The search terms report, read weekly

What people actually typed rather than what you asked for. It is the most useful document in any account and the one least often opened, and in PPC management it is the first thing to ask about.

Negative keywords, maintained

The list of what you are not, growing every week from the report above. A handful of irrelevant phrases can absorb a meaningful share of a month before anybody notices.

The geography, reviewed quarterly

Checked against where paying customers actually were, not against where somebody hoped they would be. In the GTA a drifting boundary is expensive quickly.

A named exception trigger

What has to happen for somebody to contact you between reports — a cost per enquiry threshold, a spend anomaly, a conversion drop. Without one, the first anybody hears of a problem is a month later.

Those four are what PPC management means when it means anything. Ask for them by name, and ask what happens in the weeks when nothing needs changing, because that is the honest version of the question.

How the fee is charged

How A PPC Agency Markham Charges, And What That Rewards

The commonest arrangement in this industry, and it points the wrong way.

Under a percentage fee, the supplier’s income rises when your budget rises and falls when they make the account more efficient. Nobody involved has to be dishonest for that to shape decisions over a year.

A flat monthly fee for a defined scope has the opposite problem in miniature — no reward for improvement — but it at least does not punish it, and it makes the fee comparable between suppliers.

The arrangement worth asking for is a flat fee with a stated list of what happens each month, reviewed when the account grows enough to justify more work.

Whatever the model, the number to ask for is the fee as a share of what the account produced rather than of what it spent. That figure is the one that tells you whether the arrangement is working.

Whose account

The Advertising Account Should Be In Your Name

An administrative detail for as long as the arrangement lasts, and the whole asset on the day it ends.

An account created by an agency inside their own structure holds the history, the exclusion lists, the conversion data and everything the system has learned. None of that transfers.

Starting again elsewhere means repaying for the learning period and rebuilding lists that took a year to accumulate. That cost is invisible until the day somebody wants to leave.

So the arrangement is simple and worth insisting on at the start: the account is created under your business, the supplier is granted access, and access can be withdrawn.

The same applies to the conversion setup and the audience lists. A supplier who keeps those on their side is holding the part of the work that cannot be recreated quickly, and that is usually deliberate.

Ask on the first call, before anything is built. It costs a sentence then and it is an unwinnable conversation afterwards, which is exactly why it is worth having early.

A good supplier will have said it first. Agencies that work this way raise ownership themselves, because an account the client holds is a client who stays by choice, and that is a stronger position to run a relationship from than a locked door.

A technical audience

Automated Campaign Types Need A Boundary

Markham describes itself as a High-Tech city, and the buyers here ask the right question about automation.

Campaign types that choose their own placements, audiences and geography inside whatever budget they are given will reliably find the cheapest available impressions. Cheap impressions are cheap for a reason.

Used with a tight boundary, clean conversion data and a maintained exclusion list, they work well. Used as a replacement for those three, they produce a confident report describing activity you did not choose.

So the question to put to any supplier is not whether they use them but what constrains them: which conversions are being optimised toward, what geography is enforced, what is excluded, and how any of that is reviewed.

A supplier who answers that clearly is doing the work. One who treats the question as scepticism about the platform has misunderstood it.

What We Run And How We Report It

We build and run Google and Meta accounts against cost per enquiry, write the pages the ads point at, and set up the tracking that connects an enquiry to its source. We manage over $70,000 a month in advertising spend for Ontario businesses and the owner runs the paid media personally.

What we put in writing as your ppc agency markham

As a ppc agency markham we state what happens weekly, what happens monthly, what triggers a call between reports, and what the account would be handed back as if you left. Those four lines are the whole of what management means, and writing them down costs nothing.

We do not sell percentage-of-spend management, because it pays us to recommend the opposite of what a well-run account usually needs. We are in Minesing, which changes nothing for running an account and matters only for filming.

What should management actually include?

The search terms report read weekly, negative keywords maintained from it, the geography reviewed quarterly against where paying customers were, and a named trigger for contacting you between reports. Ask for those four by name, and ask what happens in the weeks when nothing needs changing.

How do we decide what to spend?

From three numbers rather than a benchmark: what a customer is worth over the relationship, what share of enquiries convert, and the maximum defensible cost per enquiry that follows. With those the budget is arithmetic; without them any figure is borrowed from a business that is not yours.

What lowers cost per enquiry fastest?

The landing page, then the negative keyword list, then the geography, then retargeting. None of them is the bid. Advertising magnifies whatever the page already does, so doubling spend over a slow page doubles the waste rather than the result.

Should we use automated campaign types?

Yes, with a boundary. Left to choose their own placements, audiences and geography they will find the cheapest impressions available, and cheap impressions are cheap for a reason. Ask what conversions are being optimised toward, what geography is enforced, what is excluded and how it is reviewed.
