Meta Ads Thunder Bay

Meta Ads in Thunder Bay

The cheapest attention in the province, and a catchment nobody else is advertising to.

Book a strategy callHow we run Meta Ads

The market

The Cheapest Attention In Ontario

Paid social in Thunder Bay is inexpensive by any provincial standard. There are few advertisers competing, which keeps costs low and makes it realistic to reach a very large share of the local audience repeatedly.

The northwest catchment is the second opportunity, and it is almost entirely unaddressed. For a huge area Thunder Bay is the practical destination, and those people are reachable on the platform long before they search for anything.

The limiting factor is not budget or competition. It is creative, because very little produced advertising exists in this market and the standard is set accordingly low.

What we would target

Advertising To A Region Nobody Else Is

Audiences cover the city and the regional catchment separately, so each can be measured on its own terms.

Creative is the differentiator, and clearing the local bar does not require a large budget.

The audience and creative process is covered on our Meta Ads page.

The work

What Meta Ads Management Involves In Thunder Bay

Targeting is built for the city and for the northwest catchment as separate audiences, because the second is the distinctive part of this market and deserves its own measurement.

Your own data forms the warm layer; cold audiences are built on behaviour and interest, which in a low-competition market can afford to be broader than they could in the GTA.

Creative is produced on location. With so little produced advertising in the market, a properly filmed advert stands out in the feed in a way it simply would not further south.

Campaigns separate cold, warm and customer audiences, frequency is monitored because small audiences saturate, and reporting is cost per lead by geography.

All of that is standard Meta Ads work. What is specific to Thunder Bay is the order we do it in.

Where we would start

Where We Would Start On A Thunder Bay Account

With the catchment, because it is the largest unused opportunity in most accounts here and costs almost nothing to reach.

Then creative, filmed properly, since that is the main thing separating advertisers in this market.

Then structure, replacing boosted posts with real campaigns and separated audiences.

Then frequency management, because in a small market a good advert still burns out.

Straight answers

Meta Ads in Thunder Bay - FAQ

Can we reach the smaller communities?

Yes, and almost nobody is. For a region where Thunder Bay is the practical destination, that audience is both cheap and genuinely relevant.

How big a budget do we need?

Less than you would need in the south. We would rather start modestly, prove the cost per lead, and scale from a number you trust.

Why is it so cheap to advertise here?

Few advertisers are competing for the attention. It is a structural advantage of the market and the main reason a modest budget goes a long way in the northwest.
