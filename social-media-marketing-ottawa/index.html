The quiet audience

Ottawa Reads Without Engaging, And That Is Not Apathy

The Values and Ethics Code for the Public Sector, published by the Treasury Board of Canada Secretariat, requires public servants to carry out their duties impartially and to avoid anything that could be read as preferential treatment of a supplier. Most people apply that cautiously. They will read your post, send it to a colleague, and never touch the like button.

In most cities that behaviour is rare enough to ignore. In Ottawa it describes a large share of the professional audience, and it breaks the standard way agencies report. An account with thin engagement and strong reach looks like a failing account on a dashboard and is frequently the opposite here. We have seen Ottawa businesses cancel the channel that was doing the most to make them look like a credible supplier, on the strength of a number that was never going to be high in this city.

So social media marketing ottawa businesses buy has to be measured differently from the start. Reach, saves, profile visits, direct messages, and whether the name comes up in sales calls. Not likes.

A small professional city

Everyone In Ottawa Is Two Introductions Apart

Ottawa is a large city with a small professional world. The same people show up on the buying side, the competitor side and the hiring side.

What this rewards

Consistency and specifics. An Ottawa account that posts real project detail, named staff and actual results builds a reputation that travels by direct message rather than by comment. People here check who you are before they introduce you to somebody, and social media ottawa buyers encounter is frequently the first thing they find.

What this punishes

Generic content and borrowed posts. In a professional community this tightly connected, recycled industry quotes read as having nothing of your own to say, and it is noticed faster than almost anywhere. An Ottawa social media agency filling a calendar with stock posts is actively costing you credibility.

It also changes which platform matters. For most Ottawa B2B and professional services, the audience is on LinkedIn, and the second platform is wherever your recruiting happens rather than wherever consumer brands are. Ottawa social media marketing that opens with an Instagram calendar for a firm selling to departments and technology companies has started in the wrong place.

There are real exceptions. Restaurants, retail, trades and consumer services across Ottawa and eastern Ontario behave like they do anywhere, and for those businesses the usual platform logic applies. The question is which Ottawa you are selling to, and it is worth ten minutes before a calendar is written.

Where the effort goes

What Social Media Management Ottawa Budgets Should Fund First

Post the work, not the opinions

A finished project, what the problem was, what it cost to solve, who did it. Social media marketing companies ottawa firms hire often avoid this because it takes access to the delivery team. It is also the only content that proves anything.

Put names and faces on it

In a professional community this small, a reviewer frequently recognises somebody. Anonymous corporate posting removes the one advantage an Ottawa business has over a national competitor posting into the same feed.

Say the sector out loud

Defence, health, municipal, technology, construction. Vague capability language reads as inexperience here, and the specific word is also the one being searched for.

Questions

Social Media In Ottawa: Common Questions

Our Ottawa engagement is terrible. Is the account failing?

Possibly not. A large share of the professional audience in Ottawa works under public-sector conduct rules that make publicly endorsing a supplier awkward, so they read, save and forward without ever liking anything. Before concluding the channel is failing, look at reach among the right job titles, saves and sends, profile visits and whether searches for your company name are rising. If those are moving and engagement is flat, the account is working and the dashboard is misleading you.

Which platform should an Ottawa business be on?

For most professional services, technology and B2B firms here, LinkedIn carries the audience and the second platform should be wherever recruiting happens rather than wherever consumer brands are. For restaurants, retail, trades and consumer services across Ottawa and eastern Ontario, the usual platform logic applies and LinkedIn is usually the wrong place to start. The question is which of those you are, and it is a ten-minute conversation that saves a year of posting into the wrong feed.

How often should we post?

Two or three posts a week with real substance beats daily filler, particularly in a professional community as connected as this one, where recycled content is noticed quickly. The constraint is almost never the writing. It is access to the people doing the work, because the posts that perform are the ones carrying project detail only they have. We would rather agree a realistic rhythm you can sustain for a year than a daily calendar that collapses into stock quotes by month three.

Can social media actually win federal work?

Not on its own, and anybody promising that is selling something. Departments buy through standing offers, supply arrangements and posted solicitations. What social does is make you a known quantity before and during those processes, so that when somebody on an evaluation team looks you up, there is a visible record of relevant work and real people behind it. That is worth having and it is worth budgeting, but it belongs under credibility rather than under lead generation.

Can you work with our in-house marketer?

Often, and it is usually the better arrangement. Where somebody in-house knows the business and can get to the delivery team, the things most worth bringing in from outside are the measurement framework, the quarterly read on whether it is affecting sales, and a second pair of eyes on what is worth posting. Those are the pieces that tend to go unexamined for years, and they decide whether the effort is pointed anywhere useful.
