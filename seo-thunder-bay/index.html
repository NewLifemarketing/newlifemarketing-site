SEO Thunder Bay

SEO in Thunder Bay

The hub of the northwest, half a province from anywhere, with a catchment most businesses never market to.

Book a strategy callHow we run SEO

The market

Half A Province From Anywhere

Thunder Bay is the regional capital of northwestern Ontario — about a hundred and ten thousand people on the shore of Lake Superior under the Sleeping Giant, and the commercial hub for a catchment that stretches further than most people south of here imagine.

Distance is the defining commercial fact. For a very large area, Thunder Bay is simply where you go, and the alternative is a flight or a day's drive. That gives local businesses a structural advantage they frequently leave on the table, because the person in a town three hours away is searching before they make the trip and almost nobody has built a page that speaks to them.

As in the rest of the north, southern competitors are largely absent and national chains do not localise. The result is a search landscape where a well-built page can take a category and hold it.

What we would target

The Catchment Nobody Markets To

The grid here reaches well past the city. The searches worth having include the ones run from the smaller communities in the catchment, where you are the realistic destination and the competition is nil.

Within the city, the work is ordinary and effective: a page per service, properly built, because in most categories the current results are not hard to beat.

How targets get chosen and pages get built is set out on our SEO Services page.

The work

What Our SEO Services Do In Thunder Bay

Research runs monthly across the city and the wider northwest, with volume and difficulty attached to every phrase. The difficulty numbers here are frequently the lowest we see anywhere in the province, which changes the strategy: terms that would be unreachable in the south are genuinely available.

Because the results are thin, technical and on-page work produce unusually fast movement. The monthly audit — crawl errors, broken links, page speed, missing metadata, markup problems — often surfaces the single thing holding a site back, and fixing it moves positions rather than just tidying a report.

On-page optimization then runs across the priority pages every month: titles, meta descriptions, headings, body copy, image alt text and internal links, with thin pages built up before they are optimised.

Content fills the gaps the main pages cannot cover, aimed at the planning-stage questions somebody three hours away asks before making the trip. And the Google Business Profile is managed throughout, because for a regional hub it carries a disproportionate share of the local searching.

That is the same programme set out on our Ontario SEO services page, weighted for what this market needs.

Where we would start

What We Would Build First In Thunder Bay

We would build the city pages first and expect them to move, because in a lot of categories here the current results are thin enough that a properly built page takes the position.

The catchment is the second build and the more interesting one. For a very large area, Thunder Bay is the practical destination, and those searches are run in advance by people planning a trip that takes real time. Almost nobody has built for them.

Third is anything that reduces the friction of distance — being explicit about delivery, travel, remote arrangements or whatever applies to your trade. That is content that answers the question the catchment is actually asking.

The map listing is quick, cheap and worth doing first in practice, because it frequently produces the earliest measurable result.

Straight answers

SEO in Thunder Bay — FAQ

Do people really search before driving in?

For anything that takes planning, yes. A trip of two or three hours is not made on spec, which is exactly why the pre-trip search is worth owning.

How fast does this move in a market like this?

Often faster than in the south, because there is less to displace. We still will not give you a date, but the first signs tend to arrive sooner than they would in a comparable southern city.

Are the searches big enough to be worth it?

Smaller than the south and far less contested, which is usually the better trade. We check the numbers for your specific services before taking the work rather than assuming.

Why us

What SEO Thunder Bay Costs You To Ignore

There are plenty of SEO companies who will sell you SEO Thunder Bay and report on impressions. Our search engine optimization work reports on positions, the pages earning traffic, and what that traffic did — because those are the only numbers that change a decision.

Our search engine optimization services cover the whole programme — technical, keyword research, on-page, content and Google Business Profile — and SEO Thunder Bay is simply that programme pointed at this market.

The northwest is a market most agencies have never looked at properly. That is inconvenient for them and useful for you.
