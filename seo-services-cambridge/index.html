Difficulty of 9 means the pages holding these positions are barely defended. That alone makes this a market where competent, unglamorous work moves quickly, and it will not stay that way indefinitely.

The second feature is the tail. Alongside the sixteen phrases in the visible cluster sit the industrial supply terms — the precise name of a process, a material, a tolerance, a part family — each carrying ten or twenty searches a month.

Those look negligible on a spreadsheet and are frequently the most valuable traffic a manufacturer can receive, because somebody typing the exact name of what you make is already past every stage of the decision except choosing a supplier.

Telling a machine what you are

The Markup Nobody Can See

A small block of code that states, unambiguously, facts a page only implies.

A human reading a page understands that the number near the top is a telephone number and the line under it is an address. A machine infers it, usually correctly and sometimes not.

Structured markup removes the inference: this is the business name, this is the address, these are the hours, these are the services, this is the review score. It is invisible to a visitor and it is what produces the richer entries people actually click.

It is a one-off job on a small site and it is frequently absent, because nothing looks wrong without it and nobody asks.

What it cannot do is make a claim true. Marking up a review score you do not have is detectable and penalised, and the risk is entirely on the business rather than on whoever typed it.

Depth

Why A SEO Services Cambridge Page Should Answer One Question Completely

The reliable way to win a specific search is to be the page that finishes the subject.

Somebody searching a narrow phrase has a narrow question. The page that wins is the one that answers it fully — what it costs, how long it takes, what goes wrong, what the alternatives are — rather than the one that mentions it and sells.

That is not a word count. A thorough answer in six hundred words beats a padded two thousand, and padding is detectable by any reader within a paragraph.

The test is whether somebody could stop reading at the end of your page and act. If they still have to look elsewhere, the page that answered the rest will be the one they remember.

It is also what makes a page durable. A page that completely answers something keeps earning for years, while a page written around a phrase has to be rewritten every time the phrasing moves.

The local layer

Two Different Questions, Two Different Answers

Whether proximity matters at all depends on something no tool can tell you.

If customers come to you, the map result is most of what decides things and the website is what they check afterwards. If you go to them, or the work is done remotely, the map matters far less and the pages carry it.

Businesses regularly get this backwards and spend accordingly — a remote-serving firm polishing a listing that barely appears, or a shop with a perfect site and an unclaimed profile.

The question that settles it is about your own customers rather than about search: of the last twenty, how many could reasonably have chosen somebody further away without minding? The honest answer decides where the budget goes.

Where the answer is genuinely mixed, do both and in that order: the listing first because it is free and fast, then the pages, which is where the longer-term result sits.

Before a page goes up

Six Things To Check On Every Page

A short list, applied every time, which prevents most of what gets fixed later.

One search per page

Named, written down, and different from every other page on the site. Two pages after one phrase is the commonest self-inflicted problem in this subject.

A title somebody would click

What the page is and where, in that order, without the company name taking the first half of it.

The question answered above the fold

Not an introduction. The thing somebody searched for, addressed in the first paragraph, because most readers decide there.

Two links in, two links out

From related pages on the site and to them. A page nobody links to is a page the site itself is treating as unimportant.

None of these takes more than a few minutes and all of them are skipped under time pressure. A page published without them is not wrong; it is simply working at a fraction of what it could.

What we would refuse

Three Things Not To Buy In This Market

Each is commonly quoted here and each points money at the wrong problem.

Monthly link acquisition

At difficulty 9 the result is decided long before links are reached. Links have a case eventually; buying them first is paying for the hardest part of a problem you do not have.

A blog, before the tail pages

Articles reach people earlier in their thinking, which is worth something once the pages that capture decided buyers exist. Starting there inverts the order and delays revenue by quarters.

A traffic target

In a market where the valuable phrases carry ten searches, a traffic target points every decision at the wrong half of the cluster. Agree an enquiry target instead, by landing page.

Saying this costs us work, which is the only reason it is worth reading.

What seo services cambridge work should hand back

SEO Services Cambridge work should leave you holding the registry of which page owns which phrase, the tail list still unclaimed, and an hour-by-hour note of what your own engineers said while the technical pages were written. That last one is frequently the most reusable document the project produces.

What we would want before quoting is five phrases a specifier would actually type and the name of whoever can spare an hour per page. Without the second, the tail pages cannot be written honestly and the whole plan collapses back into general service copy.

Is it worth building a page for a phrase with ten searches a month?

For a manufacturer, often yes. Ten people searching the exact designation of what you make are ten people specifying it now, and the page competing for them is usually nobody’s. Twenty such pages produce a stream of enquiries that no head term would have delivered, and they are cheap because nothing is defending them.

How long until we see results?

At difficulty 9, specific phrases can move within weeks and settle within a few months. The head term takes longer. The sensible order is tail first, because it shows movement early and because the pages are easier to write well, then the broader terms once the site has a track record.

Who writes the technical pages?

We write them, with an hour of somebody who actually knows the process. There is no substitute for that hour. A page about a manufacturing process written without it reads as marketing to the only people who matter, and a specifier can tell within a paragraph.

Do reviews matter for us?

Only if your customers chose you partly because you were nearby. For a supplier whose buyers are companies that found you from a desk, reviews are housekeeping: get them right and move on. For a business people visit, they are most of the local result and worth a process rather than good intentions.
