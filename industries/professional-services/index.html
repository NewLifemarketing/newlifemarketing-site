FAQ

Professional Services Marketing FAQ

What is Growth Partner?

A monthly marketing partnership with four levels. You choose the level and what runs inside it. Every level covers content, SEO, paid ads, your website, a CRM where you need one and weekly reporting. The levels change how much of it runs, and how hard.

Which level should my practice start with?

Most clients start at Growth Partner 1, which gets everything working properly before it runs hard. If the foundations work and reach is the limit, Growth Partner 2 is next. We recommend a level on the call, based on your goals.

Can I choose between Meta and Google ads?

Yes. Growth Partner 1 runs one campaign on the platform you choose. Meta creates demand and Google captures people who are already searching, and we recommend one for your business. From Growth Partner 2 both run together.

Can you work within my profession's advertising rules?

Your professional body's rules stay yours to apply. Everything we make is planned 30 days ahead and approved by you before it is scheduled, so nothing goes live that you have not seen.

Do I need a CRM?

Most practices do. Enquiries come in, someone has to answer, and consultations get booked. We set up GoHighLevel in your name, walk every person on your team through it and run it day to day. If you do not need one, we skip it.

Do you work with law firms, accountants and brokers?

Yes. We market practices that grow on qualified leads and booked consultations: law, accounting, insurance and mortgage brokerage, financial advice and consulting. We film on site so the content shows how you actually work.

Is ad spend included in the fee?

No. Ad spend is paid directly to Meta and Google and never sits inside our management fee.

How will I know it is working?

You get your own live dashboard from day one, a text every Monday, a call every week, a video report every Friday and a monthly meeting with the whole team. We report leads, calls, bookings and sales, not vanity numbers.

Can I move up a level later?

Yes. Levels 1 to 3 build on each other, so moving up adds and never swaps. Growth Partner 3 V2 is the fork: the same foundations as Growth Partner 2, with a dedicated sales manager instead of more marketing volume.

Do I own my accounts and data?

Your ad accounts, CRM, contacts and creative are yours. We build inside your assets, never hostage ones.
