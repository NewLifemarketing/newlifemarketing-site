FAQ

Ecommerce Marketing Agency FAQ

What results should I expect from ecommerce marketing?

It depends on your margin, price point and how much creative we can test. We optimize toward profitable return for your specific numbers, and we set a realistic target with you on the call rather than promise a fantasy multiple.

How much ad budget do I need?

Enough for Meta and Google to gather data and optimize; too little and there is no signal to work with. The right level is different for every store, margin and product range, so we recommend what fits your margins on the call.

Why does creative matter so much?

On a social feed the creative is the targeting: the algorithm finds buyers based on who responds to the ad. More winning creative means more scale, which is why we film it ourselves and produce it in volume.

Do you set up a CRM or follow-up sequences?

No, and that is deliberate. A shopper buys the moment they see the product, so there is no quote to send or lead to chase. This system puts the budget into reach, traffic and conversion instead. If your business sells through conversations, you want our Growth Partner system, and we will tell you so.

Do you work with in-store retail, not just online?

Yes. We drive online revenue and, for shops with a physical location, foot traffic too. We keep your Google Business Profile active every day, and the same content feeds both.

Can you fix my website, or only run ads?

We take your website over on day one. If it is already strong we keep it and build onto it. If it is not, we rebuild it as part of the system, then optimise it every month. Sending paid traffic to a page that does not convert just burns budget.

How will I know it is working?

You get your own live dashboard from day one, a video report every Friday, a weekly call with your account manager and a monthly meeting with the whole team. We report purchases, revenue and return on ad spend, not vanity numbers.

Do I own my accounts and data?

Always. Ad accounts, store, email list and creative are yours. We build inside your assets, never hostage ones.
