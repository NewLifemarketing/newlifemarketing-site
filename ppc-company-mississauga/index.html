PPC Company Mississauga

Two Words, Two Buyers

The cluster for a PPC company Mississauga businesses might hire splits almost evenly between two vocabularies — "ppc" and "google ads" — and the people typing each one want different things, which is why so many proposals here answer half the room.

On one side: ppc mississauga, mississauga ppc agencies, ppc management mississauga, ppc consulting services mississauga. On the other: google ads agency mississauga, google ads management services mississauga, adwords management agency mississauga.

The first vocabulary belongs to somebody who thinks in terms of a discipline and usually has either run accounts before or employed somebody who did. They ask about structure, attribution and fee models, and they are buying expertise.

The second belongs to somebody who thinks in terms of the platform. They have an account, or a Google representative has called them, and they want somebody to operate it. They are buying time and competence rather than strategy.

Neither is better and they need different conversations from any PPC company Mississauga businesses bring in. The failure is a single proposal pitched at the midpoint, which reads as strategy to the operator and as operations to the strategist.

The call you will get

Somebody From The Platform Will Phone You

It is worth knowing what that call is, because it shapes a great many accounts in this city.

Advertising platforms employ account representatives whose job includes making suggestions about your account. Those suggestions are frequently sensible and they are made by somebody whose employer is paid when you spend more. That is not corruption; it is simply an interest that is not identical to yours.

The recommendations that follow a pattern worth noticing: broaden the match types, raise the budget, enable automated placements, switch to a bid strategy that spends the full budget. Each has a legitimate case. Each also increases spend, and an account that accepts all of them has been optimised toward a goal nobody at your end set.

The defence is not suspicion. It is a written objective: cost per enquiry, with a ceiling, agreed before any recommendation is evaluated. With that on the table, every suggestion becomes a testable proposition rather than an authority argument.

A good agency does the same thing with its own recommendations, and should hand you the ceiling to hold them to.

Where accounts leak

Four Ways A PPC Company Mississauga Account Leaks Quietly

None of these shows up as an error. All of them show up in the bank.

Search terms nobody reads

The keyword list is what you bid on; the search terms report is what people typed. The gap between them is the largest recoverable cost in most accounts and it takes under an hour a month to read.

Brand spend blended into the total

Bidding on your own name converts beautifully and mostly buys clicks you would have had anyway. Blended into one cost per acquisition it makes the whole account look better than it is. Report it on its own line.

One page for several campaigns

A specific search sent to a general page converts badly at any price. In a market with clicks in this range it is the most expensive habit available and the cheapest to fix.

Geography inherited from a default

A radius that includes the whole region, or excludes the industrial parks where your customers actually are, set once and never revisited. In a city of 23,700 businesses the map matters more than usual.

The fee question

Percentage Of Spend Points The Wrong Way

The common model in this city rewards volume and is silent about efficiency.

Under a percentage-of-spend fee, the agency earns more when you spend more and earns nothing additional when the same spend produces twice the result. Everyone working under it can be conscientious and the structure still leans one way, and it predicts which conversations you will have.

The alternatives each carry a problem. A flat fee is honest and makes small accounts unattractive to service properly. Performance pricing sounds ideal and founders on attribution, because the two sides rarely agree on what counts. A flat fee against a defined scope, reviewed quarterly, is the least bad arrangement we have found.

What to ask any PPC company Mississauga puts in front of you

Under this fee arrangement, what would make you recommend that we spend less? An agency that cannot answer has not thought about the incentive, and an agency that answers well has.

Long cycles

When The Sale Happens Months After The Click

In a city this weighted toward business-to-business selling, the account and the revenue are separated by a gap that breaks most reporting.

A click in January and a signature in May are connected by nothing unless somebody built the connection. By the time the deal closes, the advertising platform has forgotten, the salesperson remembers a conversation rather than a campaign, and the monthly report has already declared the January spend a failure.

The fix is dull and it is the single highest-return setup task in a B2B account: capture the campaign and the page at the moment of enquiry, carry them into the CRM record, and make them a column on whatever report the business actually reads. A week of work, and every budget argument afterwards is about numbers rather than impressions.

Without it, the account will be judged on leading indicators that do not mean what people think they mean, and the usual casualty is the campaign with the longest cycle, which is frequently the most valuable one.

We ask for this before quoting rather than after, because an account we cannot measure is an account we cannot defend.

Our scope

What We Run Here

Narrow, and with the measurement attached.

We build and run Google and Meta accounts against cost per enquiry with a ceiling you set, write the pages the ads point at, and connect the enquiry to its source so a long cycle does not erase it. We manage over $70,000 a month in advertising spend for Ontario businesses and the owner runs the paid media personally rather than passing it to a desk.

We are based in Minesing rather than Mississauga. For an advertising account that changes nothing; for filming a facility it means travel, which we price rather than hide.

Two things we would want before quoting. First, what a customer is worth and what proportion of enquiries become customers, because those two numbers set the ceiling and without them every review meeting is a discussion of clicks. Second, whether anybody is currently running the account, because taking over a live account mid-quarter and rebuilding one from nothing are different jobs with different risks and the second is rarely necessary.

What we would often recommend is a smaller account than expected alongside the search work, with the budget reviewed down as pages start to rank. That is the less profitable recommendation and in a market priced like this one it is usually the right one.

What is a reasonable management fee here?

The market quotes ten to twenty per cent of spend, and the percentage matters less than the scope. A twelve per cent fee excluding landing pages is more expensive in total than a twenty per cent fee that includes them, because you will buy the pages separately at a worse price. Compare scopes rather than percentages.

What is the minimum budget worth running?

Enough to produce a few hundred clicks a month, because below that the account cannot be optimised and managing it is guesswork. In a market with clicks in this range that is a real number. The better constraint is your maximum defensible cost per enquiry, which comes from what a customer is worth rather than from a benchmark.

Should we accept the platform representative’s recommendations?

Evaluate them against a written objective rather than accepting or refusing on principle. Many are sensible. They are also made by somebody whose employer is paid when you spend more, so a recommendation that increases spend needs the same test as any other: what will it do to cost per enquiry, and how will we know within a month.

Can you take over an existing account?

Yes, and read access plus an assessment is better than rebuilding, because history carries learning that is expensive to recreate. The usual finding is not poor construction but a geography, a schedule or a match type set once and never revisited, plus brand spend blended into the headline number.
