Working With Us, Wherever You Are

Do you only work with businesses near Barrie? No. Our office is in Simcoe County and that is where on-site production is cheapest, but paid media, SEO, CRM and reporting are location-independent and we run them across the province. Where a project needs filming, we travel and quote the travel plainly. Is there a price difference by city? Not for the management work — a campaign is priced on scope, not postal code.

Production travel is the one line that varies, and it is quoted separately so you can see exactly what distance costs. Do you understand markets outside your own? We research each one rather than assuming. A mining supply company in Timmins, a resort in Huntsville and a dealership in Brampton are three different businesses with three different buyers, and treating them the same is how agencies waste budget.

Which markets do you know best? Simcoe County, York Region and the GTA, because that is where most of our clients are and where we film most often. We have also produced on location in Sudbury and Thunder Bay. We will tell you honestly on the call how much local knowledge we are starting with. Do you work outside Ontario? Our client base is Ontario, and our production crew has worked in Aruba, Tampa and Washington DC.

If you are outside the province and the work is a fit, say so on the call and we will be straight about whether we are the right team.
