You Will Not Outbid Them

A ppc agency vaughan is managing an account that cannot win on budget: the city holds 323,103 people and sits beside a far larger one whose advertisers set the prices, so the only reliable advantage left is being more specific than anybody willing to pay more.

What A PPC Agency Vaughan Should Stop Bidding On

A deep cluster, a low difficulty, and prices set by somebody else entirely.

The broad terms in any trade here are contested by advertisers whose budgets are set against a much larger market. Bidding on them is not a strategy; it is a subscription to somebody else’s price floor.

What those advertisers cannot do is be specific. A large account chasing volume has to keep its terms broad, which leaves the precise searches — a particular service, a particular problem, a particular constraint — almost unattended.

Those searches are fewer and they convert at a different rate entirely, because the person typing one has described exactly what they want and is past every stage of the decision except choosing.

So the useful instruction for an account here is the opposite of the usual one: narrow the offer rather than the budget, and let the broad terms go to whoever is willing to pay for them.

How to find them

Four Places The Specific Searches Hide

None of them is in a keyword tool, and all four are in documents you already have.

The search terms report

What people actually typed rather than what you asked for. The most useful document in any account and the one least often opened; the long phrases in it are the whole opportunity.

The questions on your phone

Written down for a month. People type what they say, and the phrasing a customer uses is almost never the phrasing in a tool.

The jobs you decline

Each one is a search somebody made that you could have excluded in advance. Negative keywords built from that list are worth more than any bid adjustment.

The constraint that makes you different

A size, a lead time, a standard, a thing you will take on that others will not. Searches naming it are rare, cheap and almost pre-qualified.

Four sources, an afternoon each, and between them they produce an account that a larger advertiser has no mechanism to copy.

There is one more reason to settle this before a campaign exists rather than after. The exclusion list built from those four sources is an asset that keeps working no matter who runs the account afterwards, and it is the single most valuable document a paid search engagement produces. Most accounts we are handed have nothing in it at all, which means every month since launch has been paying for the same irrelevant clicks.

The five communities

What A PPC Agency Vaughan Should Do With Five Communities

The geography is the second-largest source of waste after the terms themselves.

Vaughan’s population is spread across five communities, and a campaign set to the municipal boundary treats somebody at one edge as equally reachable from the other. For a business with a physical location that is rarely true.

Split the reporting by community, which costs nothing and takes an hour, and the account stops reporting one mediocre average. A campaign working in one place and failing in another is a common and entirely invisible pattern.

It also matters at the southern boundary. A radius drawn generously reaches into a much larger market where click prices are higher and the searcher has closer alternatives, and nothing in the account announces the crossing.

Review it quarterly against the one record that settles it: where the customers who actually paid were.

The same split applies to the money as well as the reporting. A budget set city-wide is spent wherever the system finds the cheapest attention, which in practice means the communities with the least competition rather than the ones with your best customers in them, and nothing in the account announces that either.

Before any budget

Three Numbers, One Afternoon

They decide whether a specific or a broad account makes sense for you.

What a customer is worth

Over the whole relationship rather than the first job. Without it, cost per enquiry is a figure with nothing to compare it against.

What share of enquiries convert

The bridge between the two, and the number most likely to be guessed. A guess here moves the defensible budget by a factor of two.

The maximum defensible cost per enquiry

Which follows arithmetically, and which can then be given to the account as the thing to optimise toward rather than left at a default.

That third number is doing double duty now. It is both the test for the budget and the instruction an automated campaign needs, which makes working it out more valuable than it used to be.

Working with automation

Specific Accounts Need Tighter Constraints

Automated campaign types optimise toward whatever they are told, and a narrow strategy is easy for them to undo.

Left to choose their own placements, audiences and terms inside a budget, these campaign types will drift toward volume, which means drifting back toward exactly the broad searches you decided not to compete for.

Used with clean conversion data, an enforced geography and a maintained exclusion list, they work very well. Used as a substitute for those three, they will quietly rebuild the generic account you were trying to avoid.

So the question to put to any supplier is not whether they use them, but what constrains them: which conversions are optimised toward, what geography is enforced, what is excluded, and how often any of it is reviewed.

The habit that catches the drift is simple: read the search terms report monthly and ask of every line whether you would have chosen to pay for it. Anything that fails becomes a negative that week.
