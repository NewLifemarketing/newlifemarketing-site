What the numbers say

Social Media Peterborough: Nobody Searches For It Here

128,624
people in the Peterborough census metropolitan area

Statistics Canada, 2021 Census of Population

A seventeen-to-one gap between general marketing demand and this one service does not mean nobody buys it. It means nobody shops for it by typing, which is a statement about how it is sold rather than how useful it is.

What follows from that is practical. Every supplier you are offered will arrive through a recommendation or a cold approach rather than a comparison, so the usual filter of shopping around does not apply and the questions have to do the work instead.

It also means a thin market for honest advice. When something is sold rather than searched for, the version that travels is the version that is easiest to sell, which here is a monthly posting retainer with a round number attached.

The useful conversation starts somewhere else: what this channel is uniquely good at in a catchment like this one, and which parts of it you should not be paying anybody for.

What it is good at here

The Only Channel That Reaches People Before They Arrive

Search reaches somebody standing in your town. This reaches them while they are still deciding to come.

Parks Canada ran the 2026 Trent-Severn navigation season from 15 May to 12 October, and the waterway runs 386 km through this catchment. A large share of the people a seasonal business serves are somewhere else for much of the year and arrive with plans already made.

Search cannot reach them, because they are not searching yet. A paid social audience can, and it can do it six weeks early, which is when the decision about where to eat, moor, stay, book or buy is actually taken.

That is the strongest argument for the channel in this market and it is almost never the one in the proposal. It also changes the targeting: the people worth reaching in March are not in the same postal codes as the people worth reaching in July.

The reverse is true in October. The same audience is leaving, and the last message of the season is the cheapest booking you will take for next year.

The three products

Posting, Strategy And Distribution Are Not The Same Purchase

Quotes rarely say which is included, and they behave completely differently.

Posting

Somebody makes and schedules content. The most commonly sold of the three, the most visible on an invoice, and the one that produces the least on its own.

Strategy

Deciding what is worth saying, to whom and when — including the six-weeks-early question. Cheap, occasional, and what makes the other two worth anything.

Distribution

Money spent reaching people who do not follow you, which is where enquiries come from. It is also the line that quietly shrinks when a blended monthly fee is squeezed.

What the material should be

Stock Photography Is The Expensive Option

It costs less and it performs worse, which is the worst combination available.

Every supplier has the same stock library. What almost none of their clients have is footage of the actual work and the actual place: the job going in, the room as it really looks, the person who answers the phone.

In a catchment where a recommendation does most of the selling, real material confirms what the person recommending you said. Stock quietly contradicts it, and the contradiction is most visible to exactly the audience you most want.

The seasonal constraint applies here too. Anything that only exists while the water is open has to be filmed while the water is open, which means the material for next March is made in July or it is not made at all.

A posting retainer with no production budget attached is an arrangement for distributing material that does not exist.

Measurement

What A Social Media Peterborough Report Should Contain

At this scale the platform numbers are decoration and the oldest question is the best instrument.

Engagement rate is a percentage of an audience the platform chose. Reach is purchasable, so a reach figure with no spend figure beside it says nothing. Followers are the least predictive number on the page.

What works is asking every arriving customer how they heard of you and writing the answer down, with one extra column for where they were. In a catchment this dispersed and this seasonal, that column is the only honest basis for deciding where the money goes.

Read it across a rolling quarter rather than a month. A business taking eight enquiries a month cannot distinguish a good month from a bad one, and an account judged monthly at this scale gets cut and restarted on noise.

There is a second reason it matters in a seasonal market. A campaign that ran in April and a customer who arrived in July are separated by three months, and no platform attribution window is open that long. Without somebody writing down what the customer said, the six-week-early campaign that actually produced the booking gets credited to whatever was running the week they walked in.

It costs a minute per enquiry, and it is the only line in the report that comes from your business rather than from a platform.

What A Social Media Peterborough Quote Should Separate

Production, which is filming and editing and happens on days you can count, from management, which is planning, posting and replying and happens every week. They are different work at different rates, and a single monthly figure hides which one you are actually buying. Ask how many shoot days, how many posts, who writes them, who answers the messages, and what happens to the footage if you leave.

Straight answers

Social Media Peterborough FAQ

Straight answers before you ever book a call.

Nobody here searches for this. Does that mean it does not work?

No — it means it is sold rather than shopped for. That almost nobody goes looking for it, while plenty look for marketing generally, tells you how suppliers reach you rather than how well the channel performs. The thing it does best in this catchment is reaching people six weeks before they arrive, which is not something search can do at all.

When should a seasonal campaign start?

Roughly six weeks before the season opens, because that is when the decisions are made rather than when the people appear. Parks Canada ran the 2026 navigation season from 15 May to 12 October, so a campaign starting in April is reaching people while plans are still movable. The last week of the season is the second-cheapest booking of the year.

How much should we spend?

Enough to reach the audience that matters more than once in the weeks that matter, which is a calculable number rather than a guess. Spending the same amount every month regardless of season is the common pattern and it puts most of the budget into the weeks when the decision has already been taken.

Do we need to post every day?

No. Post as often as there is something worth showing, which is usually less than a daily schedule implies. Two posts a week of real work beats five of stock graphics, which teach an audience to scroll past you. The budget is better spent on distribution and on making material that only exists for five months of the year.
