Search Captures Demand. Meta Creates It.

That distinction decides which channel is right, and in Brampton it matters more than in a settled Ontario market.

Somebody searching has already decided they have a problem. They are comparing solutions, and the job of the ad is to be in that comparison. A Brampton person scrolling has decided nothing. The job of the ad is to make them aware a problem is solvable at all, which is harder, slower and occasionally far more valuable.

Brampton has unusual room for the second. A city that added almost seventy thousand people in five years is full of Brampton households that have not yet chosen a plumber, a dentist, a contractor, a gym or an accountant. Those people are not searching yet. They will be, and the Brampton business they have already seen three times has an advantage nothing in the search results can match.

The catch is the one thing a meta ads agency brampton businesses hire should say out loud before taking the money: this only works if the offer makes sense to somebody who was not looking for it. Not every offer does, and no amount of budget fixes one that does not.

Questions

Meta Ads In Brampton: Common Questions

Facebook ads or Google Ads for a Brampton business?

They do different jobs and for a Brampton business the right answer is often both, in a particular order. Search captures people who have already decided they have a problem, so it is usually the first money spent for anything urgent or well understood. Meta creates demand among people who were not looking, which is slower and in a city that added almost seventy thousand residents in five years has a great deal of room. If your offer only makes sense to somebody already looking, start with search and add Meta later, if at all.

What budget do we need to start?

Less than most proposals suggest, because the first phase should be aimed at people who already know you rather than at the whole city. A warm audience test is inexpensive and answers the only question that matters at the start: does this offer convert when somebody is not actively shopping. Scaling comes after, and the scale budget should be set by what the test produced rather than picked in advance. Anybody quoting a large cold-prospecting budget before testing is selling you information you could have bought cheaply.

Why did our ads work and then stop?

Almost always creative fatigue rather than anything about the audience. The same people are seeing the same image for the fifth time and have stopped registering it. The dashboard makes it look like a targeting problem because frequency is buried and cost per result is on the front page, so the usual reaction is to change the audience, which resets the learning and makes things worse. Refresh the footage on a schedule and leave the targeting alone.

Do we need professional video?

Rarely, and the assumption that you do holds a lot of Brampton businesses back from starting. A thirty second phone video of the owner explaining the thing Brampton customers always ask routinely outperforms produced video, because what is being bought is credibility rather than polish. Produced work earns its place later, once something has already proven it converts. Starting with a production budget is how a channel gets judged before it has been tested.

Can you take over an account someone else built?

Yes, and the first thing we do is read it rather than rebuild it. The usual findings are a budget spread across several offers so nothing produced a clean answer, cold audiences from day one, and boosted posts running alongside campaigns and muddying the data. Often the account does not need replacing so much as narrowing. If it turns out the previous setup was sound and the offer is simply wrong for the channel, we will tell you that too.
